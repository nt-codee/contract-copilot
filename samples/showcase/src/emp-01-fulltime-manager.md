# EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT: OUTLET MANAGER

This Employment Contract (the "**Contract**") is made on 1 September 2025 (the "**Effective Date**").

**BETWEEN**

(1) **KOPI KITA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1000), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (the "**Employer**"); and

(2) **MS. TAN CONTOH** (No. K/P: SAMPLE-000000), of No. 14, Jalan Contoh 5/2, Taman Contoh, 47800 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia (the "**Employee**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Employer is a Malaysian food and beverage group operating twelve cafe outlets and a central roastery.

(B) The Employee joined the Employer on 1 June 2025 as a senior barista and completed her three (3) month probation period on 31 August 2025. The Employer has confirmed her in service and appointed her as an Outlet Manager, and the parties wish to record the terms of her confirmed employment in this Contract.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Contract, unless the context requires otherwise:

"**Act**" means the Employment Act 1955 of Malaysia, as amended from time to time, and any regulations made under it.

"**Confidential Information**" means non-public information about the Employer's recipes, supplier terms, costs, sales, staff and plans that the Employee learns during employment.

"**Outlet**" means the cafe outlet to which the Employee is assigned from time to time.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only. Where this Contract gives the Employee less than the Act requires, the Act prevails and the Employee receives the statutory benefit.

## 2. APPOINTMENT AND DUTIES

2.1 The Employer employs the Employee as an Outlet Manager on a permanent basis from the Effective Date, reporting to the Operations Director.

2.2 The Employee is responsible for the daily running of her Outlet, including opening and closing, staff rosters, stock ordering, cash handling, food safety and hygiene standards, customer service and the monthly sales and cost report.

2.3 The Employee's usual place of work is the Employer's Bangsar South outlet. The Employer may reasonably assign her to another Outlet in the Klang Valley on at least fourteen (14) days' written notice, and will not require a change of home location beyond the Klang Valley without her agreement.

2.4 The Employee shall carry out her duties diligently, follow lawful instructions and the written policies given to her, and tell the Employer promptly of any conflict of interest.

## 3. PROBATION

3.1 The Employee's probation period of three (3) months ended on 31 August 2025 and she is confirmed in her post. No further probation applies to this appointment.

## 4. WORKING HOURS AND OVERTIME

4.1 The Employee works up to forty-five (45) hours a week, on a roster of five and a half (5.5) days with one rest day, across opening hours of the Outlet. The roster is published fourteen (14) days ahead.

4.2 Where the Act entitles the Employee to overtime pay, work beyond the normal hours, or on a rest day or public holiday, is paid at the rates the Act requires. The Employer will not require overtime beyond the limits in the Act.

4.3 Where the Employee is not entitled to overtime pay under the Act, the Employer will give time off in lieu for any extra rest-day work it has asked her to do in writing, to be taken within sixty (60) days.

## 5. SALARY AND STATUTORY CONTRIBUTIONS

5.1 The Employer shall pay the Employee a basic monthly salary of RM 5,200.00, paid by bank transfer on or before the last working day of each month.

5.2 The Employer shall make contributions to the Employees Provident Fund, the Social Security Organisation (SOCSO) and the Employment Insurance System (EIS) as required by law, and shall deduct the employee's share from her salary. The Employee will receive a payslip each month.

5.3 The Employer may deduct from salary only those amounts the Act permits. It will not deduct for cash shortages, breakages or uniforms.

5.4 Salary is reviewed once a year in January. A review does not oblige the Employer to increase salary.

5.5 The Employee may be eligible for a performance bonus under the Employer's published outlet bonus scheme, as it applies from time to time. The scheme is separate from, and does not form part of, her salary.

## 6. LEAVE

6.1 The Employee is entitled to fourteen (14) days of paid annual leave in each calendar year, increasing as the Act or the Employer's policy provides for longer service. Leave not taken may be carried forward up to five (5) days into the next year.

6.2 The Employee is entitled to paid sick leave, hospitalisation leave, maternity leave and public holidays as set out in the Act, and to any other leave the Employer's staff handbook grants.

6.3 The Employee shall apply for annual leave at least fourteen (14) days ahead, and the Employer shall reply within seven (7) days and not unreasonably refuse leave.

## 7. TERMINATION

7.1 After confirmation, either party may end this Contract by giving the other two (2) months' written notice, or by paying salary in lieu of any part of the notice not given. The notice period is the same for both parties.

7.2 The Employer may dismiss the Employee without notice for misconduct after a domestic inquiry held in line with the Act and the principles of natural justice.

7.3 On the end of employment the Employee shall return all keys, access cards, devices, cash and documents belonging to the Employer, and the Employer shall pay all salary, accrued leave pay and other sums due within the time the Act requires.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY AND PERSONAL DATA

8.1 The Employee shall keep Confidential Information secret during employment and for two (2) years afterwards, and use it only for the Employer's business. This does not cover information that is public other than through her breach, that she already knew, or that she must disclose by law.

8.2 The Employer shall process the Employee's personal data in line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 and its privacy notice for staff. The Employee consents to this processing for payroll, statutory filings, benefits and the administration of employment.

## 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

9.1 Recipes, menus, training materials and reports that the Employee creates in the course of her duties belong to the Employer. The Employee keeps all rights in anything she creates in her own time without using the Employer's resources or Confidential Information.

## 10. NON-SOLICITATION OF STAFF

10.1 For twelve (12) months after her employment ends, the Employee shall not directly solicit any person who is then employed by the Employer, and who she worked with in the last twelve (12) months of her service, to leave the Employer.

10.2 This clause is limited to staff. It does not stop the Employee from working for any other business, including a business in the same trade, or from dealing with customers or suppliers.

## 11. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

11.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of Malaysia. The parties will first try to settle any dispute by discussion between the Employee and the Operations Director.

11.2 Nothing in this Contract limits the Employee's right to bring a complaint to the Labour Department or the Industrial Court as the law allows. Otherwise, the courts of Kuala Lumpur have jurisdiction.

## 12. GENERAL

12.1 This Contract, with the staff handbook, is the entire agreement on its subject and replaces the Employee's earlier offer letter. Any change must be in writing and signed by both parties.

12.2 Notices shall be in writing and sent to the addresses above, or by email to the address each party has given in writing.

12.3 If any part of this Contract is unenforceable, the rest remains in force. This Contract is signed in two copies, one for each party.
