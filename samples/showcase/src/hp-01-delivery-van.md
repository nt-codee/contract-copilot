# HIRE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Hire Purchase Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 9 February 2026 (the "**Effective Date**").

**BETWEEN**

(1) **AUTO DANA CONTOH BERHAD** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-7101), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at Level 9, Menara Contoh, Jalan Contoh Niaga 2, 50250 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (the "**Owner**"); and

(2) **KOPI KITA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1000), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (the "**Hirer**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Owner carries on the business of financing the acquisition of commercial vehicles.

(B) The Hirer operates twelve cafe outlets and a central roastery in the Klang Valley and requires a refrigerated delivery van to carry roasted coffee, milk and perishable stock between the roastery and its outlets.

(C) The Owner has agreed to let and the Hirer has agreed to hire the Vehicle with an option to purchase, on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Vehicle**" means the refrigerated light commercial delivery van described in the Schedule, together with all accessories, tools, spare parts and replacements fitted to it.

"**Cash Price**" means RM 138,000.00, being the price of the Vehicle including registration and road tax for the first year.

"**Deposit**" means RM 28,000.00, paid by the Hirer to the dealer on or before the Effective Date.

"**Amount Financed**" means RM 110,000.00, being the Cash Price less the Deposit.

"**Hire Purchase Charges**" means the total charges for the credit, calculated at a flat rate of 3.60 per cent per annum on the Amount Financed for the Term, amounting to RM 19,800.00.

"**Total Hire Purchase Price**" means the Cash Price plus the Hire Purchase Charges, being RM 157,800.00.

"**Monthly Instalment**" means RM 2,163.33, payable on the 9th day of each month.

"**Effective Rate**" means the effective rate of interest of 6.52 per cent per annum, stated for information in accordance with the Hire Purchase Act 1967 and the rules made under it.

"**Term**" means sixty (60) months beginning on the Effective Date and ending on 9 February 2031.

"**Event of Default**" has the meaning given in Clause 9.

1.2 Clause headings are for convenience only. References to a statute include that statute as amended or re-enacted from time to time.

1.3 This Agreement is intended to be a hire purchase agreement within the meaning of the Hire Purchase Act 1967 to the extent that Act applies to it. Nothing in this Agreement removes any right that the Hirer has under that Act which cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 2. HIRING AND OPTION TO PURCHASE

2.1 The Owner lets and the Hirer hires the Vehicle for the Term, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 Ownership of the Vehicle remains with the Owner until the Hirer has paid every sum due under this Agreement. Possession passes to the Hirer on delivery.

2.3 On payment of the Total Hire Purchase Price, and all other sums then due under this Agreement, the Hirer shall have the option to purchase the Vehicle for a nominal sum of RM 10.00. The Owner shall transfer ownership to the Hirer within fourteen (14) days of the last payment and shall release its interest in the registration documents.

2.4 The Owner confirms that it has the right to let the Vehicle and that the Vehicle is free from any charge or encumbrance other than the Owner's interest under this Agreement.

## 3. DELIVERY AND CONDITION

3.1 The Vehicle shall be delivered to the Hirer at the roastery of the Hirer on or about the Effective Date. The Hirer shall sign a delivery acknowledgement when it has inspected the Vehicle and found it to be in good order.

3.2 The Owner shall pass to the Hirer the benefit of all manufacturer and dealer warranties on the Vehicle. The Hirer shall have the right to enforce those warranties directly.

3.3 The Vehicle is let in its existing condition. The Hirer acknowledges that it selected the Vehicle and the dealer, and that the Owner has not made any statement about the Vehicle other than those in this Agreement.

## 4. PAYMENT

4.1 The Hirer shall pay the Monthly Instalment on the 9th day of each month for sixty (60) months, the first instalment falling due on 9 March 2026. The final instalment shall be RM 2,163.53 to account for rounding.

4.2 Payment shall be made by standing instruction from the Hirer's account to the Owner's account. If the due date is not a business day, payment shall be made on the preceding business day.

4.3 The Owner shall send the Hirer a statement of account every six (6) months and shall provide a statement of account within seven (7) days of a written request.

4.4 Each payment shall be applied first to any sums due other than the Monthly Instalment, then to the Hire Purchase Charges, and then to the Amount Financed.

## 5. REGISTRATION AND USE

5.1 The Vehicle shall be registered with the Road Transport Department in the joint names of the Owner as owner and the Hirer as hirer, or in such other manner as the law requires.

5.2 The Hirer shall use the Vehicle only for its lawful business of delivering goods in Peninsular Malaysia, shall keep it in the roadworthy condition, and shall ensure that it is driven only by licensed and competent drivers employed or engaged by the Hirer.

5.3 The Hirer shall not sell, charge, lend, part with possession of or sub-let the Vehicle, and shall not modify it in any way that reduces its value, without the written consent of the Owner, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 6. INSURANCE

6.1 The Hirer shall keep the Vehicle comprehensively insured for its full market value against loss, damage, theft and third-party liability throughout the Term, with the Owner noted as owner and as the party entitled to claims proceeds up to the amount then outstanding.

6.2 The insurance shall be taken out with an insurer on the Owner's panel of insurers, as notified to the Hirer from time to time, and through the Owner's appointed insurance agent. The Hirer shall pay the premium on or before each renewal date. If the Hirer fails to do so, the Owner may pay the premium and recover it from the Hirer on demand together with an administration fee of RM 50.00 for each payment.

6.3 The Hirer shall not do or omit to do anything that would invalidate the insurance, and shall tell the Owner within forty-eight (48) hours of any accident, loss or claim.

## 7. COSTS, MAINTENANCE AND RISK

7.1 The Hirer shall bear all costs of owning and operating the Vehicle, including road tax renewals, insurance premiums, servicing, repairs, tyres, fuel, tolls, fines, summonses, inspection fees and any government levy imposed on the Vehicle or its use, and all legal, stamp, registration and administrative costs of this Agreement and of any transfer, whether incurred by the Owner or the Hirer.

7.2 The Hirer shall have the Vehicle serviced at intervals recommended by the manufacturer at an authorised service centre and shall keep service records, which the Owner may inspect on reasonable notice.

7.3 The Vehicle is at the risk of the Hirer from delivery until ownership passes to the Hirer. Loss of or damage to the Vehicle does not relieve the Hirer of its obligation to pay the Monthly Instalment unless the Owner has received the insurance proceeds in full settlement of the amount outstanding.

## 8. EARLY SETTLEMENT

8.1 The Hirer may at any time, by giving thirty (30) days' written notice, settle the account in full and terminate this Agreement.

8.2 On early settlement the Hirer shall pay the Owner the outstanding Amount Financed, all arrears and all other sums then due, less a rebate of Hire Purchase Charges. The rebate shall be calculated on the Rule of 78 formula only for the instalments remaining unpaid after the date of settlement, less an administrative deduction equal to two (2) Monthly Instalments, and shall in no case exceed the unearned Hire Purchase Charges.

8.3 No rebate shall be allowed if settlement takes place within the first twelve (12) months of the Term.

## 9. DEFAULT

9.1 Each of the following is an Event of Default:

(a) the Hirer fails to pay any instalment or other sum within fourteen (14) days of its due date;

(b) the Hirer breaches any other term of this Agreement and, where the breach can be remedied, fails to remedy it within fourteen (14) days of notice from the Owner;

(c) the Hirer becomes insolvent, enters into a scheme of arrangement, has a receiver appointed over its assets, or is wound up; or

(d) the Vehicle is seized under any legal process and the seizure is not lifted within fourteen (14) days.

9.2 If an Event of Default occurs, the Owner may by written notice terminate this Agreement and re-take possession of the Vehicle. Where the Hirer has paid less than the proportion of the Total Hire Purchase Price that the Hire Purchase Act 1967 protects, the Owner shall follow the procedure for repossession in that Act.

9.3 Following repossession the Owner shall sell the Vehicle, credit the net proceeds against the sum owed by the Hirer, and give the Hirer an account of the sale. The Hirer shall remain liable for any shortfall and shall be entitled to any surplus.

## 10. LATE PAYMENT

10.1 If the Hirer does not pay any sum on its due date, the Hirer shall pay a late payment charge of one and one half per cent (1.5%) per month on the overdue amount, calculated daily from the due date until the date of actual payment, both before and after judgment.

10.2 Late payment charges are not capitalised and shall be charged only on the overdue instalment, in accordance with applicable law and the rules of Bank Negara Malaysia.

## 11. LIABILITY

11.1 The Owner is not liable for any loss of profit, loss of business, spoilage of stock or indirect loss suffered by the Hirer arising from any defect in the Vehicle, its breakdown, or the time it is out of service.

11.2 Nothing in this Agreement excludes liability that cannot be excluded by law.

## 12. DATA PROTECTION

12.1 The Owner shall handle personal data it receives about the Hirer's directors, employees and drivers in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 and shall use it only for administering this Agreement, assessing credit, insurance and compliance with law.

12.2 The Owner may disclose the Hirer's payment record to licensed credit reporting agencies as the law permits.

## 13. NOTICES

13.1 Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, sent by registered post to the address of the party at the head of this Agreement, or sent by email to the address notified in writing. A notice by registered post is deemed received three (3) business days after posting and a notice by email on the next business day after sending.

13.2 A party may change its address for notices by giving seven (7) days' written notice.

## 14. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

14.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Malaysia, including the Hire Purchase Act 1967 where it applies and the Contracts Act 1950.

14.2 The parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Kuala Lumpur.

## 15. GENERAL

15.1 This Agreement and the Schedule are the entire agreement of the parties on the hire of the Vehicle and replace all earlier discussions.

15.2 No variation is effective unless it is in writing and signed by both parties.

15.3 A waiver of any right must be in writing. A delay in exercising a right is not a waiver of it.

15.4 If any provision is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in effect.

15.5 The Hirer may not assign this Agreement. The Owner may assign its rights to a licensed financial institution on notice to the Hirer.

15.6 This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
