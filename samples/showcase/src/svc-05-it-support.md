# MANAGED IT SUPPORT AGREEMENT

This Managed IT Support Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 1 March 2026 (the "**Effective Date**").

**BETWEEN**

(1) **LIGHTHOUSE IT SERVICES PTE. LTD.** (UEN SAMPLE-SG-7105), a company incorporated in Singapore with its registered office at 25 Contoh Road, #07-09, Singapore 000725 (the "**Provider**"); and

(2) **KOPI KITA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1000), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur (the "**Customer**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Customer operates a head office, a central roastery and twelve (12) cafe outlets in Malaysia, each using point-of-sale terminals, network equipment and office computers.

(B) The Provider provides managed IT support, and the Customer wishes to engage it to support those sites on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Business Hours**" means 8.00 a.m. to 8.00 p.m. Malaysia time, seven days a week, excluding the Customer's closure days notified in advance.

"**Customer Data**" means all data held on or passing through the Customer's systems, including sales, staff and customer data.

"**Fees**" means the Monthly Fee and any approved additional charges.

"**Monthly Fee**" means S$6,200 per month, exclusive of goods and services tax.

"**Services**" means the helpdesk, point-of-sale support, network support and monitoring services described in Schedule 1.

"**Sites**" means the head office, the roastery and the outlets listed in Schedule 2.

## 2. THE SERVICES

2.1 The Provider shall provide the Services to the Sites from the Effective Date with reasonable skill and care and in line with good industry practice.

2.2 The Provider shall maintain a helpdesk that accepts requests by telephone, email and ticket portal during Business Hours, and by telephone for priority 1 incidents at any time.

2.3 The Provider shall respond to and resolve incidents within the targets below, measured from the time the incident is logged:

(a) Priority 1 (a site cannot trade, or point-of-sale is down): response within thirty (30) minutes; on-site or remote fix within four (4) hours;

(b) Priority 2 (a degraded service affecting trading): response within two (2) hours; fix within one (1) business day;

(c) Priority 3 (a single user or minor fault): response within one (1) business day; fix within three (3) business days.

2.4 If the Provider misses a priority 1 target in a month, the Customer is entitled to a credit of five per cent (5%) of the Monthly Fee for each missed target, up to twenty per cent (20%) in the month.

2.5 The Customer shall give the Provider remote access and, on reasonable notice, physical access to the equipment at the Sites, and shall tell the Provider promptly of changes that may affect the Services.

2.6 Hardware, licences and third-party software are not included in the Fees. The Provider shall obtain the Customer's written approval before buying or ordering anything on its behalf.

## 3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 The Provider shall invoice the Monthly Fee monthly in advance. The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

3.2 After the first anniversary of the Effective Date, and not more than once in any twelve (12) months, the Provider may increase the Monthly Fee by written notice of at least sixty (60) days. The increase shall not exceed the lower of three per cent (3%) and the percentage increase in the Singapore Consumer Price Index (All Items) over the preceding twelve (12) months.

3.3 Work outside the Services shall be charged at S$140 per hour only if the Customer has approved it in writing in advance.

## 4. TERM AND RENEWAL

4.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for twelve (12) months until 28 February 2027 (the "**Initial Term**").

4.2 At the end of the Initial Term and of each renewal term, this Agreement renews automatically for a further twelve (12) months unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal by email at least thirty (30) days before the end of the current term. For the Initial Term, the notice must be sent on or before 29 January 2027.

## 5. DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY

5.1 The Customer Data belongs to the Customer. The Provider shall access it only to provide the Services and shall not copy it, sell it or use it for any other purpose.

5.2 The Provider shall protect the Customer Data with security measures that are appropriate to its sensitivity, including unique administrator credentials, multi-factor authentication for remote access, encryption of backups and logging of administrator access.

5.3 The Provider shall notify the Customer of any actual or suspected breach affecting the Customer Data within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of it, shall give the Customer all the information it has, and shall help the Customer to investigate, contain and report the breach.

5.4 Each party shall comply with the data protection laws that apply to it. Where the Provider handles personal data for the Customer, it does so as the Customer's data intermediary and shall comply with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 of Singapore as it applies to the Provider, and shall assist the Customer in complying with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia.

5.5 On expiry or termination, the Provider shall within fourteen (14) days return all Customer Data, administrator credentials, network diagrams and configuration files in a usable format, shall then securely delete any copies, and shall certify the deletion in writing.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party shall keep the other's non-public information confidential, use it only for this Agreement, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need it and are bound by confidentiality.

6.2 This does not apply to information that is public other than through breach, was already known to the recipient, was independently developed or lawfully received from a third party, or must be disclosed by law.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

7.1 Each party keeps its existing intellectual property. The Provider grants the Customer a non-exclusive licence to use any scripts, documentation and configuration produced for the Customer during the Term, for the Customer's internal business, including after the Term ends.

## 8. LIABILITY

8.1 Subject to Clause 8.3, the total liability of each party to the other under or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall not exceed the Fees paid and payable in the twelve (12) months before the event giving rise to the claim.

8.2 Neither party is liable to the other for loss of profit, loss of goodwill, or indirect or consequential loss.

8.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, for wilful default, for a party's breach of Clause 6 or, in the case of the Provider, for breach of Clause 5, up to a limit of two (2) times the Fees for twelve (12) months, or for any liability that cannot be limited by law.

8.4 The Provider shall maintain professional indemnity and cyber liability insurance of at least S$2,000,000 and shall give the Customer a copy of each certificate on request.

## 9. TERMINATION

9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of notice, or becomes insolvent or goes into liquidation or judicial management.

9.2 After the first six (6) months, the Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving sixty (60) days' written notice.

9.3 On termination, the Provider shall cooperate in good faith in handing over to the Customer or a replacement provider for up to thirty (30) days at the rates in Clause 3.3, and the Customer shall pay for Services performed to the termination date.

## 10. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.

10.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall first be referred to senior representatives of the parties, who shall meet within fourteen (14) days. If the dispute is not resolved within thirty (30) days, it shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration Rules of the Singapore International Arbitration Centre, with a sole arbitrator and Singapore as the seat. Either party may instead bring a claim of S$100,000 or less in the courts of Singapore, which shall have non-exclusive jurisdiction for that purpose.

10.3 A person who is not a party to this Agreement has no right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 2001 of Singapore to enforce any of its terms.

## 11. NOTICES

11.1 Notices must be in writing and sent by hand, courier or email to the addresses above. Notices of non-renewal under Clause 4.2 may be sent by email. A notice sent by email is received on the next business day.

## 12. GENERAL

12.1 This Agreement and its Schedules are the entire agreement on their subject. A variation must be in writing and signed by both parties.

12.2 The Provider may subcontract only with the Customer's prior written consent and remains responsible for its subcontractors.

12.3 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

12.4 Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, but the Provider shall keep to its disaster recovery plan.

## SCHEDULES

Schedule 1: helpdesk, point-of-sale support, network and Wi-Fi support, endpoint monitoring, patching, backup monitoring and a quarterly review meeting. Schedule 2: the head office, the roastery and the twelve (12) outlets, with the number of terminals and devices at each.
