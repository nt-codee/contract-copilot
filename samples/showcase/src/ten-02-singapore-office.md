# OFFICE TENANCY AGREEMENT

This Tenancy Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 1 April 2026.

**BETWEEN**

(1) **TANJONG CONTOH PROPERTIES PTE. LTD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-SG-7201), a company incorporated in Singapore with its registered office at 20 Contoh Road, #10-01 Contoh Tower, Singapore 068000 (the "**Landlord**"); and

(2) **KOPI KITA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1000), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur, acting through its Singapore representative office (the "**Tenant**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Landlord is the owner of the office unit at #08-05, Contoh Tower, 20 Contoh Road, Singapore 068000 (the "**Premises**"), with a net area of about 1,100 square feet.

(B) The Tenant wishes to use the Premises as its representative office in Singapore to support its sourcing and its planned Singapore cafe business, and the Landlord has agreed to let the Premises on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement: "**Term**" means the period in clause 2.1; "**Rent**" means the monthly rent in clause 3.1; "**Security Deposit**" means the deposit in clause 4; "**Break Date**" has the meaning in clause 9.1; and "**Building**" means Contoh Tower, including its common areas and building services.

## 2. TERM

2.1 The Landlord lets the Premises to the Tenant for a term of two (2) years, starting on 1 April 2026 and ending on 31 March 2028.

2.2 The Landlord shall give the Tenant access to the Premises on 1 April 2026 for fit-out. The first two (2) months of the Term, from 1 April 2026 to 31 May 2026, are rent-free fit-out months. The Tenant shall still pay the utilities it uses and the service charge during that period.

## 3. RENT AND OUTGOINGS

3.1 From 1 June 2026, the Tenant shall pay monthly rent of S$6,200 (the "**Rent**"), in advance, on or before the first (1st) day of each month, by bank transfer to the account the Landlord notifies in writing. The Rent is fixed for the whole Term.

3.2 The Rent is exclusive of goods and services tax, which the Tenant shall pay in addition at the rate set by law.

3.3 The Tenant shall pay the electricity, water, telephone and internet charges for the Premises, and the Building's service charge of S$480 a month, which the Landlord shall not increase during the Term except to pass on an increase in the charges set by the Building's management body.

3.4 If any sum is not paid within fourteen (14) days after the due date, the Tenant shall pay interest on the late amount at the rate of four per cent (4%) a year above the prime lending rate of the Landlord's bank, from the due date until payment.

## 4. SECURITY DEPOSIT

4.1 On signing this Agreement, the Tenant shall pay the Landlord a security deposit of S$12,400, equal to two (2) months' Rent (the "**Security Deposit**").

4.2 The Landlord may deduct from the Security Deposit any Rent or other sum that the Tenant has not paid when due, and the cost of repairing damage to the Premises caused by the Tenant beyond fair wear and tear.

4.3 The Landlord shall return the Security Deposit, less any lawful deduction supported by a written statement, within thirty (30) days after the Tenant has handed back the Premises.

## 5. USE OF THE PREMISES

5.1 The Tenant shall use the Premises only as a general office and for no other purpose without the Landlord's written consent, which the Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold.

5.2 The Tenant shall comply with the Building's house rules as notified to it in writing, and with all laws that apply to its use of the Premises. The Tenant shall not cause a nuisance or damage to other occupiers of the Building.

5.3 The Tenant shall not store dangerous or flammable goods in the Premises. Samples of roasted coffee for tasting and sourcing meetings are permitted in quantities reasonable for an office.

## 6. FIT-OUT AND ALTERATIONS

6.1 The Tenant may fit out the Premises at its own cost, after the Landlord has approved the plans in writing, which approval shall not be unreasonably withheld or delayed. The Tenant shall use contractors that follow the Building's rules and shall comply with the requirements of the relevant authorities.

6.2 At the end of the Term the Tenant shall remove only the partitions and cabling it installed that the Landlord asks in writing to be removed, at least three (3) months before the end of the Term, and shall make good any damage caused by the removal. The Tenant is not required to reinstate the Premises to a bare shell.

## 7. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE

7.1 The Landlord shall keep in good repair the structure of the Building and the Premises, including the roof, external walls, floors, common areas, lifts and the main air-conditioning, electrical, plumbing and fire-safety systems, and shall carry out repairs within a reasonable time after the Tenant tells it of the need.

7.2 The Tenant shall keep the inside of the Premises clean and in good order, and shall repair any damage that it or its visitors cause, but is not responsible for fair wear and tear.

## 8. ENTRY BY THE LANDLORD

8.1 The Landlord may enter the Premises at reasonable times, on at least forty-eight (48) hours' written notice, to inspect or repair them, except in an emergency when no notice is needed. The Landlord shall try not to disturb the Tenant's work and shall be accompanied by the Tenant's representative if the Tenant asks.

## 9. BREAK CLAUSE

9.1 The Tenant may end this Agreement early by giving the Landlord at least three (3) months' written notice, expiring on or after 31 March 2027, which is twelve (12) months after the start of the Term (the "**Break Date**"). For example, notice given on 31 December 2026 may take effect on 31 March 2027 or any later date.

9.2 On the Break Date the Tenant shall pay all Rent and other sums due up to that date, and hand back the Premises in the condition required by clause 6.2. The Landlord shall return the Security Deposit under clause 4.3. No other compensation is payable for ending the Agreement under this clause.

## 10. DEFAULT AND TERMINATION

10.1 If the Rent is unpaid for twenty-one (21) days after the due date and the Tenant has not paid within fourteen (14) days of a written reminder, the Landlord may end this Agreement by written notice and take back the Premises, without affecting its right to claim the sums due.

10.2 If the Tenant breaks any other term of this Agreement and does not fix the breach within thirty (30) days of the Landlord's written notice, the Landlord may end this Agreement by written notice.

10.3 If the Landlord breaks this Agreement and does not fix the breach within thirty (30) days of the Tenant's written notice, the Tenant may end this Agreement by written notice and recover its proven losses.

## 11. INSURANCE AND LIABILITY

11.1 The Landlord shall insure the Building against fire and the usual risks. The Tenant shall insure its own contents and shall carry public liability insurance of at least S$1,000,000 for each claim.

11.2 If the Premises cannot be used because of damage by fire or another insured risk, the Rent stops until they can be used again. If this lasts more than four (4) months, either party may end this Agreement by written notice.

## 12. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

12.1 The Tenant shall not assign or sublet the Premises without the Landlord's written consent, which the Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, but the Tenant may let a Kopi Kita group company share the Premises without consent if it tells the Landlord in writing.

## 13. RENEWAL

13.1 This Agreement does not renew automatically. If the Tenant would like to stay after 31 March 2028, it should tell the Landlord in writing at least six (6) months before the end of the Term, and the parties shall talk in good faith about new terms.

## 14. STAMP DUTY AND COSTS

14.1 The Tenant shall pay the stamp duty on this Agreement under the Stamp Duties Act and shall have it stamped on time. Each party bears its own legal fees.

## 15. NOTICES

15.1 Notices shall be in writing and sent by hand, by registered post or by email to the addresses above. A notice is treated as received on delivery by hand, two (2) business days after posting, or on the next business day after email.

## 16. GENERAL

16.1 This Agreement is the whole agreement between the parties about the Premises. Any change must be in writing and signed by both parties.

16.2 A person who is not a party to this Agreement has no right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 2001 to enforce any of its terms.

16.3 This Agreement is governed by the law of Singapore. The courts of Singapore shall have jurisdiction over any dispute.
