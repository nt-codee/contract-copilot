# INTERNSHIP AGREEMENT: MARKETING INTERN

This Internship Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 7 September 2026 (the "**Effective Date**").

**BETWEEN**

(1) **KOPI KITA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1000), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (the "**Host**");

(2) **MR. DANIEL CONTOH** (No. K/P: SAMPLE-000000), of No. 27, Jalan Contoh 2/4, Taman Contoh, 40150 Shah Alam, Selangor, Malaysia (the "**Intern**"); and

(3) **POLITEKNIK CONTOH** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-7210), an institution with its address at Jalan Contoh Politeknik, 40450 Shah Alam, Selangor, Malaysia (the "**Institution**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Intern is a final-year student of the Institution on a Diploma in Digital Marketing and must complete a work placement as part of his programme.

(B) The Host operates twelve cafe outlets and a central roastery and has agreed to host the Intern in its marketing team on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context requires otherwise:

"**Internship**" means the work placement described in Clause 2.

"**Internship Period**" means the period from 7 September 2026 to 6 March 2027.

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information of the Host that the Intern learns during the Internship, including sales figures, plans for campaigns and launches, recipes, supplier terms, customer data and staff information.

"**Work**" means all designs, copy, photographs, videos, posts, reports and other materials created by the Intern during the Internship.

## 2. THE INTERNSHIP

2.1 The Host shall place the Intern in its marketing team at the head office in Kuala Lumpur for the Internship Period, as a Marketing Intern reporting to the Marketing Manager.

2.2 The Intern will help with social media content, campaign calendars, photography at outlets, customer feedback analysis and reports. The Host will give the Intern a named mentor and set learning goals with the Institution at the start.

2.3 The Intern works five (5) days a week, Monday to Friday, from 9.00 a.m. to 6.00 p.m., with a one-hour lunch break. The Intern is not required to work on public holidays.

2.4 The Intern is a student on placement. This Agreement does not create a contract of service between the Host and the Intern, but the Host shall provide a safe workplace and brief the Intern on safety and hygiene rules.

## 3. ALLOWANCE

3.1 The Host shall pay the Intern a monthly allowance of RM 600.00, paid by bank transfer on or before the last working day of each month. The allowance is pro-rated for any part month.

3.2 The Host shall reimburse the reasonable cost of travel the Host asks the Intern to make between outlets on Host business, on production of receipts. The Intern is not otherwise entitled to any salary, bonus or benefit.

3.3 The Intern is responsible for the costs of his own accommodation and daily travel to the head office.

## 4. TERM AND END OF THE INTERNSHIP

4.1 The Internship starts on 7 September 2026 and ends on 6 March 2027, unless it is ended earlier under this Clause.

4.2 The Host may end the Internship at any time by giving the Intern and the Institution twenty-four (24) hours' written notice.

4.3 The Intern may end the Internship by giving the Host and the Institution thirty (30) days' written notice.

4.4 The Host may end the Internship immediately, without notice, if the Intern commits serious misconduct, breaches Clause 6 or damages the Host's reputation.

4.5 On the end of the Internship the Intern shall return all property of the Host, including access cards, devices and documents, and shall remove his personal materials from Host systems.

## 5. CERTIFICATE AND ASSESSMENT

5.1 The Host shall complete the Institution's assessment forms at the mid-point and at the end of the Internship.

5.2 The Host shall issue a certificate of completion only if the Intern completes the full Internship Period. An Intern who leaves or is dismissed before the end of the Internship Period will not be issued any certificate or letter confirming the work done.

5.3 The Host will answer reasonable requests from the Institution about the Intern's attendance and progress.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 The Intern shall keep Confidential Information secret during the Internship and for five (5) years afterwards, and shall use it only for the Internship.

6.2 This obligation does not apply to information that is public other than through the Intern's breach, or that the Intern is required by law to disclose.

6.3 The Intern shall not post about the Host's business on his personal social media accounts without the Marketing Manager's prior approval, other than content the Host has published itself.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

7.1 All intellectual property rights in the Work, whenever created and whether or not in the course of working hours, belong to the Host from creation. The Intern assigns to the Host all rights in the Work, including copyright, and waives any moral rights to the extent the law allows.

7.2 The Intern shall sign any document the Host reasonably requires to confirm this assignment.

7.3 The Intern may show the Work in his Institution portfolio only with the Host's written consent, which the Host will not unreasonably withhold for academic assessment.

## 8. PERSONAL DATA

8.1 The Host shall process the personal data of the Intern for administration of the Internship, payment of the allowance and reporting to the Institution, in line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

8.2 The Intern shall handle any customer personal data he sees in the course of the Internship only as instructed by the Host, and shall not copy it out of the Host's systems.

## 9. CONDUCT AND INSURANCE

9.1 The Intern shall follow the Host's rules on dress, hygiene, attendance and use of company devices, and shall behave professionally towards colleagues, customers and suppliers.

9.2 The Host will register the Intern for workplace injury cover to the extent the law requires. The Institution is responsible for any student insurance it provides.

## 10. THE INSTITUTION

10.1 The Institution confirms that the Intern is a registered student and that the Internship forms part of his programme. The Institution will name a academic tutor as the contact for the Host.

10.2 The Institution is not liable for the Intern's acts at the Host's premises, and does not take on any payment obligation under this Agreement.

## 11. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Malaysia. The parties shall first try to settle any dispute by discussion, with the Institution's academic tutor present.

11.2 Failing settlement within thirty (30) days, the courts of Kuala Lumpur shall have jurisdiction.

## 12. GENERAL

12.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject. Any change must be in writing and signed by all three parties.

12.2 Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses above or by email to the address each party has given in writing.

12.3 If any part of this Agreement is unenforceable, the rest remains in force. A person who is not a party has no right to enforce it.
