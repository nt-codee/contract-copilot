# EQUIPMENT FINANCE AND LEASE AGREEMENT: POINT-OF-SALE TERMINALS

This Equipment Finance and Lease Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 22 June 2026 (the "**Effective Date**").

**BETWEEN**

(1) **ORCHARD CONTOH LEASING PTE. LTD.** (UEN SAMPLE-SG-7301), a company incorporated in Singapore with its registered office at 18 Contoh Road, #12-04 Contoh Tower, Singapore 000731 (the "**Lessor**"); and

(2) **KOPI KITA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1000), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (the "**Lessee**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Lessor provides equipment finance and leasing to businesses in Singapore and the region.

(B) The Lessee operates a group of cafe outlets and wishes to equip its Singapore pop-up counters and its Kuala Lumpur outlets with a standard point-of-sale system.

(C) The Lessor has agreed to purchase the Equipment from the Supplier and to lease it to the Lessee for the Term on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Equipment**" means the twenty-four (24) point-of-sale terminals, card readers, receipt printers and cash drawers described in Schedule 1, with all software installed on them at delivery, and all replacements, parts and additions.

"**Supplier**" means the vendor of the Equipment named in Schedule 1.

"**Term**" means thirty-six (36) months beginning on the Effective Date and ending on 21 June 2029, unless extended under Clause 12.

"**Rental**" means the monthly rental of S$ 3,420.00, exclusive of goods and services tax, payable in advance on the 22nd day of each month.

"**Total Rentals**" means the Rental multiplied by thirty-six (36), being S$ 123,120.00.

"**Delivery Date**" means the date the Lessee signs the delivery certificate in Schedule 2.

"**Purchase Option Price**" means the fair market value of the Equipment at the end of the Term, as agreed by the parties or, failing agreement, determined by an independent valuer, but not less than S$ 6,000.00.

1.2 A reference to a statute includes that statute as amended or re-enacted. Headings do not affect interpretation.

## 2. LEASE

2.1 The Lessor shall purchase the Equipment from the Supplier and lease it to the Lessee for the Term. The Lessee shall take the lease on the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The Equipment remains the property of the Lessor at all times. The Lessee shall not do anything to suggest that it is the owner. The Lessee shall keep the Equipment free of any charge, lien or encumbrance and shall allow the Lessor to affix plates showing the Lessor's ownership.

2.3 The Lessee has no right to buy the Equipment during the Term. At the end of the Term, and if the Lessee is not then in default, the Lessee may by written notice given at least sixty (60) days before the end of the Term buy all (but not some) of the Equipment for the Purchase Option Price, in its existing condition.

## 3. DELIVERY AND ACCEPTANCE

3.1 The Supplier shall deliver the Equipment to the Lessee's premises listed in Schedule 1. The Lessee shall inspect the Equipment within three (3) business days of delivery and sign the delivery certificate if the Equipment is in good order.

3.2 If the Lessee finds the Equipment faulty or not as ordered, it shall tell the Lessor and the Supplier in writing within the same three (3) business day period. The Lessor shall not be required to accept the Equipment until the fault is cured.

## 4. SUPPLIER WARRANTY AND SUPPORT

4.1 The Lessee selected the Equipment and the Supplier. The Lessor gives no warranty as to the Equipment's quality, fitness or performance, and all implied terms are excluded to the extent the law allows.

4.2 The Lessor assigns to the Lessee, for the Term, the benefit of the Supplier's warranties and support. The Lessee shall enforce them at its own cost and shall tell the Lessor of any claim.

## 5. RENTAL AND PAYMENT

5.1 The Lessee shall pay the Rental in advance on the 22nd day of each month for thirty-six (36) months, the first Rental being due on the Delivery Date.

5.2 The Lessee's obligation to pay the Rental and every other sum under this Agreement is absolute and unconditional. It shall be paid in full without set-off, counterclaim, deduction or withholding, and shall not be reduced, suspended or ended for any reason, including any defect in, damage to, loss of, or failure of the Equipment, any interruption of the Lessee's business, any dispute with the Supplier, or any interruption of the payment network, even if the Equipment cannot be used for any part of the Term.

5.3 If the Lessee is required by law to withhold tax, it shall increase the payment so that the Lessor receives the amount it would have received without the withholding.

5.4 Payments shall be made by GIRO or telegraphic transfer in Singapore dollars. Where the Lessee pays from a Malaysian account, the Lessee bears all bank charges and exchange costs.

5.5 The Lessee shall pay goods and services tax on the Rental at the prevailing rate.

## 6. USE AND MAINTENANCE

6.1 The Lessee shall use the Equipment only in the ordinary course of its cafe business, at the premises in Schedule 1, in accordance with the Supplier's instructions.

6.2 The Lessee shall not move the Equipment from the listed premises without the Lessor's written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld for a move between the Lessee's own outlets.

6.3 The Lessee shall keep the Equipment in good working order and shall maintain a support contract with the Supplier for the Term. The Lessor shall bear no cost of repair.

6.4 The Lessee shall keep the software on the Equipment updated, and shall comply with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard as it applies to the Lessee.

## 7. RISK AND INSURANCE

7.1 From delivery until the Equipment is returned to the Lessor, all risk of loss, theft, damage or destruction of the Equipment lies with the Lessee. No such event reduces or delays the Rental.

7.2 The Lessee shall insure the Equipment for its full replacement value against all usual risks, with the Lessor as loss payee, and shall give the Lessor a copy of the policy on request.

7.3 If the Equipment is lost or destroyed, the Lessee shall pay the Lessor, on the next Rental date, the Rental for the rest of the Term plus the Purchase Option Price, less any insurance proceeds the Lessor actually receives.

## 8. DATA ON THE EQUIPMENT

8.1 The Lessee is the controller of all customer, sales and card data on the Equipment. Before returning any Equipment, the Lessee shall remove that data. The Lessor does not access it.

8.2 The Lessor shall comply with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 of Singapore in respect of personal data it receives from the Lessee about its directors and staff, and shall use it only for administering this Agreement and for credit checks.

## 9. LIABILITY

9.1 The Lessor is not liable for any indirect or consequential loss, loss of revenue, or loss of data suffered by the Lessee.

9.2 The Lessor's total liability in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the Rental paid in the twelve (12) months before the claim arose.

9.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for fraud, for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for anything else that cannot be limited under Singapore law.

## 10. DEFAULT

10.1 Each of the following is an event of default:

(a) the Lessee does not pay any sum within seven (7) days of its due date;

(b) the Lessee breaches any other term and, if it can be remedied, does not remedy it within fourteen (14) days of notice;

(c) the Lessee is unable to pay its debts, enters into any arrangement with its creditors, or has a receiver, judicial manager or liquidator appointed, or an equivalent step is taken in any country; or

(d) the Lessee ceases to carry on business.

10.2 On an event of default the Lessor may, by notice, end the lease, require the Lessee to return the Equipment at the Lessee's cost, and recover from the Lessee the unpaid Rental to the end of the Term discounted at three per cent (3%) per annum, together with interest, costs and any loss on resale of the Equipment.

10.3 The Lessee shall pay interest on overdue sums at two per cent (2%) per annum above the Lessor's prevailing base rate, from the due date to the date of payment.

## 11. EARLY TERMINATION

11.1 The Lessee may end this Agreement before the end of the Term only by returning the Equipment and paying the Lessor all Rentals for the remainder of the Term, discounted at three per cent (3%) per annum.

## 12. END OF TERM AND RETURN

12.1 At the end of the Term, unless the Lessee has bought the Equipment under Clause 2.3, the Lessee shall return the Equipment to the Lessor's warehouse in Singapore within seven (7) days, at its own cost and risk, packed to the Lessor's reasonable satisfaction, in good condition apart from fair wear and tear.

12.2 If the Lessee does not return all of the Equipment within seven (7) days after the end of the Term, the lease is automatically extended for a further twelve (12) months on the same terms, at one hundred per cent (100%) of the Rental, and continues to extend for further periods of twelve (12) months until the Equipment is returned and the Lessor has accepted it in writing.

12.3 The Lessor shall inspect the returned Equipment within fourteen (14) days of receipt and shall tell the Lessee of any damage beyond fair wear and tear, which the Lessee shall pay for at the reasonable cost of repair.

## 13. NOTICES

13.1 Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, sent by registered post or sent by email to the addresses in this Agreement or as later notified. A notice by hand is received on delivery, a notice by registered post three (3) business days after posting, and a notice by email on the next business day.

## 14. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

14.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.

14.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration in Singapore under the Arbitration Rules of the Singapore International Arbitration Centre for the time being in force. The tribunal shall consist of one arbitrator, and the language of the arbitration shall be English.

14.3 A person who is not a party to this Agreement has no right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 2001 to enforce any of its terms.

## 15. GENERAL

15.1 This Agreement, with its Schedules, is the whole agreement on the lease of the Equipment.

15.2 A variation must be in writing signed by both parties.

15.3 The Lessee may not assign or sub-lease. The Lessor may assign its rights on notice to the Lessee.

15.4 A failure or delay in exercising a right is not a waiver.

15.5 If a provision is held invalid, the rest remains in effect.

15.6 This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
