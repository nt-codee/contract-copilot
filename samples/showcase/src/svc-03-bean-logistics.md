# GREEN BEAN IMPORT AND LOGISTICS AGREEMENT

This Green Bean Import and Logistics Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 10 November 2025 (the "**Effective Date**").

**BETWEEN**

(1) **STRAITS MERIDIAN FREIGHT PTE. LTD.** (UEN SAMPLE-SG-7103), a company incorporated in Singapore with its registered office at 7 Contoh Quay, #11-05, Singapore 000703 (the "**Carrier**"); and

(2) **KOPI KITA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1000), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur (the "**Customer**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Customer roasts specialty coffee at its central roastery in Malaysia and imports green coffee beans from origin countries in containerised shipments.

(B) The Carrier provides ocean and road freight forwarding, customs clearance, bonded warehousing and last-mile delivery services between origin ports, Singapore and Malaysia.

(C) The Customer wishes to appoint the Carrier to carry and store its green coffee imports on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Contract Year**" means each period of twelve (12) months starting on the Effective Date or an anniversary of it.

"**Freight Charges**" means the charges for carriage of a Shipment set out in the Rate Card in Schedule 1.

"**Goods**" means green coffee beans in bags or bulk liners.

"**Minimum Volume**" means forty-eight (48) twenty-foot container equivalents ("**TEU**") of Goods in each Contract Year.

"**Services**" means freight forwarding, customs clearance, warehousing, handling and delivery of Goods.

"**Shipment**" means a consignment of Goods carried under one bill of lading.

"**Term**" has the meaning in Clause 7.

## 2. APPOINTMENT AND SERVICES

2.1 The Customer appoints the Carrier to provide the Services for Shipments requested by the Customer during the Term, and the Carrier accepts the appointment.

2.2 The Customer shall give the Carrier a booking request at least twenty-one (21) days before the intended loading date, stating the origin, quantity and required delivery date. The Carrier shall confirm or decline the booking within three (3) business days.

2.3 The Carrier shall carry and store the Goods with the care of a professional carrier, shall keep them dry and ventilated, and shall give the Customer tracking updates at each port call.

2.4 The Carrier shall clear Goods through customs on the Customer's behalf on the basis of information supplied by the Customer, and shall give the Customer copies of all customs declarations.

## 3. CHARGES AND PAYMENT

3.1 The Customer shall pay the Freight Charges and the warehousing and handling charges in the Rate Card. The Carrier may pass on any surcharge imposed by a shipping line, port or authority at cost.

3.2 The Carrier shall invoice each Shipment on loading. The Customer shall pay each invoice within fourteen (14) days of the invoice date.

3.3 Any amount not paid when due bears interest at the rate of eighteen per cent (18%) per annum, accruing daily from the due date until full payment, both before and after judgment.

3.4 The Carrier may withhold delivery of any Goods and may sell them to recover unpaid amounts, on seven (7) days' notice to the Customer, if any invoice is overdue.

## 4. MINIMUM VOLUME AND EXCLUSIVITY

4.1 The Customer shall ship not less than the Minimum Volume through the Carrier in each Contract Year.

4.2 If the Customer ships less than the Minimum Volume in a Contract Year, it shall pay the Carrier, within seven (7) days of the Carrier's invoice, an amount equal to the Freight Charges at the standard Rate Card for each TEU of the shortfall, whether or not the Carrier has carried or stored any Goods for that shortfall. A shortfall in one Contract Year cannot be made up in a later one.

4.3 During the Term, the Customer shall use the Carrier as its sole and exclusive provider of freight forwarding and warehousing services for all green coffee it imports into Malaysia or Singapore, and shall not engage another provider for any such Services.

## 5. RISK, LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY

5.1 Risk in the Goods passes to the Carrier on loading and returns to the Customer on delivery at the named destination.

5.2 The Customer is liable to the Carrier for all loss, damage, delay, penalty, demurrage, detention, fine and expense of any kind arising out of or in connection with the Customer's performance of, or any breach of, this Agreement, and for any claim by a third party relating to the Goods. The Customer's liability under this Agreement is not limited in amount.

5.3 The liability of the Carrier for loss of or damage to Goods, or for delay, shall not exceed the Freight Charges actually paid by the Customer for the Shipment in respect of which the claim arises. The Carrier is not liable for any indirect or consequential loss, loss of profit or loss of market.

5.4 The Customer shall indemnify the Carrier against all claims, losses and costs, including legal costs on a full indemnity basis, arising from the Goods, from information supplied by the Customer, or from any act or omission of the Customer.

5.5 Any claim by the Customer against the Carrier must be notified in writing within seven (7) days of delivery, or of the date on which delivery should have occurred, failing which the Carrier is discharged from all liability.

5.6 The Customer is responsible for insuring the Goods. The Carrier does not insure the Goods unless the Customer instructs it in writing to do so and pays the premium.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential the other's non-public information, including pricing, supplier identities and shipping volumes, and shall use it only to perform this Agreement.

6.2 Each party shall comply with the data protection laws that apply to it, including the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 of Singapore and the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia, in respect of any personal data of contact persons exchanged under this Agreement.

## 7. TERM AND RENEWAL

7.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for twenty-four (24) months, ending on 9 November 2027 (the "**Initial Term**").

7.2 At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for a further period of twenty-four (24) months, and at the end of each renewal period for a further twenty-four (24) months, unless the Customer gives the Carrier not less than one hundred and twenty (120) days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current period. For the Initial Term, the notice must be received on or before 12 July 2027.

7.3 Notice of non-renewal is effective only if it is sent by registered post to the Carrier's registered office. A notice sent by email, courier or any other means is not effective for this purpose.

7.4 The Initial Term and every renewal period are together the "**Term**".

## 8. TERMINATION

8.1 The Carrier may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving thirty (30) days' written notice, or immediately by written notice if the Customer is late in paying any invoice or breaches this Agreement.

8.2 The Customer may terminate this Agreement only if the Carrier commits a material breach which is not remedied within sixty (60) days of the Customer's written notice.

8.3 If this Agreement ends before the end of the Term for any reason other than the Carrier's uncured material breach, the Customer shall pay to the Carrier, within seven (7) days of demand, an amount equal to the Freight Charges for the Minimum Volume for each remaining Contract Year or part of one, together with all other fees that would have been payable up to the end of the Term, as a genuine pre-estimate of the Carrier's loss.

8.4 Termination does not affect rights accrued before it. Clauses 3, 4.2, 5, 6, 8.3, 9 and 10 survive termination.

## 9. GOVERNING LAW AND ARBITRATION

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.

9.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including any question regarding its existence, validity or termination, shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre ("**SIAC**") in accordance with the Arbitration Rules of the SIAC for the time being in force. The seat of arbitration is Singapore. The tribunal shall consist of one arbitrator. The language of the arbitration is English.

9.3 Nothing in this Clause prevents the Carrier from applying to any court for an order to enforce payment or to recover or sell Goods.

9.4 A person who is not a party to this Agreement has no right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 2001 of Singapore to enforce any of its terms.

## 10. NOTICES

10.1 Notices to the Carrier must be addressed to its registered office. Notices to the Customer may be sent to the address above or by email to its finance department, and are treated as received two (2) days after dispatch.

11.1 This Agreement, with its Schedules, is the entire agreement of the parties on its subject and replaces earlier proposals and dealings.

11.2 The Carrier may subcontract any part of the Services. The Customer may not assign this Agreement or any right under it without the Carrier's written consent.

11.3 The Carrier may amend the Rate Card on thirty (30) days' notice to reflect changes in its costs.

11.4 The Carrier is not liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including port congestion, strikes, weather and shipping line schedule changes.

11.5 The Customer waives any right of set-off or counterclaim against sums due to the Carrier.
