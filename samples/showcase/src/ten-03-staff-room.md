# ROOM RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Room Rental Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 1 July 2026.

**BETWEEN**

(1) **PUAN ZAINAB CONTOH** (No. K/P: SAMPLE-000000), of Unit 12-08, Residensi Contoh, Jalan Contoh 5/2, 47810 Petaling Jaya, Selangor (the "**Landlord**"); and

(2) **KOPI KITA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1000), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur (the "**Tenant**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Landlord owns the condominium unit at Unit 12-08, Residensi Contoh, Jalan Contoh 5/2, 47810 Petaling Jaya, Selangor (the "**Unit**").

(B) The Tenant trains barista staff who come from outside the Klang Valley and needs accommodation for them while they train. The Landlord has agreed to rent the master bedroom of the Unit (the "**Room**"), with shared use of the kitchen, living area and the facilities of the condominium, to the Tenant for use by trainees nominated by the Tenant.

## 1. THE ROOM

1.1 The Landlord rents the Room to the Tenant, with the bed, wardrobe, desk and air-conditioner listed in the inventory signed by both parties. The Room may be occupied by one (1) trainee at a time, nominated by the Tenant.

1.2 The Landlord shall give the Tenant two (2) access cards for the condominium and two (2) keys for the Unit on 1 July 2026.

## 2. TERM

2.1 The rental is for twelve (12) months, starting on 1 July 2026 and ending on 30 June 2027.

2.2 If the parties wish to continue after 30 June 2027, they shall agree the new terms in writing at least thirty (30) days before the end of the term. This Agreement does not renew automatically.

## 3. RENT

3.1 The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of RM1,400 for the Room, on or before the fifth (5th) day of each month, by bank transfer to the account the Landlord notifies in writing. The Landlord shall give a receipt on request.

3.2 The rent includes the condominium maintenance fee, the sinking fund, water and the internet service. The rent does not include electricity or the cost of the air-conditioner's electricity use, which are dealt with in clause 6.

3.3 If the rent is not paid within seven (7) days after the due date, the Landlord may charge interest at one per cent (1%) a month on the late amount until payment.

## 4. SECURITY DEPOSIT

4.1 On signing this Agreement, the Tenant shall pay a security deposit of RM2,800, equal to two (2) months' rent, and a utility deposit of RM300 (together, the "**Deposit**").

4.2 The Landlord holds the Deposit as security for unpaid rent, unpaid bills and damage to the Room or the Unit beyond fair wear and tear.

## 5. USE OF THE ROOM

5.1 The Room shall be used only as a residence for one trainee at a time. The trainee shall keep the Room and the shared areas clean, observe the rules of the condominium, and respect the Landlord and other occupants of the Unit, especially after 11 pm.

5.2 The trainee shall not smoke in the Unit, keep pets, hold parties, or store dangerous goods.

5.3 The Tenant is responsible for the acts of the trainees it nominates and shall pay for damage that they cause beyond fair wear and tear.

## 6. UTILITIES

6.1 The Tenant shall pay for the electricity used in the Unit that is attributable to the Room and the shared areas, calculated by usage on the submeter or, if there is no submeter, by dividing the monthly electricity bill for the Unit by the number of persons then living in the Unit. The Landlord shall give the Tenant a copy of the bill within seven (7) days of receiving it, and the Tenant shall pay within seven (7) days afterwards.

6.2 The Tenant shall pay all utilities charges by usage at the rate charged by the utility provider, without any maximum amount.

## 7. RENT REVIEW

7.1 After the first six (6) months of the term, that is, from 1 January 2027, the Landlord may increase the monthly rent by up to ten per cent (10%) by giving the Tenant thirty (30) days' written notice. The increased rent applies for the rest of the term.

## 8. CHANGE OF OCCUPANT AND SUBLETTING

8.1 The Tenant shall not sublet the Room, allow anyone other than the nominated trainee to live in it, or change the nominated trainee, without the Landlord's prior written consent. The Landlord may take reasonable time to consider a request and may ask to meet the new trainee.

8.2 A trainee's overnight guests shall not stay more than three (3) nights in a row without telling the Landlord.

## 9. REPAIRS AND ENTRY

9.1 The Landlord is responsible for the structure of the Unit, the plumbing, the wiring and the air-conditioner's servicing, and shall carry out repairs within fourteen (14) days of being told, or sooner in an emergency.

9.2 The Tenant is responsible for minor repairs and for damage caused by the trainee or the trainee's guests.

9.3 The Landlord may enter the Room to inspect or repair it, on at least forty-eight (48) hours' notice, except in an emergency.

## 10. EARLY TERMINATION

10.1 The Tenant may end this Agreement before 30 June 2027 only by giving the Landlord two (2) months' written notice. If the Tenant ends this Agreement before the end of the term, in any way, the Deposit is forfeited to the Landlord in full as agreed compensation, whatever the Landlord's actual loss.

10.2 The Landlord may end this Agreement before the end of the term only if the Tenant fails to pay rent for fourteen (14) days after a written reminder, or seriously breaks this Agreement and does not fix the breach within fourteen (14) days of written notice.

10.3 If this Agreement ends at the end of the term, the Landlord shall return the Deposit within thirty (30) days after the Tenant hands back the keys and access cards, less any amount lawfully deducted under clause 4.2.

## 11. HANDING BACK

11.1 At the end of the Agreement, the trainee shall remove personal belongings, leave the Room in the condition recorded in the inventory, apart from fair wear and tear, and return the keys and access cards.

## 12. PERSONAL DATA

12.1 The Landlord shall use the personal information of the trainees only to manage this rental and to comply with condominium rules, and shall not share it with others except as the law or the condominium management requires, in line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

## 13. STAMP DUTY AND NOTICES

13.1 The parties shall share the stamp duty on this Agreement equally, and the Tenant shall have the Agreement stamped within thirty (30) days of signing, in accordance with the Stamp Act 1949.

13.2 Notices shall be in writing and sent to the addresses above, by hand, by registered post or by email. A notice is treated as received on delivery by hand, two (2) days after posting, or on the next business day after email.

## 14. GENERAL

14.1 This Agreement is the whole agreement between the parties about the Room. Any change must be in writing and signed by both parties.

14.2 This Agreement is governed by the law of Malaysia, including the Contracts Act 1950. The courts of Malaysia shall have jurisdiction over any dispute.
