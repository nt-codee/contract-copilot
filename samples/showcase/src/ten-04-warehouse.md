# TENANCY AGREEMENT (WAREHOUSE)

This Tenancy Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 1 November 2025.

**BETWEEN**

(1) **KLANG SELATAN LOGISTIK HAB SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-7401), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 25, Jalan Contoh Perindustrian 3, Kawasan Perindustrian Contoh, 41200 Klang, Selangor (the "**Landlord**"); and

(2) **KOPI KITA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1000), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur (the "**Tenant**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Landlord is the registered proprietor of a single-storey detached warehouse at Lot 22, Jalan Contoh Perindustrian 3, Kawasan Perindustrian Contoh, 41200 Klang, Selangor, with a built-up area of about 9,500 square feet and an open yard (the "**Premises**").

(B) The Tenant roasts coffee at its central roastery and wishes to rent the Premises to store green coffee beans, packaging and finished stock, and the Landlord has agreed to let the Premises on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement: "**Term**" means the period in clause 2.1; "**Rent**" means the monthly rent in clause 3.1; "**Deposit**" means the deposit in clause 4.1; and "**Business Day**" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Selangor.

## 2. TERM

2.1 The Landlord lets the Premises to the Tenant for a term of two (2) years, starting on 1 November 2025 and ending on 31 October 2027, subject to clause 9.

2.2 The Landlord shall hand over the Premises on 1 November 2025 in the condition seen by the Tenant. The Tenant has a rent-free period of fourteen (14) days after handover to move in.

## 3. RENT AND PAYMENT

3.1 The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of RM14,000 (the "**Rent**"), in advance, on or before the first (1st) day of each month, by bank transfer to the account the Landlord notifies in writing.

3.2 The Rent is exclusive of any sales and service tax, which the Tenant shall pay in addition if it applies. The Tenant shall also pay for the electricity, water, telephone and internet used at the Premises, and the quit rent and assessment for the Premises.

3.3 If any sum is not paid when due, the Tenant shall pay a late charge of two per cent (2%) of the unpaid amount for each week or part of a week that it remains unpaid, from the due date until payment in full.

## 4. DEPOSIT

4.1 On signing this Agreement, the Tenant shall pay the Landlord a security deposit of RM84,000, equal to six (6) months' Rent (the "**Deposit**").

4.2 The Deposit is held as security for the Tenant's obligations under this Agreement. The Landlord may apply the Deposit towards any sum due from the Tenant.

4.3 If the Tenant breaches any term of this Agreement, the Deposit is forfeited to the Landlord as agreed compensation, without affecting the Landlord's right to claim any further loss.

4.4 Subject to clause 4.3, the Landlord shall return the Deposit, without interest, within sixty (60) days after the Tenant has handed back the Premises and the Landlord is satisfied that all obligations are met.

## 5. USE OF THE PREMISES

5.1 The Tenant shall use the Premises only for storing coffee, packaging and related goods and for loading and unloading, and shall not carry out roasting or retail sales there without the Landlord's written consent.

5.2 The Tenant shall obtain and keep in force at its own cost all licences and permits needed for its use of the Premises and shall comply with all laws and the requirements of the local authority and the fire department.

5.3 The Tenant shall not cause a nuisance to neighbouring occupiers and shall keep the yard and access roads clear.

## 6. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE

6.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises in good repair and shall bear the cost of all repairs, maintenance and replacements of every kind to the Premises, including the roof, structure, foundation, external walls, floor slab, main drainage and main electrical supply, whether or not the need for repair was caused by the Tenant.

6.2 The Landlord may enter the Premises at any time on prior notice, which may be given orally, to inspect them, to carry out works, or to show them to others.

## 7. INSURANCE

7.1 The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord, within seven (7) days of demand, the premium for the Landlord's insurance of the Premises, including fire, special perils and loss of rental income, in the amount the Landlord chooses. The Tenant shall also insure its own stock and equipment and carry public liability insurance of at least RM1,000,000 for each claim.

## 8. RENT REVIEW

8.1 The Landlord may review the Rent at any time during the Term, in its sole discretion, and increase it by any amount by giving the Tenant seven (7) days' written notice. The Tenant shall pay the increased Rent from the date stated in the notice.

8.2 The Tenant has no right to dispute the increased Rent or to end this Agreement because of an increase.

## 9. TERMINATION

9.1 The Landlord may end this Agreement at any time, without giving any reason, by giving the Tenant at least fourteen (14) days' written notice, and the Tenant shall hand back the Premises when the notice ends.

9.2 The Tenant may end this Agreement before 31 October 2027 only by giving the Landlord at least six (6) months' written notice, and the Tenant shall continue to pay the Rent until the end of the notice period.

9.3 If any Rent is unpaid on the due date, or the Tenant breaks any other term of this Agreement, the Landlord may end this Agreement at once by written notice, re-enter the Premises, and keep the Tenant's goods until all sums due are paid.

9.4 Ending this Agreement does not affect any right or claim of the Landlord that arose before it ended.

## 10. DELIVERING UP THE PREMISES

10.1 At the end of this Agreement, the Tenant shall, at its own cost, remove all its goods, fixtures, racking, partitions, wiring, signage and installations, reinstate the Premises to a bare shell, repair all damage, and repaint the Premises. If the Tenant does not do so within seven (7) days, the Landlord may do the work and recover the cost from the Tenant, and the Tenant shall pay Rent at the rate then payable until the work is finished.

## 11. LANDLORD'S LIABILITY

11.1 The Landlord is not liable for any loss, damage or injury to the Tenant, its staff or its goods in the Premises, however caused, including by flood, fire, theft, leakage or the Landlord's own act or omission.

11.2 The Tenant shall indemnify the Landlord against all claims, loss and costs arising from the Tenant's use of the Premises.

## 12. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

12.1 The Tenant shall not assign or sublet the Premises, or part of them, or share possession of them, without the Landlord's prior written consent, which the Landlord may give or withhold in its absolute discretion. The Landlord may assign its rights under this Agreement at any time without consent.

## 13. RENEWAL

13.1 This Agreement does not renew automatically. Any renewal must be agreed in writing and the Landlord is not bound to renew.

## 14. STAMP DUTY AND COSTS

14.1 The Tenant shall bear all stamp duty and registration fees on this Agreement and its counterpart, and shall pay the Landlord's legal fees for preparing it. The Tenant shall pay the Landlord's costs of enforcing this Agreement on a solicitor-and-client basis.

## 15. NOTICES

15.1 Notices shall be in writing and sent to the addresses above. A notice from the Landlord is treated as received on the day it is sent, by any means. A notice from the Tenant is treated as received only when the Landlord acknowledges it in writing.

## 16. GENERAL

16.1 This Agreement is the whole agreement between the parties about the Premises. Any change must be in writing and signed by both parties.

16.2 A failure or delay in using a right is not a waiver of it. If any term is found unenforceable, the rest of this Agreement continues.

16.3 This Agreement is governed by the law of Malaysia, including the Contracts Act 1950 and the National Land Code. The courts of Kuala Lumpur shall have jurisdiction over any dispute.
