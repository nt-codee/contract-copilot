# CUSTOM ORDERING APP DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT

This Custom Ordering App Development Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 15 July 2026 (the "**Effective Date**").

**BETWEEN**

(1) **PIXELHAUS STUDIO PTE. LTD.** (UEN SAMPLE-SG-7108), a company incorporated in Singapore with its registered office at 41 Contoh Road, #02-17, Singapore 000841 (the "**Developer**"); and

(2) **KOPI KITA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1000), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur (the "**Client**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Client operates twelve (12) cafe outlets and a central roastery, and wishes to offer its customers an online ordering and pick-up app of its own.

(B) The Developer designs and builds mobile and web applications and has agreed to design, build and maintain the app described in Schedule 1 for the Client on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**App**" means the custom online ordering application, including its mobile apps for iOS and Android, web ordering page and administrator dashboard, as described in Schedule 1.

"**Change Request**" means a request by the Client, or a requirement arising during the Project, that adds to, alters or removes anything from the scope in Schedule 1.

"**Developer Materials**" means all software, source code, object code, designs, interfaces, database structures, algorithms, documentation, tools and know-how created, used or provided by the Developer in connection with the App, including the App itself.

"**Development Fee**" means S$96,000, exclusive of goods and services tax.

"**Go-Live**" means the release of the App to the public app stores.

"**Maintenance Services**" means the hosting, bug-fixing and support services in Clause 9.

"**Project**" means the design, build and delivery of the App up to 14 April 2027.

## 2. THE PROJECT

2.1 The Developer shall design, build, test and release the App in accordance with the milestones in Schedule 2, with the aim of Go-Live on or before 14 April 2027.

2.2 The Developer shall carry out the Project with reasonable skill and care using suitably qualified personnel.

2.3 The Client shall provide timely access to its menus, brand assets, outlet information and a nominated project contact, who shall give feedback on each deliverable within ten (10) business days. The Developer's dates are extended by any delay caused by the Client.

2.4 The Developer shall hold a progress meeting with the Client every two (2) weeks and shall give the Client access to a test version of the App after each milestone.

## 3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 The Client shall pay the Development Fee as follows:

(a) fifty per cent (50%) on the Effective Date, being a prepayment which is non-refundable once paid;

(b) twenty-five per cent (25%) on completion of the design milestone; and

(c) twenty-five per cent (25%) on Go-Live.

3.2 The Client shall pay each invoice within seven (7) days of the invoice date. Interest at the rate of two per cent (2%) per month is payable on overdue sums, and the Developer may suspend work or disable the App while any sum is overdue.

3.3 The Maintenance Services are charged at S$3,200 per month, starting on Go-Live.

3.4 The Client shall also pay the cost of third-party services required for the App, including app store accounts, payment gateway fees, mapping and messaging services, at cost plus fifteen per cent (15%).

## 4. CHANGE REQUESTS

4.1 The Developer shall carry out each Change Request requested in writing by the Client, whatever its size or effect on the App, and the Client shall pay for it at the Developer's then-current hourly rate, currently S$220 per hour, on the basis of the Developer's own estimate of the time required.

4.2 The Developer shall decide whether anything the Client asks for or expects is within the scope in Schedule 1 or is a Change Request, and its decision is final. The Developer may extend the delivery dates for any Change Request by any period it considers reasonable.

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 All intellectual property rights in the App and the Developer Materials, including everything created in the course of the Project and including the parts that were specially developed or paid for by the Client, belong to and are owned by the Developer from creation.

5.2 The Developer grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable licence to use the App for the Client's own business for as long as the Client remains in compliance with this Agreement and pays the Maintenance Services charges when due. The licence ends automatically on termination or expiry of this Agreement, or if the Client fails to pay any sum when due.

5.3 The Developer is not obliged to deliver, and the Client has no right to receive, the source code of the App or the Developer Materials, whether during or after the Term, and no source code escrow is provided.

5.4 The Client owns its trade marks, logos, menus and content ("**Client Content**") and grants the Developer a worldwide, perpetual, royalty-free licence to use the Client Content to provide the App and to promote the Developer's business.

5.5 The Developer may reuse the App, its design and its components for other clients, including competitors of the Client.

## 6. CLIENT DATA

6.1 The App will collect personal data of the Client's customers, such as names, phone numbers, order histories and payment tokens ("**Customer Data**"). The Developer shall process Customer Data only to provide the App and shall comply with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 of Singapore and, where it applies, the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia.

6.2 The Developer shall keep the Customer Data secure, and shall notify the Client within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of any data breach affecting it.

6.3 The Developer may export or delete Customer Data when this Agreement ends, and shall provide a copy to the Client on written request, at the Developer's then-current rates.

## 7. WARRANTIES AND LIABILITY

7.1 The App is provided as is. The Developer gives no warranty that the App will be error free, will meet the Client's requirements or will be fit for any particular purpose, and all conditions and warranties implied by law are excluded to the fullest extent permitted by law.

7.2 The total liability of the Developer to the Client arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall not exceed the Maintenance Services charges for one (1) month.

7.3 The Developer is not liable for any loss of profit, loss of revenue, loss of data, loss of goodwill or any indirect or consequential loss.

7.4 The Client shall indemnify the Developer, and keep it indemnified, against all losses, damages, liabilities, claims, costs and expenses of any kind (including legal fees on a full indemnity basis) suffered or incurred by the Developer arising out of or in connection with the Client's use of the App, the Client Content, any claim by a customer of the Client, or any breach of this Agreement by the Client.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 Each party shall keep the other's non-public information confidential and use it only for this Agreement. The Client's obligations extend to the Developer Materials, the App's design and the Developer's pricing.

8.2 This Clause does not apply to information that is public other than through breach or that must be disclosed by law.

## 9. MAINTENANCE SERVICES

9.1 From Go-Live, the Developer shall host the App, fix defects that the Developer accepts as defects, and provide support by email on business days. No response time or availability level is guaranteed.

9.2 The Maintenance Services begin on Go-Live and continue for twelve (12) months, and renew automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months unless the Client gives the Developer written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current period. The Developer may increase the monthly charge on each renewal by any amount on thirty (30) days' notice.

## 10. TERMINATION

10.1 The Developer may terminate this Agreement or suspend the Services at any time by written notice if the Client is late in paying any sum or breaches this Agreement, or on thirty (30) days' notice for any reason.

10.2 The Client may terminate this Agreement only for the Developer's material breach that is not remedied within sixty (60) days of the Client's written notice.

10.3 On termination for any reason, all sums that have been invoiced or would become due under Clause 3 and the Maintenance Services charges for the remainder of the then-current period become immediately payable, no sum paid is refundable, and the Client's licence under Clause 5.2 ends. The Developer may disable the App on termination.

## SCHEDULE 1 - SCOPE

Customer-facing iOS and Android apps and web ordering page: menu browsing, cart, pick-up scheduling, payment gateway integration, order history, push notifications. Administrator dashboard: menu and price management for twelve (12) outlets, order queue, daily sales report.

## SCHEDULE 2 - MILESTONES

Requirements and design: by 15 September 2026. Build of core ordering: by 15 December 2026. Integration and testing: by 28 February 2027. Pilot at three (3) outlets: by 31 March 2027. Go-Live: by 14 April 2027.
