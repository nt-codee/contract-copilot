# CONFIDENTIALITY, NON-COMPETITION AND INVENTION ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 23 February 2026 (the "**Effective Date**").

**BETWEEN**

(1) **KOPI KITA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1000), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur (the "**Company**"); and

(2) **MR. RIZAL CONTOH** (No. K/P: SAMPLE-000000), of No. 15, Jalan Contoh Perdana 3, Taman Contoh, 47100 Puchong, Selangor (the "**Employee**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Company has engaged the Employee as its Head Roaster under a letter of appointment of the same date (the "**Employment**").

(B) In the Employment the Employee will have access to the Company's roast profiles, green coffee sourcing, recipes and other confidential information, and will develop new products and processes.

(C) The Company requires the Employee to agree to the terms below as a condition of the Employment.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Confidential Information**" means all information relating to the Company, its affiliates, suppliers, customers and business, in any form, that the Employee learns or receives during the Employment, including roast profiles, recipes, blends, supplier names and prices, financial information, staff details and business plans.

"**Inventions**" means all inventions, discoveries, recipes, blends, roast profiles, processes, designs, works, improvements and ideas, whether or not patentable or protected by copyright, that the Employee conceives, develops or reduces to practice, alone or with others, during the Employment.

"**Restricted Business**" means the roasting, wholesale or retail sale of coffee, and the operation of any cafe, coffee shop or beverage outlet.

"**Restricted Period**" means the period of twenty-four (24) months after the Employment ends, for any reason.

## 2. CONFIDENTIALITY

2.1 The Employee shall keep all Confidential Information secret and shall not use or disclose it, during the Employment and for five (5) years after it ends, except in the proper performance of his duties.

2.2 All Confidential Information is the property of the Company. The Employee shall return all documents, samples, devices and copies when the Employment ends or when the Company asks.

2.3 The Employee shall not keep any copy of Confidential Information in any personal device, account or notebook.

## 3. INVENTIONS

3.1 The Employee assigns to the Company, with full title guarantee, all right, title and interest in all Inventions, wherever and whenever made, including Inventions made outside working hours, away from the Company's premises, without the use of the Company's resources, and not related to the business of the Company.

3.2 The Employee shall promptly disclose every Invention to the Company in writing and shall sign all documents and do all things the Company requires to register, perfect and enforce its rights. The Employee irrevocably appoints the Company as his attorney to do so in his name.

3.3 The Employee waives all moral rights in the Inventions.

## 4. NON-COMPETITION

4.1 During the Restricted Period the Employee shall not, anywhere in Malaysia, directly or indirectly, whether as employee, consultant, director, owner, partner or otherwise:

(a) be engaged, employed or interested in any Restricted Business;

(b) assist any person who carries on a Restricted Business; or

(c) hold any interest in any business that competes with the Company, other than a holding of less than one per cent (1%) in a listed company.

4.2 The Employee agrees that the restrictions in this Clause are reasonable and necessary to protect the Company and are not in restraint of trade. The Employee acknowledges that each of paragraphs (a) to (c) and each state of Malaysia is a separate restriction.

## 5. NON-SOLICITATION

5.1 During the Restricted Period the Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, deal with or accept business from any supplier, customer or business contact of the Company, or solicit, employ or engage any person who was an employee of the Company at any time in the twelve (12) months before the Employment ended.

## 6. NO CARVE-OUTS

6.1 Nothing in this Agreement shall be read as limiting any obligation of the Employee, and no information shall cease to be Confidential Information for any reason, including because it is public, was known to the Employee before the Employment, or was developed by the Employee.

## 7. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES AND REMEDIES

7.1 If the Employee breaches Clause 2, 3, 4 or 5, he shall immediately pay the Company, as liquidated damages and not as a penalty, a sum equal to twelve (12) months of his gross salary at the rate at the time of the breach, for each breach.

7.2 The Company may set off any sum owed under this Clause against any wages, bonus, commission or other amounts due to the Employee, including final salary.

7.3 The payment of liquidated damages does not limit the Company's right to an injunction, an account of profits or damages for any further loss.

7.4 The Employee shall pay the Company's legal costs of enforcing this Agreement on a solicitor and client basis.

## 8. TERM AND SURVIVAL

8.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the end of the Restricted Period. Clauses 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 survive termination of the Employment for any reason, including where the Company terminates the Employment without cause.

8.2 The Company may enforce this Agreement against the Employee whether or not the Employee is paid during the Restricted Period.

## 9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Malaysia, including the Contracts Act 1950.

9.2 The courts of Kuala Lumpur have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of this Agreement.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 This Agreement forms part of the terms of the Employment and is in addition to the letter of appointment and the Employment Act 1955 so far as it applies. If there is a conflict, this Agreement prevails.

10.2 The Company may assign this Agreement to any affiliate or successor. The Employee may not assign it.

10.3 If any provision or part of a provision is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

10.4 Notices to the Employee may be given by email or by post to his address above. Notices to the Company must be marked for the attention of the Managing Director, Ms. Lim Contoh.

10.5 The Employee confirms that he has read and understood this Agreement, has had the opportunity to take independent advice, and signs it voluntarily.
