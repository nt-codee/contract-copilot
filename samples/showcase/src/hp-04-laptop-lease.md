# LAPTOP LEASE AGREEMENT

This Laptop Lease Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 10 August 2026 (the "**Effective Date**").

**BETWEEN**

(1) **KENANGA CONTOH TECH LEASING PTE. LTD.** (UEN SAMPLE-SG-7401), a company incorporated in Singapore with its registered office at 42 Contoh Road, #05-01 Contoh Centre, Singapore 000742 (the "**Lessor**"); and

(2) **KOPI KITA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1000), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (the "**Lessee**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Lessor leases computer equipment to businesses.

(B) The Lessee requires ten (10) laptop computers for the finance, procurement and marketing team at its head office.

(C) The parties have agreed that the Lessor will lease the Equipment to the Lessee for a fixed term on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Equipment**" means the ten (10) business laptop computers, with chargers and carry cases, described in the Schedule.

"**Term**" means twenty-four (24) months beginning on the Effective Date and ending on 9 August 2028, unless ended earlier under this Agreement.

"**Monthly Rental**" means S$ 2,150.00 for all ten (10) laptops, exclusive of goods and services tax, being S$ 215.00 for each laptop.

"**Business Day**" means a day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a public holiday in Singapore.

"**Fair Market Value**" means the price at which the Equipment would be sold between a willing buyer and a willing seller, in its actual condition, as agreed by the parties or, if they do not agree within fourteen (14) days, as determined by an independent valuer chosen by both parties, whose fees the parties shall share equally.

"**Fair Wear and Tear**" means the ordinary deterioration of the Equipment that arises from proper use, including light scratches, keyboard wear and reduced battery capacity, but not breakage, liquid damage or missing parts.

1.2 Headings do not affect interpretation.

## 2. LEASE OF THE EQUIPMENT

2.1 The Lessor leases the Equipment to the Lessee, and the Lessee takes the Equipment on lease, for the Term.

2.2 The Equipment belongs to the Lessor at all times during the Term. The Lessee shall keep it free of any lien or charge and shall not sell, sub-lease or lend it, except that the Lessee may allow its employees and contractors to use it for the Lessee's business.

2.3 The Lessor shall deliver the Equipment to the Lessee's head office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur within ten (10) Business Days of the Effective Date, with the operating system and office software installed and licensed.

## 3. RENTAL AND PAYMENT

3.1 The Lessee shall pay the Monthly Rental monthly in arrears, within thirty (30) days of receiving the Lessor's invoice. The Lessor shall issue the invoice on or after the last day of each month.

3.2 The Monthly Rental is fixed for the Term and shall not be increased.

3.3 The Lessee shall pay goods and services tax on the Monthly Rental where it applies. Where the Lessee must withhold tax by law, the parties shall co-operate to claim any available relief, and the Lessee shall give the Lessor the withholding certificate.

3.4 If the Lessee disputes an invoice in good faith, it shall pay the part not in dispute on time and tell the Lessor in writing of the reasons for the dispute. The parties shall try to settle the dispute within fourteen (14) days.

3.5 Interest accrues on any sum overdue for more than fourteen (14) days at the rate of one per cent (1%) per month above the prevailing prime lending rate, from the due date to the date of payment.

## 4. WARRANTY, SUPPORT AND REPLACEMENT

4.1 The Lessor warrants that the Equipment is in good working order at delivery, is new and of satisfactory quality, and is fit for ordinary office use.

4.2 The Lessor shall provide, at its cost, a manufacturer's warranty for the Term, repair of any fault that is not caused by misuse, and technical support during business hours on Business Days.

4.3 If any laptop fails, the Lessee shall tell the Lessor by email or telephone. The Lessor shall deliver a replacement laptop of equal or better specification to the Lessee's head office within two (2) Business Days of the report. The Lessee shall hand the faulty laptop to the Lessor's courier at the same time.

4.4 If a replacement is not delivered within two (2) Business Days, the Monthly Rental for that laptop shall be reduced by one thirtieth (1/30) for each further day that passes until the replacement is delivered.

## 5. USE, CARE AND DATA

5.1 The Lessee shall use the Equipment with reasonable care, only for lawful business purposes, in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions, and shall not open the casing or alter the hardware, except to add memory or storage with the Lessor's consent.

5.2 The Lessee may use the Equipment in Malaysia and Singapore, and while travelling for business, but shall tell the Lessor if it intends to keep any laptop outside those two countries for more than thirty (30) days.

5.3 The Lessee is solely responsible for its data on the Equipment, and shall back it up regularly. Before the Lessee returns a laptop or hands it to the Lessor for repair, the Lessee may wipe it. If the Lessee does not wipe it, the Lessor shall securely erase all data on it using a recognised standard and give the Lessee a certificate of erasure, and shall not access or copy the data.

5.4 To the extent the Lessor handles personal data on behalf of the Lessee, it shall comply with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 of Singapore, shall process it only on the Lessee's instructions, and shall tell the Lessee within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of any breach affecting it.

## 6. RISK AND INSURANCE

6.1 The risk of loss, theft or damage to the Equipment passes to the Lessee on delivery and returns to the Lessor when the Lessor takes back the Equipment.

6.2 The Lessor shall insure the Equipment against fire, theft and accidental damage, and the cost of this insurance is included in the Monthly Rental. The Lessee shall pay the excess under any claim, up to a maximum of S$ 300.00 for each incident.

6.3 If a laptop is lost, stolen or damaged beyond repair, the Lessee shall tell the Lessor within five (5) Business Days, and shall make a police report where a theft occurs. The Lessor shall replace the laptop under Clause 4.3, and the Lessee shall pay only the excess under Clause 6.2.

## 7. LIABILITY

7.1 Each party's total liability to the other under or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the total Monthly Rental payable for the Term, except for the Lessee's obligation to pay the Monthly Rental and to return the Equipment.

7.2 Neither party is liable to the other for any indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of profit.

7.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for fraud, for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for any liability that cannot be limited under Singapore law.

## 8. TERMINATION

8.1 Either party may end this Agreement by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and, where the breach can be remedied, does not remedy it within thirty (30) days of written notice requiring it to do so.

8.2 Either party may end this Agreement by written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, makes an arrangement with its creditors, or has a liquidator, receiver or judicial manager appointed.

8.3 The Lessee may end this Agreement early, for any reason, on giving not less than three (3) months' written notice to the Lessor. On early termination, the Lessee shall return the Equipment under Clause 9 and shall pay an early termination fee equal to two (2) times the Monthly Rental, in addition to the Monthly Rental up to the termination date. No other sum is payable because of early termination.

8.4 If the Lessor ends this Agreement for the Lessee's breach, the Lessee shall pay the Monthly Rental up to the date the Equipment is returned, and the Lessor shall take reasonable steps to re-lease the Equipment. The Lessor shall credit any rental received from re-leasing against any further sum it claims from the Lessee for the rest of the Term.

## 9. END OF TERM, RETURN AND PURCHASE OPTION

9.1 Not earlier than ninety (90) days and not later than sixty (60) days before the end of the Term, the Lessor shall write to the Lessee to ask whether the Lessee wishes to buy, renew or return the Equipment.

9.2 The Lessee may, by written notice given not later than thirty (30) days before the end of the Term, buy all or any of the laptops at their Fair Market Value at the end of the Term. Ownership passes on payment of the price.

9.3 If the Lessee wishes to renew the lease, the parties shall negotiate in good faith for a new lease. This Agreement shall not renew automatically.

9.4 If the Lessee does not buy the Equipment, it shall return the Equipment to the Lessor at its Singapore office or, at the Lessee's option, shall allow the Lessor's courier to collect it from the Lessee's head office in Kuala Lumpur, at the Lessor's cost, within fourteen (14) days after the end of the Term. The Equipment shall be in good condition, Fair Wear and Tear excepted. The Lessor shall not charge for Fair Wear and Tear.

9.5 If the Lessee returns the Equipment late, the Lessee shall pay the Monthly Rental for each laptop on a pro rata daily basis until it is returned. The lease does not otherwise continue.

## 10. NOTICES

10.1 Notices shall be in writing, in English, and delivered by hand, sent by registered post or sent by email to the addresses in this Agreement, or to other addresses notified in writing. A notice delivered by hand is received on delivery, a notice sent by registered post is received three (3) Business Days after posting, and a notice sent by email is received on the next Business Day.

## 11. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.

11.2 The parties shall first try to settle any dispute by discussion between their senior managers within twenty-one (21) days of a written notice of the dispute.

11.3 If the dispute is not settled, it shall be finally resolved in the courts of Singapore, to whose non-exclusive jurisdiction the parties submit.

11.4 A person who is not a party to this Agreement has no right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 2001 to enforce any term of it.

## 12. GENERAL

12.1 This Agreement and its Schedule are the whole agreement between the parties about the Equipment.

12.2 A variation of this Agreement must be in writing signed by both parties.

12.3 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the written consent of the other, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

12.4 A delay in exercising a right is not a waiver.

12.5 If a provision is invalid, the rest of this Agreement remains in effect.

12.6 Each party shall keep the commercial terms of this Agreement confidential except where disclosure is required by law, or to its professional advisers or financiers.

12.7 This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
