# OUTLET CLEANING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Outlet Cleaning Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 1 January 2026 (the "**Effective Date**").

**BETWEEN**

(1) **BERSIH JAYA SERVIS SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-7102), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 22, Jalan Contoh Bersih 5, Taman Contoh, 47100 Puchong, Selangor (the "**Contractor**"); and

(2) **KOPI KITA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1000), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur (the "**Customer**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Customer operates twelve (12) cafe outlets in the Klang Valley, listed in Schedule 1 (the "**Outlets**").

(B) The Contractor provides commercial cleaning services and has agreed to clean the Outlets daily on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Monthly Fee**" means the fee of RM28,800 per month, being RM2,400 for each Outlet, exclusive of any tax that applies.

"**Services**" means the daily cleaning services described in Schedule 2.

"**Service Level**" means a standard in Schedule 3.

"**Term**" means the period from the Effective Date to 31 December 2026, unless ended earlier under this Agreement.

## 2. THE SERVICES

2.1 The Contractor shall perform the Services at each Outlet every day of the week, after closing time, to the standards in Schedule 2 and the Service Levels.

2.2 The Contractor shall supply all cleaning staff, equipment, supervision and consumables other than hand soap and paper products, which the Customer shall provide.

2.3 The Contractor shall use only cleaning products that are safe for food preparation areas and shall give the Customer the safety data sheet for each product on request.

2.4 The Contractor's staff shall wear identification, follow the Customer's reasonable site and food safety rules, and shall not use the Customer's equipment or take photographs inside the Outlets without permission.

## 3. PERSONNEL

3.1 The Contractor is responsible for recruiting, training, paying and managing its staff, and for all wages, EPF, SOCSO, EIS and other statutory contributions for them. No Contractor staff member becomes an employee of the Customer.

3.2 The Contractor warrants that every worker it assigns holds a valid right to work in Malaysia, and shall give the Customer a current list of workers assigned to each Outlet on request.

3.3 The Customer may require the Contractor to remove a worker from an Outlet for reasonable cause by notice, and the Contractor shall replace that worker within two (2) days.

## 4. SERVICE LEVELS AND CREDITS

4.1 The Customer's outlet managers shall inspect each Outlet each morning and record any failure to meet the Service Levels through the inspection form or the Contractor's reporting app.

4.2 If an Outlet fails the Service Levels on a day, the Contractor shall re-clean the affected area within three (3) hours of notice, at no charge.

4.3 For each day on which an Outlet fails a Service Level and the Contractor does not re-clean within that time, the Customer is entitled to a credit of five per cent (5%) of that Outlet's monthly fee, up to a maximum of twenty-five per cent (25%) of that Outlet's monthly fee in any month.

4.4 Credits are deducted from the next invoice. They are the Customer's remedy for the failures to which they relate and do not limit its right to terminate for repeated or serious failure under Clause 9.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

5.1 The Contractor shall invoice the Monthly Fee at the end of each calendar month, with a separate line for each Outlet and any credit due.

5.2 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt. If the Customer disputes part of an invoice, it shall pay the rest on time and notify the Contractor of the reason for the dispute within the same period, and the parties shall try in good faith to resolve it within fourteen (14) days.

5.3 The Monthly Fee is fixed for the Term. Additional cleaning requested by the Customer, such as deep cleaning before a re-opening, shall be charged at the rates in Schedule 4 only if the Customer has approved the request in writing beforehand.

5.4 Interest on undisputed overdue amounts is payable at the rate of one per cent (1%) per month.

## 6. INSURANCE

6.1 The Contractor shall maintain throughout the Term public liability insurance of at least RM2,000,000 for each claim, workmen's compensation cover for its staff, and cover for loss of or damage to the Customer's property in the Contractor's care. It shall give the Customer a copy of each certificate on request.

## 7. LIABILITY

7.1 Subject to Clause 7.3, the total liability of each party to the other under or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the total fees paid and payable under this Agreement for the twelve (12) months from the Effective Date.

7.2 Neither party is liable to the other for loss of profit or any indirect or consequential loss.

7.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, for the Contractor's liability to pay damages for loss of or damage to the Customer's property that its insurance covers, or for liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION

8.1 Each party shall keep confidential the other's non-public information, use it only for this Agreement, and disclose it only to those who need it for that purpose and are bound to confidentiality. This does not apply to information that is public other than through breach, was already known to the recipient, was independently developed, or must be disclosed by law.

8.2 The Contractor's staff may come across personal data of the Customer's staff and customers, including through CCTV monitors and cash handling areas. The Contractor shall not copy or record such data, and shall comply with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 in respect of any personal data it handles.

8.3 The Contractor shall keep the keys, access cards and alarm codes issued to it secure, shall report any loss immediately, and shall bear the reasonable cost of re-keying if the loss results from its failure to take reasonable care.

## 9. TERMINATION

9.1 This Agreement ends on 31 December 2026. It may be renewed only by a written agreement signed by both parties. It does not renew automatically, and the Contractor shall send the Customer a renewal proposal no later than 1 November 2026.

9.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving the other not less than thirty (30) days' written notice.

9.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within fourteen (14) days of written notice, or becomes insolvent or is wound up.

9.4 The Customer may terminate the Services at any single Outlet on fourteen (14) days' written notice if that Outlet closes or relocates, and the Monthly Fee shall be reduced by the fee for that Outlet.

9.5 On termination, the Contractor shall return all keys, access cards and Customer property, and the Customer shall pay for Services performed to the termination date, less any credits due.

## 10. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Malaysia, including the Contracts Act 1950.

10.2 The parties shall first try to resolve any dispute by discussion between their senior managers. If it is not resolved within thirty (30) days, either party may bring proceedings, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Kuala Lumpur.

## 11. NOTICES

11.1 Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by registered post or by email to the addresses on the first page, or to another address that a party notifies. A notice by hand is received on delivery, by registered post three (3) business days after posting, and by email on the next business day.

## 12. GENERAL

12.1 This Agreement and its Schedules are the entire agreement on their subject. A variation must be in writing and signed by both parties.

12.2 Neither party may assign this Agreement or subcontract the Services without the other's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

12.3 Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, but the Contractor shall use reasonable efforts to keep performing the Services at any Outlet that remains open.

12.4 Each party shall bear its own costs of preparing this Agreement. Any stamp duty payable under the Stamp Act 1949 shall be borne equally.

## SCHEDULES

Schedule 1: the twelve (12) Outlets and their addresses. Schedule 2: daily scope, including floors, counters, washrooms, kitchen surfaces, bins and glass. Schedule 3: Service Levels, including no visible dirt on floors, sanitised food contact surfaces and washrooms stocked by 8.00 a.m. Schedule 4: rates for additional cleaning.
