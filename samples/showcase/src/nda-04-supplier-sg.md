# NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (SUPPLIER PRICE LISTS)

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 8 December 2025 (the "**Effective Date**").

**BETWEEN**

(1) **KOPI KITA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1000), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur ("**Kopi Kita**"); and

(2) **EQUATOR BEANS TRADING PTE. LTD.** (UEN SAMPLE-SG-7104), a company incorporated in Singapore with its registered office at 9 Contoh Road, #03-02 Contoh Commodity House, Singapore 000904 ("**Equator Beans**").

Each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

**BACKGROUND**

(A) Equator Beans trades in green coffee and sources lots from producing origins in Africa, Latin America and Asia.

(B) Kopi Kita roasts and sells coffee through its own outlets and is considering buying green coffee from Equator Beans for its central roastery.

(C) In order to negotiate supply terms the Parties will share price lists, origin and lot information, volume forecasts, blend specifications and related commercial information (the "**Purpose**"), and wish to protect that information.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "**Discloser**") to the other (the "**Recipient**") in connection with the Purpose, in any form, including price lists, discount and rebate structures, origin and lot details, supplier and customer identities, volume forecasts, roast profiles, blend specifications, cupping notes, financial information and the terms of any proposed supply arrangement.

"**Representatives**" means a Party's directors, employees and professional advisers who need to know Confidential Information for the Purpose.

"**Residual Information**" means information retained in the unaided memory of a Recipient's personnel after access to Confidential Information.

## 2. CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

2.1 The Recipient shall:

(a) keep the Discloser's Confidential Information confidential;

(b) use it only for the Purpose;

(c) disclose it only to those of its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by duties of confidence no less strict than those in this Agreement; and

(d) protect it using at least the degree of care that it applies to its own confidential information, and not less than reasonable care.

2.2 Each Party is liable for breaches of this Agreement by its Representatives.

2.3 The Recipient shall not use any Residual Information for any purpose, and nothing in this Agreement gives the Recipient the right to use Residual Information, whether or not the Recipient's personnel intentionally memorised it.

2.4 The Recipient shall promptly tell the Discloser in writing if it learns of any unauthorised access to, or use or disclosure of, the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 3. EXCEPTIONS

3.1 The obligations in Clause 2 do not apply to information that the Recipient can show by written records:

(a) is or becomes public other than through a breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully in the Recipient's possession before the Discloser disclosed it; or

(c) was developed independently by the Recipient without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

3.2 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information where required by law, court order or a regulator, if it gives the Discloser prompt written notice (where lawful) and discloses only what is required.

## 4. OWNERSHIP AND NO OBLIGATION

4.1 Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. No licence is granted except the right to use it for the Purpose.

4.2 Neither Party is obliged to disclose any particular information or to enter into any supply agreement. Any supply of green coffee will be governed only by a separate written contract signed by both Parties.

## 5. TERM

5.1 This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues for five (5) years, until 7 December 2030.

5.2 Either Party may stop disclosing information at any time by written notice, but the obligations in Clause 2 continue for the full five-year term in respect of all Confidential Information disclosed before the end of that term.

## 6. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

6.1 Within thirty (30) days of a written request, the Recipient shall return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information and certify in writing that it has done so.

6.2 The Recipient may retain one archival copy to the extent required by law or in its routine electronic back-ups, which remain subject to this Agreement.

## 7. DATA PROTECTION

7.1 The Parties do not expect to share personal data. If any personal data is shared, each Party shall handle it only for the Purpose and in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 of Singapore and, where it applies, the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia.

## 8. REMEDIES

8.1 The Recipient acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement will cause the Discloser irreparable harm. The Discloser is entitled to an injunction, specific performance or other equitable relief to restrain any actual or threatened breach, without having to prove actual loss or damage and without posting security.

8.2 This Clause is in addition to any other remedy available to the Discloser.

## 9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.

9.2 The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute by discussion between their senior managers for thirty (30) days after written notice of the dispute.

9.3 Failing resolution, the courts of Singapore have non-exclusive jurisdiction over the dispute.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and may be varied only in writing signed by both Parties.

10.2 Neither Party may assign its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the other's prior written consent.

10.3 A person who is not a party to this Agreement has no right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 2001 of Singapore to enforce any of its terms.

10.4 If any provision of this Agreement is invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in force.

10.5 A delay in exercising a right is not a waiver of it.

10.6 Notices must be in writing and sent by hand, courier or email to the Party's address above. Notices to Kopi Kita are marked for the attention of the Chief Financial Officer, Mr. Ravi Contoh.

10.7 This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
