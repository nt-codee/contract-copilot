# TENANCY AGREEMENT (SHOP LOT)

This Tenancy Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 1 December 2025.

**BETWEEN**

(1) **BUKIT AMAN CONTOH HARTANAH SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-7101), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 12, Jalan Contoh Damai, Taman Contoh, 50480 Kuala Lumpur (the "**Landlord**"); and

(2) **KOPI KITA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1000), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur (the "**Tenant**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Landlord is the registered proprietor of a four-storey shop-lot building at Lot 8, Jalan Contoh Telawi, Bangsar, 59100 Kuala Lumpur (the "**Building**").

(B) The Tenant operates a chain of cafes and wishes to rent the ground floor and mezzanine of the Building, with a floor area of about 1,850 square feet (the "**Premises**"), to operate one of its cafe outlets. The Landlord has agreed to let the Premises to the Tenant on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement: "**Term**" means the period in clause 2.1 and, if the Option is exercised, the Renewal Term; "**Rent**" means the monthly rent in clause 3.1; "**Deposits**" means the Security Deposit and the Utility Deposit in clause 4; "**Option**" means the Tenant's right to renew in clause 10; and "**Renewal Term**" means the further term of two (2) years described in clause 10.

## 2. TERM

2.1 The Landlord lets the Premises to the Tenant for a term of two (2) years, starting on 1 December 2025 and ending on 30 November 2027.

2.2 The Landlord shall hand over vacant possession of the Premises on 1 December 2025. The Tenant has a rent-free fit-out period of forty-five (45) days from the handover, during which the Tenant shall pay only the utilities it uses.

## 3. RENT AND PAYMENT

3.1 The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of RM9,500 (the "**Rent**"), in advance, on or before the seventh (7th) day of each month, by bank transfer to the account the Landlord notifies in writing. The Rent is fixed for the first two (2) years of the Term.

3.2 The Rent is exclusive of any sales and service tax or similar tax, which the Tenant shall pay in addition if the law requires it.

3.3 The Tenant shall also pay the electricity, water, sewerage, telephone and internet charges for the Premises, and the Building's common-area service charge of RM420 a month.

3.4 If any sum is not paid within fourteen (14) days after the due date, the Tenant shall pay interest on the late amount at one per cent (1%) a month from the due date until payment.

## 4. DEPOSITS

4.1 On signing this Agreement, the Tenant shall pay the Landlord a security deposit of RM28,500, equal to three (3) months' Rent (the "**Security Deposit**"), and a utility deposit of RM9,500, equal to one (1) month's Rent (the "**Utility Deposit**").

4.2 The Deposits are held as security for the Tenant's obligations under this Agreement. The Landlord may deduct from the Deposits any Rent, utility charges or cost of repairing damage that the Tenant has not paid.

4.3 The Landlord shall return the balance of the Deposits, without interest, within thirty (30) days after the Tenant has handed back vacant possession and the final utility bills have been settled.

## 5. USE OF THE PREMISES

5.1 The Tenant shall use the Premises only to run a cafe selling food, beverages and related items, under the "Kopi Kita" name or another name approved by the Landlord, and for no other purpose without the Landlord's written consent.

5.2 The Tenant shall obtain and keep in force at its own cost all licences and permits needed for its business, including the business licence from Dewan Bandaraya Kuala Lumpur and the food premises registration.

5.3 The Tenant may put up a shop sign in the place and of the size approved by the Landlord in writing, which approval shall not be unreasonably withheld. The Tenant shall remove the sign at the end of the Term and make good any damage.

5.4 The Tenant shall not cause a nuisance to other occupiers of the Building, shall keep the five-foot way in front of the Premises clear, and shall dispose of its rubbish and kitchen waste in the way required by the local authority.

## 6. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE

6.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises, and all fittings, fixtures, equipment, pipes, wiring, air-conditioning and signage in them, in good and tenantable condition, and shall bear the cost of all repairs, maintenance and replacements of every kind to the Premises, whether or not the need for repair was caused by the Tenant, fair wear and tear included.

6.2 The Landlord may enter the Premises at any time on twenty-four (24) hours' notice to inspect their condition, to carry out works to the Building, or to show the Premises to a prospective buyer, tenant or valuer. The Landlord shall try to avoid disturbing the Tenant's trading hours.

6.3 The Tenant shall carry out its fit-out at its own cost, after the Landlord has approved the plans in writing, and shall comply with the requirements of the local authority and the fire department.

## 7. INSURANCE

7.1 The Landlord shall insure the Building against fire and other usual risks. The Tenant shall insure its own stock, equipment and fit-out, and shall carry public liability insurance of at least RM2,000,000 for each claim, and shall give the Landlord a copy of the certificate on request.

## 8. DEFAULT AND TERMINATION

8.1 If the Rent is unpaid for twenty-one (21) days after the due date and the Tenant has not paid within seven (7) days of a written reminder, the Landlord may end this Agreement by written notice and take back the Premises, without affecting its right to claim the sums due.

8.2 If the Tenant breaks any other term of this Agreement and does not fix the breach within thirty (30) days of the Landlord's written notice, the Landlord may end this Agreement by written notice.

8.3 If the Landlord breaks this Agreement, including by failing to give quiet enjoyment of the Premises, and does not fix the breach within thirty (30) days of the Tenant's written notice, the Tenant may end this Agreement by written notice and recover its proven losses.

8.4 The Tenant may end this Agreement during the Term only under clause 8.3 or clause 11.1.

## 9. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

9.1 The Tenant shall not assign or sublet the Premises, or part of them, without the Landlord's written consent, which the Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold or delay. A change of the Tenant's shareholders is not an assignment.

9.2 If the Landlord sells the Building, this Agreement continues against the buyer, and the Landlord shall pay the Deposits to the buyer.

## 10. OPTION TO RENEW

10.1 The Tenant may renew this Agreement for one further term of two (2) years, from 1 December 2027 to 30 November 2029 (the "**Renewal Term**"), by giving the Landlord written notice at least six (6) months before the end of the Term, that is, on or before 31 May 2027, if the Tenant is not then in breach.

10.2 The rent for the Renewal Term shall be the amount the Landlord decides, which shall not be more than fifteen per cent (15%) above the Rent payable at the end of the Term. The Landlord shall tell the Tenant the new rent in writing within thirty (30) days after receiving the Tenant's notice. Other terms stay the same, except that there is no further option to renew.

## 11. DAMAGE TO THE PREMISES

11.1 If the Premises are damaged so that they cannot be used, the Rent stops until the Premises are fit for use again. If they are not restored within four (4) months, either party may end this Agreement by written notice.

## 12. END OF THE TERM

12.1 At the end of the Term, the Tenant shall hand back the Premises clean and in good condition, remove its signs, stock and movable equipment, and make good any damage caused by the removal. The Tenant need not remove fixed fittings approved by the Landlord in writing.

## 13. STAMP DUTY AND COSTS

13.1 The Tenant shall bear the stamp duty on this Agreement and shall have it stamped within thirty (30) days of signing, in accordance with the Stamp Act 1949. Each party bears its own legal fees.

## 14. NOTICES

14.1 Notices shall be in writing and sent by hand, by registered post or by email to the addresses above. A notice is treated as received on delivery by hand, two (2) days after posting, or on the next business day after email.

## 15. GENERAL

15.1 This Agreement is the whole agreement between the parties about the Premises. Any change must be in writing and signed by both parties.

15.2 A failure or delay in using a right is not a waiver of it. If any term is found unenforceable, the rest of this Agreement continues.

15.3 This Agreement is governed by the law of Malaysia, including the Contracts Act 1950 and, as to the Landlord's title, the National Land Code. The courts of Kuala Lumpur shall have jurisdiction over any dispute.
