# LOYALTY AND ORDERING APP SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Loyalty and Ordering App Subscription Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 1 February 2026 (the "**Effective Date**").

**BETWEEN**

(1) **PELITA LOYALTY TECHNOLOGIES PTE. LTD.** (UEN SAMPLE-SG-7101), a company incorporated in Singapore with its registered office at 18 Contoh Road, #04-02, Singapore 000118 (the "**Provider**"); and

(2) **KOPI KITA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1000), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (the "**Customer**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Provider operates a hosted customer loyalty and mobile ordering platform for food and beverage businesses.

(B) The Customer operates twelve (12) cafe outlets and a central roastery in Malaysia and wishes to offer its customers a loyalty programme and a mobile ordering app.

(C) The Provider has agreed to supply, and the Customer has agreed to subscribe to, the platform on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**App**" means the white-labelled iOS and Android mobile application and the web ordering page made available by the Provider to the Customer's end customers.

"**Customer Data**" means all data entered into or generated through the Platform by or for the Customer, including member profiles, order histories, points balances and transaction records.

"**Fees**" means the Subscription Fee and any other amount payable under this Agreement.

"**Initial Term**" means the period from the Effective Date to 31 July 2027.

"**Platform**" means the Provider's hosted loyalty, campaign management, ordering and reporting software, the App and the back-office dashboard.

"**Services**" means the provision of the Platform, hosting, support and the onboarding services described in Schedule 1.

## 2. THE SERVICES

2.1 The Provider shall make the Platform available to the Customer for all twelve (12) outlets from the Effective Date, and shall complete onboarding of each outlet's point-of-sale connection within sixty (60) days of the Effective Date.

2.2 The Provider shall provide the Services with reasonable skill and care and shall use reasonable efforts to keep the Platform available 99.5 per cent of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance notified at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance.

2.3 The Provider shall provide email and telephone support on business days between 9.00 a.m. and 6.00 p.m. Singapore time, and shall respond to a fault that stops customers from ordering within four (4) hours.

2.4 The Customer shall provide the information and access reasonably required for onboarding, and shall be responsible for the accuracy of the menus, prices and promotions it loads onto the Platform.

## 3. LICENCE AND RESTRICTIONS

3.1 The Provider grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the Platform and the App for its own business during the Term.

3.2 The Customer shall not copy, reverse engineer or resell the Platform, and shall keep its administrator login details secure.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 The Customer shall pay the Provider a monthly subscription fee of S$4,800 (the "**Subscription Fee**"), exclusive of goods and services tax, for the period from the Effective Date to the first anniversary of the Effective Date.

4.2 The Provider shall invoice the Subscription Fee monthly in advance. The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

4.3 The onboarding fee of S$6,000 is payable on the Effective Date.

4.4 On each anniversary of the Effective Date, the Subscription Fee shall increase by eight per cent (8%) over the Subscription Fee applying immediately before that anniversary.

4.5 Overdue amounts that are not disputed in good faith bear interest at one per cent (1%) per month from the due date until payment.

## 5. TERM AND RENEWAL

5.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for the Initial Term of eighteen (18) months, ending on 31 July 2027.

5.2 At the end of the Initial Term, and of each Renewal Term, this Agreement renews automatically for a further period of twelve (12) months (each a "**Renewal Term**") unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term. For the Initial Term, notice must be given on or before 1 June 2027.

5.3 The Initial Term and all Renewal Terms together are the "**Term**".

## 6. DATA AND SECURITY

6.1 As between the parties, the Customer owns the Customer Data. The Provider may use the Customer Data only to provide the Services, and may use anonymised and aggregated usage statistics to improve the Platform.

6.2 The Provider shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards that are reasonable for a service of this kind, including encryption of data in transit and daily backups retained for thirty (30) days.

6.3 If the Provider becomes aware of unauthorised access to Customer Data, it shall notify the Customer in writing within seven (7) days of becoming aware and shall give the Customer the information it reasonably has about the incident.

6.4 On expiry or termination, the Provider shall make the Customer Data available for export in a standard format for thirty (30) days, and shall then delete it from the Platform.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

7.1 The Provider and its licensors own the Platform and all intellectual property rights in it. The Customer owns its trade marks, logos, menus and content and grants the Provider a licence to use them solely to provide the Services.

## 8. WARRANTIES AND LIABILITY

8.1 Each party warrants that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement.

8.2 Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, the Provider gives no warranty that the Platform will be uninterrupted or error free, and all warranties and conditions implied by law are excluded to the extent the law permits.

8.3 Subject to Clause 8.5, the total liability of the Provider to the Customer arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall not exceed an amount equal to the Fees paid or payable for the three (3) months immediately before the event giving rise to the claim.

8.4 Neither party is liable to the other for loss of profit, loss of revenue, loss of goodwill or any indirect or consequential loss.

8.5 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for any liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 9. TERMINATION

9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and, where the breach can be remedied, fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of written notice requiring it to do so.

9.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, enters into liquidation or judicial management, or ceases to carry on business.

9.3 If the Customer terminates early other than under Clause 9.1 or 9.2, the Fees for the remainder of the then-current term remain payable.

9.4 On termination, the Customer shall stop using the Platform and each party shall return or delete the other's confidential information.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other party disclosed in connection with this Agreement, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to its employees and advisers who need to know it and are bound to keep it confidential.

10.2 This Clause does not apply to information that is or becomes public other than through breach, was already known to the recipient, is independently developed, or must be disclosed by law or a court.

11.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Singapore to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

11.3 A person who is not a party to this Agreement has no right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 2001 of Singapore to enforce any of its terms.

## 12. NOTICES

12.1 Notices must be in writing and sent by hand, courier or email to the addresses below, or to any other address a party notifies. Notices to the Customer shall be marked for the attention of the Chief Financial Officer, and notices to the Provider for the attention of the Managing Director.

12.2 A notice sent by email is treated as received on the next business day after it is sent, and a notice sent by courier two (2) business days after dispatch.

## 13. GENERAL

13.1 This Agreement, with Schedule 1, is the entire agreement of the parties on its subject and replaces earlier discussions and proposals.

13.2 A change to this Agreement is effective only if made in writing and signed by both parties.

13.3 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

13.4 Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, other than a failure to pay.
