# FIXED-TERM EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT: ROASTERY TEAM LEAD

This Fixed-Term Employment Contract (the "**Contract**") is made on 2 March 2026 (the "**Effective Date**").

**BETWEEN**

(1) **KOPI KITA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1000), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 1, Jalan Contoh Kopi, 50450 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (the "**Employer**"); and

(2) **MR. KAMAL CONTOH** (No. K/P: SAMPLE-000000), of No. 33, Jalan Contoh 9/1, Taman Contoh, 41200 Klang, Selangor, Malaysia (the "**Employee**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Employer operates a central roastery in the Klang Valley that supplies twelve cafe outlets and wholesale customers.

(B) The Employer wishes to employ the Employee as Roastery Team Lead for a fixed term, and the Employee wishes to accept, on the terms of this Contract.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Contract, unless the context requires otherwise:

"**Act**" means the Employment Act 1955 of Malaysia, as amended from time to time, and any regulations made under it.

"**Fixed Term**" means the period of eighteen (18) months from 2 March 2026 to 31 August 2027.

"**Confidential Information**" means non-public information about the Employer's roast profiles, blends, green bean sources, pricing, customers and plans.

"**Klang Valley**" means Kuala Lumpur, Putrajaya and the districts of Petaling, Klang, Gombak, Hulu Langat, Sepang and Kuala Langat in Selangor.

1.2 Where this Contract gives the Employee less than the Act requires, the Act prevails.

## 2. APPOINTMENT AND DUTIES

2.1 The Employer employs the Employee as Roastery Team Lead for the Fixed Term, reporting to the Head Roaster.

2.2 The Employee shall lead a team of six (6) roasters and packers, schedule daily roasts, keep quality records, oversee cleaning and equipment checks, and make sure the roastery meets food safety and occupational safety rules.

2.3 The Employee's place of work is the Employer's roastery in Shah Alam, Selangor. The Employer may reasonably require him to visit outlets and suppliers within Malaysia.

## 3. PROBATION

3.1 The first three (3) months of the Fixed Term are a probation period. The Employer shall review the Employee's performance and tell him in writing whether he is confirmed. During probation either party may end this Contract by giving fourteen (14) days' written notice.

## 4. HOURS OF WORK

4.1 The Employee works up to forty-five (45) hours a week over five and a half (5.5) days, with one rest day each week, on a roster published at least fourteen (14) days ahead.

4.2 Overtime, rest day and public holiday work are paid at the rates the Act requires where the Act applies to the Employee.

## 5. SALARY, ALLOWANCES AND BONUS

5.1 The Employer shall pay the Employee a basic monthly salary of RM 6,800.00, paid by bank transfer on or before the last working day of each month.

5.2 The Employer shall pay an allowance of RM 300.00 a month for shift and on-call duties, which forms part of the Employee's wages for statutory contributions.

5.3 The Employer shall make contributions to the Employees Provident Fund, the Social Security Organisation (SOCSO) and the Employment Insurance System (EIS) as required by law.

5.4 The Employee may be considered for an annual performance bonus. Whether any bonus is paid, and its amount, is entirely in the Employer's discretion. The payment of a bonus in one year creates no right to a bonus in any other year, and an Employee who has given or been given notice is not eligible.

## 6. LEAVE

6.1 The Employee is entitled to sixteen (16) days of paid annual leave for each completed year of service, pro-rated for part years, together with paid sick leave, hospitalisation leave and public holidays as set out in the Act.

6.2 Annual leave must be applied for at least fourteen (14) days ahead and is subject to the Employer's operational needs, but will not be unreasonably refused.

## 7. TERM, RENEWAL AND TERMINATION

7.1 This Contract starts on 2 March 2026 and, unless ended earlier under this Clause, ends on 31 August 2027.

7.2 Unless either party gives written notice to the other, not later than sixty (60) days before the end of the Fixed Term (that is, on or before 2 July 2027), that this Contract is not to continue, this Contract shall automatically convert into a new fixed term of eighteen (18) months on the same terms, except that the salary will be as then in force.

7.3 After the probation period, the Employee may end this Contract before the end of the Fixed Term by giving the Employer three (3) months' written notice. The Employer may end this Contract before the end of the Fixed Term by giving the Employee one (1) month's written notice. Either party may pay salary in lieu of any notice not given.

7.4 The Employer may dismiss the Employee without notice for misconduct after a domestic inquiry held in line with the Act and the principles of natural justice.

7.5 On termination the Employee shall return all keys, access cards, tools, recipes and documents belonging to the Employer, and the Employer shall pay all sums due within the time the Act requires.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY AND PERSONAL DATA

8.1 The Employee shall keep Confidential Information secret during employment and for three (3) years afterwards. This does not apply to information that is public other than through his breach, that he already knew, or that he must disclose by law.

8.2 The Employer shall process the Employee's personal data under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010, for payroll, statutory filings and the administration of employment.

## 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

9.1 Roast profiles, blends, procedures and training materials that the Employee develops in the course of his duties belong to the Employer. The Employee keeps all rights in anything he creates in his own time without using the Employer's resources or Confidential Information.

## 10. RESTRICTION AFTER EMPLOYMENT

10.1 For twelve (12) months after his employment ends, the Employee shall not, within the Klang Valley, be employed by, or provide services to, or own more than five percent (5%) of, any business that roasts or sells roasted coffee in competition with the Employer.

10.2 For the same period, the Employee shall not directly solicit any employee of the Employer to leave the Employer.

10.3 The Employee agrees that these restrictions are reasonable. If a court finds any part too wide, it shall apply to the extent the court thinks reasonable.

## 11. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

11.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of Malaysia, including the Contracts Act 1950.

11.2 The parties shall first try to settle any dispute by discussion. Nothing in this Contract stops the Employee from making a complaint to the Labour Department or the Industrial Court. Otherwise, the courts of Kuala Lumpur have jurisdiction.

## 12. GENERAL

12.1 This Contract and the staff handbook are the entire agreement on their subject. Any change must be in writing and signed by both parties.

12.2 Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses above or by email to the address each party has given in writing.

12.3 If any part of this Contract is unenforceable, the rest remains in force. This Contract is signed in two copies, one for each party.
