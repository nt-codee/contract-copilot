# CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT (INVESTOR DISCUSSIONS)

This Confidentiality Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 11 September 2026 (the "**Effective Date**").

**BETWEEN**

(1) **MERLION BRIDGE CAPITAL PTE. LTD.** (UEN SAMPLE-SG-1301), a company incorporated in Singapore with its registered office at 20 Sample Quay, #18-01, Singapore 001301 (the "**Investor**"); and

(2) **DAPUR NUSANTARA FOODS SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1302), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 15, Jalan Contoh 10/6, Bangsar South, 59200 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (the "**Company**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Company produces and sells ready-to-eat frozen meals and operates eleven (11) cloud kitchens in Malaysia.

(B) The Investor is considering a possible equity investment in, or a commercial partnership with, the Company (the "**Purpose**").

(C) For the Purpose the Company will give the Investor certain information about its business, and the parties agree as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Confidential Information**" means all information disclosed by or for the Company to the Investor in connection with the Purpose, in any form, including financial statements, supplier and customer terms, recipes and formulations, production methods, expansion plans and valuation material.

"**Representatives**" means the Investor's directors, employees, advisers, co-investors and the companies in its group and the portfolio companies in which the Investor or its funds hold an interest.

"**Group**" means, for a party, that party, its holding companies and subsidiaries, and the other subsidiaries of its holding companies from time to time.

"**Transaction**" means any equity investment, convertible loan, joint venture or commercial partnership between the Investor (or any member of its Group) and the Company that the parties may agree after the Effective Date, which is not binding until a separate written agreement is signed.

"**Residual Information**" means any ideas, concepts, know-how, techniques or data in Confidential Information that are retained in the unaided memory of the Investor's Representatives after reviewing it.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only. References to days are to calendar days, and references to a clause are to a clause of this Agreement. The words "including" and "includes" do not limit the words that come before them.

1.3 The parties have not agreed any terms of a Transaction, and neither is obliged to negotiate or to complete one. Each party bears its own costs in connection with the Purpose.

## 2. USE AND PROTECTION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

2.1 The Company shall mark its Confidential Information as confidential when it discloses it in writing, and shall confirm in writing within seven (7) days any oral disclosure that it wishes to be treated as confidential.

2.2 The Investor may use Confidential Information to evaluate the Purpose and may share it with its Representatives. The Investor is not obliged to keep Confidential Information confidential, to restrict its use to the Purpose, or to apply any particular standard of care to it.

2.3 The Investor shall not be responsible for any use or disclosure of Confidential Information by its Representatives.

2.4 Nothing in this Agreement obliges the Investor to make or continue discussions, to make any investment, or to return or destroy any Confidential Information.

## 3. RESIDUALS

3.1 The Investor and its Representatives are free to use Residual Information for any purpose, including in the evaluation of, investment in or support of businesses that compete with the Company, without any obligation to the Company and without any payment to it.

3.2 The Company acknowledges that the Investor and its Representatives may already be evaluating or may in future evaluate other food and beverage businesses, and that nothing in this Agreement restricts them from doing so.

## 4. EXCLUSIONS

4.1 Information is not Confidential Information to the extent that it is public through no fault of the Company, was known to the Investor before disclosure, was received lawfully from a third party, or was developed independently by the Investor.

## 5. NO LIABILITY

5.1 The Company accepts that the Investor will have no liability to it for any loss arising from the use or disclosure of Confidential Information, and the Company waives any claim in respect of such use or disclosure.

5.2 The Company shall remain liable to the Investor for any inaccuracy in the information it provides and shall indemnify the Investor against any loss arising from reliance on that information.

## 6. TERM

6.1 This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and the Company's disclosures under it are covered for one (1) year from the Effective Date. After that date the Investor has no obligation of any kind in respect of any Confidential Information, whether or not it has been disclosed to third parties.

## 7. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.

7.2 Any dispute shall be referred first to the senior management of both parties. If it is not resolved within thirty (30) days, it shall be decided by the courts of Singapore.

## 8. GENERAL

8.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent.

8.2 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter. Any variation must be in writing and signed by both parties.

8.3 This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
