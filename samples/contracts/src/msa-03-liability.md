# APPLICATION DEVELOPMENT AND SUPPORT AGREEMENT

This Application Development and Support Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 28 July 2026 (the "**Commencement Date**").

**PARTIES**

(1) **STRATAKIN DIGITAL PTE. LTD.** (UEN SAMPLE-SG-0701), a company incorporated in Singapore with its registered office at 2 Sample Science Park Drive, #03-08, Singapore 000701 (the "**Vendor**"); and

(2) **RIMBUN PAGI CAFE GROUP SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-0702), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 3-1, Jalan Contoh 22/8, Seksyen 22, 40300 Shah Alam, Selangor, Malaysia (the "**Customer**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Vendor designs, builds and maintains mobile and web applications for businesses in Singapore and Malaysia.

(B) The Customer operates a chain of eighteen (18) cafes in the Klang Valley and wishes to launch a branded mobile ordering and loyalty application for its customers.

(C) The Customer has engaged the Vendor to design, develop, launch and support that application on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Application**" means the iOS and Android mobile ordering and loyalty application, the web ordering site and the administration dashboard to be developed by the Vendor for the Customer as described in the Statement of Work.

"**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means the statement of work in Schedule 1, describing the features, milestones, acceptance criteria and fees for the Application.

"**Deliverables**" means the Application and all designs, source code, object code, graphics, documentation and other materials produced by the Vendor under this Agreement.

"**Fees**" means the development fees and the monthly support fees set out in the SOW.

"**Customer Materials**" means the Customer's trade marks, menus, photographs, product information, customer lists and other materials supplied by the Customer.

"**End User Data**" means personal data of the Customer's customers collected through the Application.

1.2 Unless stated otherwise, references to days are to calendar days.

## 2. DEVELOPMENT SERVICES

2.1 The Vendor shall design and develop the Application in the phases and by the milestone dates set out in the SOW.

2.2 At the end of each phase the Vendor shall deliver the relevant Deliverables for acceptance testing. The Customer shall test them within ten (10) days and either accept them or notify the Vendor of any failure to meet the acceptance criteria. The Vendor shall correct notified failures and redeliver within ten (10) days.

2.3 The Customer shall provide the Customer Materials and timely decisions reasonably required by the Vendor. Delays caused by the Customer shall extend the milestone dates accordingly.

2.4 Change requests shall be agreed in writing and may adjust the Fees and milestone dates.

## 3. SUPPORT SERVICES

3.1 From the launch of the Application on the app stores, the Vendor shall provide hosting, bug fixing, security updates and compatibility updates for new operating system versions, as described in Schedule 2, in return for the monthly support fees.

3.2 The support services continue for twelve (12) months from launch and then renew monthly until terminated by either party on thirty (30) days' written notice.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 The development fees are payable in instalments on the milestones in the SOW. Monthly support fees are invoiced monthly in advance.

4.2 Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of receipt. Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes.

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 The Customer retains ownership of the Customer Materials and grants the Vendor a licence to use them solely to perform this Agreement.

5.2 All Intellectual Property Rights in the Deliverables, including any software developed specifically for the Customer, shall vest in and remain with the Vendor. Subject to payment of all Fees, the Vendor grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the Application during the term of this Agreement.

5.3 The Vendor shall not be obliged to deliver the source code of the Application to the Customer. On termination of this Agreement for any reason, the licence in Clause 5.2 shall end and the Vendor may remove the Application from the app stores.

## 6. WARRANTIES

6.1 The Vendor warrants that the Application will materially conform to the SOW for sixty (60) days after acceptance of the final phase, and will correct any material non-conformity reported in that period.

6.2 The Customer warrants that the Customer Materials do not infringe the rights of any third party.

## 7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

7.1 The Vendor shall not be liable for any loss of profits, revenue, business, goodwill or data, or for any indirect, special or consequential loss, however arising.

7.2 The Vendor's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall not exceed the Fees paid by the Customer in the one (1) month immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

7.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits the Customer's liability to the Vendor, and the limitations in Clauses 7.1 and 7.2 apply to the Vendor only.

## 8. INDEMNITY

8.1 The Customer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Vendor, its affiliates and their officers and employees from and against any and all losses, liabilities, damages, costs and expenses (including legal fees) arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, the Application or the Customer's business, whether or not caused by the negligence of the Vendor.

8.2 The Vendor gives no indemnity to the Customer in respect of any matter, including any claim that the Application infringes the intellectual property rights of a third party.

## 9. DATA PROTECTION

9.1 The Customer is responsible for obtaining the consent of its customers to the collection of End User Data. The Vendor shall process End User Data only to host and operate the Application, protect it with reasonable security measures, and notify the Customer promptly of any security breach affecting it.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 Each party shall keep the other party's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement, subject to the usual exceptions for information that is public, already known, lawfully received from a third party or required by law to be disclosed. This obligation survives for three (3) years after termination.

## 11. TERM AND TERMINATION

11.1 This Agreement starts on the Commencement Date and continues until the support services end under Clause 3.2.

11.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice, or becomes insolvent.

11.3 On termination the Customer shall pay all Fees for work performed up to the date of termination.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.

12.2 Any dispute shall be referred first to senior management of both parties. If not resolved within thirty (30) days, it shall be finally resolved by the courts of Singapore.

## 13. GENERAL

13.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent.

13.2 This Agreement and its Schedules constitute the entire agreement between the parties and supersede any prior proposals or quotations. Any variation must be in writing and signed by both parties.

13.3 The Vendor may engage subcontractors to perform any part of the services, and remains responsible for their performance.
