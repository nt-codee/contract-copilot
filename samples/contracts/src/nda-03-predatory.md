# CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

THIS CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of 14 September 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

(1) **HALVEXA GLOBAL HOLDINGS INC.**, a corporation organised under the laws of the State of Delaware, United States of America, having its principal office at 400 Example Avenue, Suite 1900, New York, NY 10000, United States of America ("**Halvexa**" or the "**Company**"); and

(2) **LUMIO TANI AGRITECH SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-0302), a company incorporated in Malaysia having its registered office at Lot 12, Jalan Contoh Perindustrian 2, 43650 Bandar Baru Bangi, Selangor, Malaysia (the "**Counterparty**").

**WHEREAS**

(A) Halvexa, directly and through its subsidiaries, manufactures and distributes precision irrigation controllers, soil sensors and farm analytics software in North America, Europe and Asia.

(B) The Counterparty designs and sells smart irrigation kits and a farm monitoring mobile application to smallholders and plantation operators in Malaysia and Indonesia.

(C) Halvexa is considering appointing the Counterparty as a non-exclusive distributor of certain Halvexa products in Malaysia, and the parties wish to hold discussions for that purpose (the "**Transaction**").

(D) Halvexa is willing to make Confidential Information available to the Counterparty only on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Confidential Information**" means any information disclosed by or on behalf of Halvexa to the Counterparty in connection with the Transaction that is marked or identified as confidential, or that would reasonably be considered confidential, including product designs, firmware, pricing, distributor terms, customer lists and market plans.

1.2 "**Group**" means Halvexa and each of its subsidiaries and affiliates from time to time.

1.3 "**Representatives**" means the officers, employees and professional advisers of the Counterparty who need to know Confidential Information for the Transaction.

1.4 "**Territory**" means the countries of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations as constituted from time to time.

## 2. CONFIDENTIALITY UNDERTAKINGS

2.1 The Counterparty shall hold the Confidential Information in confidence, use it solely for the purpose of evaluating the Transaction, and disclose it only to its Representatives on a need-to-know basis, provided that such Representatives are informed of its confidential nature.

2.2 The Counterparty shall protect the Confidential Information with at least the degree of care that it uses for its own confidential information, and no less than a reasonable degree of care.

## 3. EXCLUSIONS

3.1 The obligations in Section 2 shall not apply to information that (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Counterparty; (b) was lawfully in the possession of the Counterparty before disclosure without restriction; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed by the Counterparty without use of the Confidential Information.

3.2 The Counterparty may disclose Confidential Information if required by law or court order, provided it gives Halvexa prompt prior written notice where lawful to do so.

## 4. TERM

4.1 This Agreement shall remain in force for a period of three (3) years from the Effective Date. The obligations in Section 2 shall survive for three (3) years after expiry or termination.

## 5. RETURN OF INFORMATION

5.1 Upon Halvexa's written request, the Counterparty shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and certify the same in writing, save for copies retained in automatic electronic archives, which shall remain subject to this Agreement.

## 6. PROTECTION OF BUSINESS INTERESTS

6.1 The Counterparty acknowledges that, by reason of the Confidential Information made available to it, it will acquire knowledge of the Group's products, markets and commercial strategy, and that the restrictions in this Section 6 are reasonable to protect the Group's legitimate business interests.

6.2 Accordingly, during the term of this Agreement and for a period of three (3) years thereafter, the Counterparty shall not, and shall procure that its affiliates, directors and shareholders shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in, develop, market or invest in any business that competes with any business of the Group anywhere in the Territory, including the design or sale of irrigation controllers, soil sensors or farm analytics software.

6.3 During the same period, the Counterparty shall not solicit, or accept business from, any person that was a customer or distributor of the Group in the Territory at any time during the term of this Agreement.

6.4 The Counterparty agrees that its existing products shall not be excluded from Section 6.2 unless expressly approved in writing by Halvexa.

## 7. NO LICENCE; NO WARRANTY

7.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of Halvexa. No licence under any patent, copyright, trade secret or other right is granted by this Agreement.

7.2 Confidential Information is provided "AS IS" without warranty of any kind.

## 8. REMEDIES AND LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

8.1 The Counterparty acknowledges that any breach of Section 2 or Section 6 would cause the Group irreparable harm, and that Halvexa shall be entitled to injunctive relief without the need to post a bond or prove actual damage.

8.2 In addition to any other remedy, the Counterparty shall pay to Halvexa, as liquidated damages and not as a penalty, the sum of USD 250,000 for each breach of this Agreement, without any limit on the aggregate amount payable, together with all attorneys' fees and costs incurred by Halvexa in enforcing this Agreement. The parties agree that this sum is a reasonable pre-estimate of the loss that Halvexa would suffer.

8.3 Each separate disclosure, use, sale or day of continuing breach shall constitute a separate breach for the purposes of Section 8.2.

## 9. ASSIGNMENT

9.1 Halvexa may assign this Agreement to any member of the Group without the Counterparty's consent. The Counterparty shall not assign this Agreement without Halvexa's prior written consent.

## 10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, United States of America, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

10.2 Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, and waives any objection to venue in such courts, including any objection that such courts are an inconvenient forum.

10.3 THE COUNTERPARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

## 11. MISCELLANEOUS

11.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning its subject matter. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.

11.2 If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remainder of this Agreement shall continue in full force.

11.3 Notices shall be in writing and sent by courier or email to the addresses above. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
