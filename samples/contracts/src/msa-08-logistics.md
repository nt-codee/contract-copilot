# LAST-MILE DELIVERY SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Last-Mile Delivery Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 27 August 2026 (the "**Effective Date**").

**PARTIES**

(1) **PANTAS ANTAR EXPRESS LIMITED** (Company No. SAMPLE-HK-1101), a company incorporated in Hong Kong with its registered office at Unit 12, 8/F, Sample Logistics Centre, 1 Contoh Road, Kwai Chung, New Territories, Hong Kong (the "**Carrier**"), operating through its Malaysian branch at No. 30, Jalan Contoh 2/1, Shah Alam, Selangor; and

(2) **KEDAI BUNGA RIA SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1102), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 6, Jalan Contoh 9/4, Taman Contoh, 43000 Kajang, Selangor, Malaysia (the "**Customer**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Carrier provides last-mile parcel collection and delivery services in Malaysia.

(B) The Customer is an online florist and gift retailer that ships about 1,500 parcels a month, and wishes to use the Carrier for delivery to its customers in the Klang Valley and Johor.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Parcel**" means any item handed by the Customer to the Carrier for delivery under this Agreement.

"**Services**" means the collection, sorting, transport and delivery of Parcels described in Schedule 1.

"**Base Rate**" means the per-Parcel delivery rate set out in the rate card in Schedule 2.

"**Declared Value**" means the value of the contents of a Parcel as stated by the Customer on the consignment note.

## 2. THE SERVICES

2.1 The Carrier shall collect Parcels from the Customer's premises each working day and shall deliver them to the address on the consignment note within the delivery times in Schedule 1.

2.2 The Carrier shall make up to two (2) delivery attempts. If both attempts fail, the Carrier shall return the Parcel to the Customer and may charge the Base Rate again for the return.

2.3 The Customer shall pack Parcels suitably, label them correctly and shall not hand over prohibited items, which are listed in Schedule 1.

2.4 The Carrier shall provide the Customer with online tracking and a daily delivery status report.

## 3. RATES AND PAYMENT

3.1 The Customer shall pay the Base Rate for each Parcel. The Carrier shall invoice the Customer weekly, and the Customer shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

3.2 In addition to the Base Rate the Customer shall pay a fuel surcharge on each Parcel. The fuel surcharge is set by the Carrier from time to time at its sole discretion and is notified on the Carrier's website. The Carrier is not required to link the fuel surcharge to any published fuel price.

3.3 Fees are exclusive of sales and service tax. The Carrier may review the Base Rate once every twelve (12) months by giving sixty (60) days' written notice.

3.4 The Customer may hold back payment of any part of an invoice that it disputes in good faith, if it pays the undisputed part on time.

## 4. LIABILITY FOR LOSS AND DAMAGE

4.1 The Carrier is responsible for loss of or damage to a Parcel while it is in the Carrier's custody, subject to this Clause 4.

4.2 The Carrier's liability for any loss of or damage to a Parcel, for delay, or for any other claim connected with that Parcel is limited to RM 50 per Parcel, regardless of the Declared Value of the Parcel and regardless of the cause of the loss.

4.3 The Carrier is not liable for any indirect or consequential loss, including loss of sales, loss of goodwill and the cost of replacing perishable goods such as fresh flowers.

4.4 The Customer must report any loss or damage in writing within forty-eight (48) hours of the scheduled delivery time, failing which the claim is barred.

## 5. CUSTOMER LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY

5.1 The Customer shall indemnify the Carrier against third-party claims, fines and confiscation costs arising from the contents of a Parcel or from the Customer's breach of Clause 2.3.

## 6. DATA PROTECTION

6.1 The Carrier shall use the recipient names, addresses and telephone numbers it receives only to deliver the Parcels, shall keep them secure and shall comply with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia. The Carrier shall delete delivery data after twenty-four (24) months.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

7.1 Each party keeps its own trade marks and other intellectual property. Neither party may use the other's name or logo without written consent.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 Each party shall keep the other's confidential information, including rates and Parcel volumes, confidential, except for information that is public, already known to the recipient, lawfully received from a third party or required by law to be disclosed. This obligation continues for three (3) years after termination.

## 9. TERM AND TERMINATION

9.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for twelve (12) months. It then continues until either party ends it by giving the other sixty (60) days' written notice.

9.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice, or becomes insolvent.

9.3 On termination the Customer shall pay for all Parcels collected up to the effective date of termination.

## 10. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

10.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Hong Kong. The Customer shall bear its own travel and legal costs.

## 11. GENERAL

11.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except that the Carrier may subcontract delivery to other carriers and remains responsible for them.

11.2 This Agreement and its Schedules are the entire agreement between the parties. Any variation must be in writing and signed by both parties.

11.3 Notices shall be sent by email or courier to the addresses stated above.
