# LETTER OF OFFER AND CONTRACT OF EMPLOYMENT

This Letter of Offer and Contract of Employment (the "**Contract**") is made on 5 October 2026 (the "**Date of Offer**").

**BETWEEN**

(1) **SERI MAJU LOGISTIK SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1501), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at Level 7, Menara Contoh, No. 18, Jalan Contoh 3/9, 50450 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (the "**Company**"); and

(2) **NUR AISYAH BINTI SAMPLE** (NRIC No. SAMPLE-000000-14-1501), of No. 22, Jalan Contoh 8, Taman Contoh, 56000 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (the "**Employee**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Company is a small logistics and warehousing business serving retail and e-commerce customers in the Klang Valley, and employs about forty (40) people at its Kuala Lumpur office and its warehouse in Shah Alam.

(B) Following interviews held in September 2026, the Company wishes to employ the Employee as an Operations Executive, and the Employee wishes to accept that employment, on the terms set out in this Contract.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Contract, unless the context requires otherwise:

"**Act**" means the Employment Act 1955 of Malaysia, as amended from time to time, together with any regulations made under it.

"**Commencement Date**" means 2 November 2026, or any other date the parties agree in writing.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Clause 10.1.

"**Client**" means any person who buys logistics, warehousing or delivery services from the Company.

"**Probation Period**" means the period described in Clause 3.1.

"**Work Product**" has the meaning given in Clause 11.1.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect the meaning of this Contract. References to a clause are to a clause of this Contract. The words "including" and "includes" do not limit the words that come before them. Where this Contract gives the Employee less than the Act requires, the Act prevails and the Employee receives the statutory benefit.

## 2. APPOINTMENT AND DUTIES

2.1 The Company employs the Employee as an Operations Executive, reporting to the Operations Manager, with effect from the Commencement Date. The Employee's normal place of work is the Company's Kuala Lumpur office, with visits to the Shah Alam warehouse when reasonably required.

2.2 The Employee's main duties are to plan daily delivery schedules, coordinate with warehouse staff and drivers, monitor service levels for Clients, and prepare weekly operations reports. A written job description has been given to the Employee with this Contract.

2.3 The Company may reasonably vary the Employee's duties from time to time, provided that the new duties are consistent with the Employee's position, skills and experience, and that any material change is discussed with the Employee in advance and confirmed in writing.

## 3. PROBATION

3.1 The Employee's first three (3) months of employment, ending on 1 February 2027, are a probation period. During probation the Operations Manager will meet the Employee at least once a month to review progress and give written feedback on any area that needs improvement.

3.2 The Company may extend the probation period once only, by up to a further three (3) months, if it has given the Employee written reasons and a written improvement plan at least two (2) weeks before the original probation period ends.

3.3 During probation either party may end the employment by giving two (2) weeks' written notice or paying salary in lieu of notice. Unless the Company has given written notice of extension or termination before the probation period ends, the Employee is confirmed in employment automatically on that date and the Company will confirm this in writing.

## 4. SALARY AND BENEFITS

4.1 The Company shall pay the Employee a basic monthly salary of RM 5,200 (Ringgit Malaysia Five Thousand Two Hundred only), payable by bank transfer no later than the seventh (7th) day after the end of each calendar month, as required by the Act.

4.2 Salary will be reviewed each January, with the first review in January 2028. Any increase is at the Company's discretion and will take into account the Employee's performance and the Company's results, and will be confirmed in writing.

4.3 The Company shall make contributions for the Employee to the Employees Provident Fund, the Social Security Organisation (SOCSO) and the Employment Insurance System at the rates required by law, and shall deduct the Employee's own statutory contributions and monthly tax deductions from salary.

4.4 The Employee is entitled to the Company's group medical and hospitalisation insurance from the Commencement Date, and to reimbursement of reasonable business travel expenses incurred with prior approval and supported by receipts, in line with the Company's expense policy.

## 5. DEDUCTIONS FROM SALARY

5.1 The Company shall not make any deduction from the Employee's salary other than deductions allowed by the Act, namely statutory contributions and taxes, recovery of any overpayment of salary made by mistake in the preceding three (3) months, and recovery of advances of wages that the Employee has asked for in writing.

5.2 Any other deduction, including for any loss or damage, may be made only with the Employee's prior written consent for that particular deduction, and only where the Act permits it. The Company shall give the Employee an itemised payslip each month showing every deduction made.

## 6. WORKING HOURS AND OVERTIME

6.1 The Employee's normal working hours are 9.00 a.m. to 6.00 p.m., Monday to Friday, with a one (1) hour lunch break, giving a total of forty (40) working hours per week. The Company shall not require normal working hours of more than forty-five (45) hours in any week.

6.2 The Employee may be asked to work beyond normal hours when operations reasonably require it, for example at peak season. Overtime must be approved in advance by the Operations Manager, and the Company will not require the Employee to work more than the overtime limit set by the Act.

6.3 For approved overtime the Company shall, at the Employee's choice, either pay the Employee at not less than one and a half (1.5) times the hourly rate of pay, calculated as provided in the Act, or give the Employee equivalent time off in lieu, to be taken within three (3) months. Work on a rest day or public holiday is paid at the rates in the Act.

## 7. LEAVE

7.1 The Employee is entitled to paid annual leave of twelve (12) working days for each complete year of service, rising to sixteen (16) working days after five (5) years of service. Leave in the first year accrues pro rata. Up to five (5) days of unused annual leave may be carried forward into the following year.

7.2 The Employee is entitled to paid sick leave of fourteen (14) days a year, and to hospitalisation leave of up to sixty (60) days a year, in each case on production of a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, and with the higher entitlements in the Act applying as the Employee's length of service increases.

7.3 The Employee is entitled to maternity leave of ninety-eight (98) consecutive days or paternity leave of seven (7) consecutive days, as the case may be, with maternity or paternity allowance, on the terms and conditions set out in the Act.

7.4 The Employee is entitled to the paid public holidays gazetted for the Federal Territory of Kuala Lumpur, being not fewer than eleven (11) days a year, including the holidays required by the Act.

## 8. NOTICE AND TERMINATION

8.1 After confirmation, either party may end the employment by giving the other one (1) month's written notice, or by paying the other one (1) month's basic salary in lieu of notice. The Company shall give at least the period of notice required by the Act where that is longer.

8.2 The Company may end the employment without notice only for serious misconduct, after a due inquiry at which the Employee has been told the allegations in writing and given a fair chance to answer them, as required by the Act.

8.3 On termination the Company shall pay the Employee all salary, approved overtime and pay in lieu of untaken annual leave due up to the last day of employment, within the time required by the Act, and shall issue a certificate of employment on request.

## 9. CONDUCT AND COMPANY POLICIES

9.1 The Employee shall comply with the Company's employee handbook, its safety rules for the warehouse, and its policies on anti-bribery, sexual harassment and use of IT systems, copies of which have been given to the Employee. Where a policy conflicts with this Contract, this Contract prevails.

9.2 The Employee may take up other paid work outside working hours with the Company's prior written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld where the work does not compete with the Company or affect the Employee's performance of the job.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 "**Confidential Information**" means non-public information about the Company's Clients, pricing, contracts, routes, systems and finances that the Employee learns in the course of employment. It does not include information that is or becomes public through no fault of the Employee, or the general skills, knowledge and experience the Employee gains in the job.

10.2 During employment and for two (2) years after it ends, the Employee shall not disclose Confidential Information to any person outside the Company, or use it except for the Company's business, unless required by law or by a court, or with the Company's written consent.

10.3 On the last day of employment, the Employee shall return all Company property and documents, and delete any copies of Confidential Information held on personal devices, and confirm this in writing if the Company asks.

## 11. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

11.1 "**Work Product**" means reports, templates, software scripts, process documents and other materials that the Employee creates in the course of the employment and as part of the Employee's duties, or using the Company's time, equipment or Confidential Information.

11.2 All intellectual property in Work Product belongs to the Company. Anything the Employee creates outside working hours, without using Company resources and unrelated to the Company's business, belongs to the Employee, as do materials the Employee owned before the Commencement Date.

## 12. NON-SOLICITATION

12.1 For six (6) months after the employment ends, the Employee shall not, on behalf of a competing logistics business, solicit for business any Client with whom the Employee had material dealings in the twelve (12) months before the employment ended.

12.2 Nothing in this Contract prevents the Employee from working for any other employer, including a competitor of the Company, after the employment ends, or from accepting business from a Client that approaches the Employee without solicitation.

## 13. PERSONAL DATA

13.1 The Company shall process the Employee's personal data only for purposes connected with the employment, such as payroll, statutory contributions, insurance and performance management, and in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010. The Employee may ask to see and correct that data at any time.

## 14. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

14.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of Malaysia.

14.2 The parties shall first try to resolve any grievance through the Company's internal grievance procedure, under which the Employee may raise a concern in writing with the Operations Manager or, if the concern involves the Operations Manager, with a director, and shall receive a written response within fourteen (14) days.

14.3 Nothing in this Clause limits the Employee's right to make a complaint to the Director General of Labour or a representation to the Industrial Relations Department. Subject to that, any dispute is subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of Kuala Lumpur.

## 15. GENERAL

15.1 This Contract and the documents it refers to are the entire agreement between the parties about the employment and replace any earlier offer or discussion. Any variation must be in writing and signed by both parties.

15.2 If any provision of this Contract is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force, and the invalid provision shall be read down to the extent needed to make it valid.

15.3 Please confirm acceptance by signing and returning a copy of this Contract by 19 October 2026. This Contract may be signed by electronic signature.
