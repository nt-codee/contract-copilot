# FACILITIES SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Facilities Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 3 August 2026.

**BETWEEN**

(1) **SERIKAYA FACILITY CARE SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-0801), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 9, Jalan Contoh 1/12, Taman Contoh Jaya, 46000 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia (the "**Contractor**"); and

(2) **ORKID SENYUM DENTAL GROUP SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-0802), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at Unit 2-5, Plaza Contoh, Jalan Contoh 8, 47800 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia (the "**Client**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Contractor provides commercial cleaning, sanitation, waste handling and minor maintenance services to clinics, offices and retail premises in the Klang Valley.

(B) The Client operates five dental clinics in Selangor and Kuala Lumpur (the "**Premises**") and wishes to engage the Contractor to provide cleaning and facilities services at the Premises.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Services**" means the daily cleaning, clinical-area sanitation, general waste handling, pest control coordination and minor maintenance services described in Schedule 1, at the frequencies stated there.

"**Monthly Fee**" means the monthly fee for the Services set out in Schedule 2.

"**Premises**" means the clinic locations listed in Schedule 1, as updated by agreement in writing.

"**Staff**" means the employees and subcontractors of the Contractor who perform the Services.

1.2 Clinical waste is excluded from the Services and remains the responsibility of the Client's licensed clinical waste contractor.

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 The Contractor shall perform the Services at the Premises in accordance with Schedule 1, outside clinic operating hours unless otherwise agreed.

2.2 The Contractor shall ensure that its Staff are properly trained, including in infection control procedures appropriate for dental clinics, wear identification at all times and comply with the Client's reasonable site rules.

2.3 The Contractor shall supply all cleaning equipment, consumables and chemicals necessary for the Services. Chemicals used in clinical areas shall be approved in advance by the Client.

2.4 The Client shall give the Contractor's Staff access to the Premises at the agreed times and shall provide secure storage for equipment.

2.5 The Contractor's supervisor shall inspect each of the Premises at least once a week and meet the Client's operations manager monthly to review performance.

## 3. TERM

3.1 This Agreement begins on 1 September 2026 and continues for an initial period of twelve (12) months.

3.2 Thereafter this Agreement renews automatically for further periods of twelve (12) months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the current period.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 The Client shall pay the Monthly Fee for each of the Premises. The Contractor shall invoice monthly in arrears.

4.2 The Client shall pay each invoice in full within fourteen (14) days of the invoice date, without any deduction or set-off.

4.3 Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of two per cent (2%) per month from the due date until the date of payment, and the Client shall also reimburse the Contractor's collection costs.

4.4 The Monthly Fee may be reviewed once a year on the anniversary of the start date by agreement between the parties.

4.5 All fees are exclusive of service tax, which the Contractor shall add to its invoices where applicable.

## 5. STANDARDS AND COMPLAINTS

5.1 The Contractor shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care and in accordance with good industry practice for healthcare premises.

5.2 If the Client notifies the Contractor that any part of the Services has not been performed to the required standard, the Contractor shall re-perform it within twenty-four (24) hours at no additional charge.

## 6. INSURANCE

6.1 The Contractor shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than RM 1,000,000 per occurrence and workers' compensation or SOCSO coverage for its Staff, and shall provide evidence of cover on request.

## 7. LIABILITY

7.1 Neither party limits its liability for death or personal injury caused by its negligence, or for fraud.

7.2 Subject to Clause 7.1, the Contractor's total liability for all claims under or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to three (3) months of the Monthly Fee, and the Contractor shall not be liable for any indirect loss, loss of profit or loss of business.

7.3 The Contractor shall be responsible for damage to the Client's equipment at the Premises caused by the negligence of its Staff, subject to Clause 7.2.

## 8. INDEMNITY

8.1 Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims for death, personal injury or damage to property to the extent caused by its negligence or that of its employees or subcontractors.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY AND PERSONAL DATA

9.1 The Contractor shall keep confidential all information it obtains about the Client's business, patients and Premises, and shall ensure its Staff do not access, copy or remove patient records or other documents.

9.2 If the Contractor's Staff come across personal data in the course of the Services, the Contractor shall not use or disclose it and shall comply with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

## 10. TERMINATION

10.1 The Contractor may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving the Client seven (7) days' written notice.

10.2 The Client may terminate this Agreement by giving the Contractor not less than ninety (90) days' written notice, or immediately if the Contractor commits a material breach which it fails to remedy within fourteen (14) days of written notice.

10.3 On termination the Client shall pay all fees for Services performed up to the termination date, and the Contractor shall remove its equipment and return all keys and access cards.

## 11. NON-SOLICITATION

11.1 During this Agreement and for six (6) months after it ends, the Client shall not directly employ any member of the Contractor's Staff who worked at the Premises, without the Contractor's consent.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Malaysia.

12.2 The parties shall try to resolve any dispute by discussion between their directors. If the dispute is not resolved within thirty (30) days, either party may refer it to the courts of Malaysia.

## 13. GENERAL

13.1 The Contractor may not assign or subcontract this Agreement without the Client's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

13.2 This Agreement, with its Schedules, is the entire agreement between the parties. Any change must be in writing and signed by both parties.
