# FLEET TRACKING SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Fleet Tracking Subscription Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into on 15 August 2026 (the "**Effective Date**").

**BETWEEN**

(1) **KIRIVANE TELEMATICS PTE. LTD.** (UEN SAMPLE-SG-0601), a company incorporated in Singapore with its registered office at 30 Sample Industrial Link, #06-11, Singapore 000601 ("**Kirivane**" or the "**Provider**"); and

(2) **BAYU SELATAN LOGISTICS SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-0602), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 14, Jalan Contoh Niaga 3, Taman Perindustrian Contoh, 81200 Johor Bahru, Johor, Malaysia (the "**Customer**").

**RECITALS**

(A) The Provider supplies GPS tracking devices, a fleet management web portal, a driver mobile application and related support services to transport operators in Singapore and Malaysia.

(B) The Customer operates a fleet of approximately forty (40) lorries and vans providing cross-border delivery services between Johor and Singapore.

(C) The Customer wishes to subscribe to the Provider's fleet tracking service on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement the following words have the following meanings:

"**Devices**" means the GPS tracking units and accessories supplied by the Provider for installation in the Customer's vehicles.

"**Portal**" means the Provider's web-based fleet management portal and the associated mobile applications.

"**Subscription Fees**" means the monthly fees per vehicle set out in Schedule 1, as adjusted under Clause 5.

"**Service**" means the provision of the Devices on a rental basis, access to the Portal, data connectivity for the Devices and the support services described in Schedule 2.

"**Customer Data**" means vehicle location data, trip records, driver records and other data generated by the Customer's use of the Service.

"**Subscription Term**" means the Initial Term together with each Renewal Term.

1.2 References to Clauses and Schedules are to the clauses of and schedules to this Agreement. The Schedules form part of this Agreement.

## 2. THE SERVICE

2.1 The Provider shall deliver and install the Devices in the Customer's vehicles within twenty-one (21) days of the Effective Date at locations agreed with the Customer, and shall provide the Service from the date of installation of the first Device.

2.2 The Provider shall use reasonable endeavours to ensure the Portal is available 99% of the time in each month, excluding scheduled maintenance.

2.3 Devices remain the property of the Provider. The Customer shall take reasonable care of the Devices and shall not remove or tamper with them except as instructed by the Provider. Faulty Devices shall be replaced by the Provider free of charge unless damaged by misuse.

2.4 The Provider shall provide support by telephone and email from 8.00 a.m. to 8.00 p.m. on weekdays, and shall respond to critical faults within four (4) hours.

## 3. CUSTOMER OBLIGATIONS

3.1 The Customer shall make its vehicles available for installation, ensure that its drivers are informed of the tracking of the vehicles, and use the Service in accordance with applicable law.

3.2 The Customer is responsible for keeping its Portal login credentials secure and for all activity carried out under its accounts.

## 4. TERM AND RENEWAL

4.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial period of twelve (12) months from the installation of the first Device (the "**Initial Term**").

4.2 Upon the expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of twenty-four (24) months each (each a "**Renewal Term**") unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal to the other party at least ninety (90) days before the expiry of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term.

4.3 Notice of non-renewal is effective only if sent by registered post to the Provider's registered office marked for the attention of the Contracts Department. Notice by email or through the Portal shall not be effective for the purposes of Clause 4.2.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

5.1 The Customer shall pay the Subscription Fees monthly in advance for each vehicle fitted with a Device, together with the one-time installation fee set out in Schedule 1.

5.2 The Provider shall invoice the Customer monthly. The Customer shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice. All amounts are exclusive of goods and services tax, sales and service tax and any other applicable taxes, which shall be borne by the Customer.

5.3 With effect from each anniversary of the Effective Date, the Provider shall be entitled to increase the Subscription Fees by twenty per cent (20%) of the Subscription Fees then in effect, without further notice to the Customer. Such increase shall apply automatically and shall not give the Customer any right to terminate this Agreement.

5.4 If any undisputed amount remains unpaid fourteen (14) days after a written reminder, the Provider may charge interest at one per cent (1%) per month on the overdue amount and, after a further fourteen (14) days' written notice, suspend the Service until payment is received.

## 6. EARLY TERMINATION

6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party commits a material breach of this Agreement which is not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice requiring it to be remedied, or if the other party becomes insolvent.

6.2 The Customer may terminate this Agreement before the end of the Subscription Term for convenience by giving thirty (30) days' written notice, provided that it pays to the Provider, as an early termination charge, all Subscription Fees that would have been payable for the remainder of the then-current Subscription Term.

6.3 On termination, the Customer shall make the vehicles available for removal of the Devices, and the Provider shall make the Customer Data available for download for thirty (30) days.

## 7. DATA

7.1 As between the parties, the Customer owns the Customer Data. The Provider may use the Customer Data only to provide the Service and to produce anonymised, aggregated statistics that do not identify the Customer, its drivers or its customers.

7.2 To the extent the Customer Data includes personal data of the Customer's drivers, the Provider shall process it only on the Customer's instructions, protect it with reasonable security measures, and comply with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia) and the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (Singapore) as applicable. The Provider shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of it.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 Each party shall keep confidential the other party's confidential information, use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and disclose it only to those of its employees and advisers who need to know it. This obligation does not apply to information that is public, already known to the recipient, lawfully received from a third party, or required to be disclosed by law. This Clause 8 survives for two (2) years after termination.

## 9. WARRANTIES

9.1 The Provider warrants that the Service will be provided with reasonable skill and care and that the Devices will be free from material defects throughout the Subscription Term.

## 10. LIABILITY

10.1 Neither party limits its liability for fraud, or for death or personal injury caused by its negligence.

10.2 Subject to Clause 10.1, each party's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the Subscription Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, and neither party is liable for loss of profit or indirect loss.

## 11. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

11.1 All intellectual property rights in the Devices, the Portal and the Provider's software remain with the Provider. The Customer is granted a non-exclusive licence to use the Portal during the Subscription Term for its internal business purposes.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.

12.2 The parties shall attempt to resolve any dispute by negotiation between senior managers for thirty (30) days. Failing resolution, the dispute shall be referred to the courts of Singapore, which shall have non-exclusive jurisdiction.

## 13. GENERAL

13.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

13.2 This Agreement, together with its Schedules, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. No variation shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.

13.3 Neither party shall be liable for any delay or failure caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control.
