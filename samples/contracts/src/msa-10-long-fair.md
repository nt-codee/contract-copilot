(A) The Supplier provides software development, systems integration and managed IT services to small and medium businesses in Malaysia.

(C) The parties have agreed that the Supplier will deliver and support that system on the terms of this Agreement and its Schedules.

# SCHEDULE 1: SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES

## Part A. Overview of the System

1.1 The System is a web-based warehouse and shipment tracking platform that will replace the Customer's current spreadsheets and its existing legacy application. It shall be configured to the Customer's business processes for two (2) warehouses at Port Klang and Shah Alam.

1.2 The System shall include the following modules: (a) inbound receiving and put-away; (b) stock location management and cycle counts; (c) outbound picking, packing and dispatch; (d) shipment tracking with customer notifications by email and SMS; (e) customs document storage and export to the Customer's customs agent; (f) management reports and a dashboard; and (g) interfaces to the Customer's accounting software and to two (2) courier tracking services.

## Part B. Implementation milestones


2.1 Milestone 1 (Design): the Supplier delivers the functional specification and the data migration plan for approval by the Customer, by 16 October 2026. The instalment due on Acceptance is twenty per cent (20%) of the implementation fee.

2.2 Milestone 2 (Build): the Supplier delivers the configured System in a test environment with the interfaces to the accounting software and the courier services, by 18 December 2026. The instalment due on Acceptance is thirty per cent (30%) of the implementation fee.

2.3 Milestone 3 (Migration and training): the Supplier migrates the Customer's stock and shipment data and trains the Customer's staff for a total of five (5) days, by 29 January 2027. The instalment due on Acceptance is twenty-five per cent (25%) of the implementation fee.

2.4 Milestone 4 (Go-Live): the Supplier takes the System live in both warehouses and provides on-site support during the first ten (10) Business Days. The instalment due on Acceptance is the final twenty-five per cent (25%) of the implementation fee.

## Part C. Acceptance criteria

3.1 Each module must perform the functions described in the functional specification approved at Milestone 1, without any defect of severity 1 or severity 2 as defined in Schedule 3.

3.2 The System must process at least 500 shipments a day and support thirty (30) concurrent users with page response times under three (3) seconds for ninety-five per cent (95%) of requests during the performance test.

3.3 At least ninety-nine per cent (99%) of migrated stock records must match the Customer's stock count in the sample check carried out by the project managers.

## Part D. Support and managed services

4.1 After Go-Live the Supplier shall provide: (a) hosting and monitoring of the System; (b) a help desk for the Customer's staff on Business Days from 8.00 a.m. to 6.00 p.m., with emergency support outside these hours for severity 1 incidents; (c) bug fixes and security updates; (d) a minor enhancement allowance of twenty (20) hours a quarter; and (e) a quarterly service review meeting.

4.2 Hosting shall be in data centres in Malaysia with daily backups retained for thirty (30) days and a disaster recovery copy in a second Malaysian data centre.

# SCHEDULE 3: SERVICE LEVELS

## Part A. Incident severity

1.1 Severity 1: the System is unavailable or a critical function cannot be used, and there is no workaround. Severity 2: a major function is seriously impaired, or a workaround is available but is not practical for daily use. Severity 3: a minor function does not work and a workable workaround is available. Severity 4: a cosmetic issue, a question or a request for information.

## Part B. Response and resolution times

2.1 The Supplier shall respond to and resolve incidents within the times in paragraph 2.2, measured within support hours (and around the clock for severity 1).


2.2 Severity 1: response in thirty (30) minutes; resolution or workaround in four (4) hours. Severity 2: response in two (2) hours; resolution or workaround in one (1) Business Day. Severity 3: response in four (4) hours; resolution in five (5) Business Days. Severity 4: response in one (1) Business Day; resolution in the next scheduled release.

## Part C. Availability

3.1 The Supplier shall make the System available for at least 99.9% of the time in each calendar month, measured from 6.00 a.m. to 10.00 p.m. every day, excluding planned maintenance notified at least five (5) Business Days in advance and carried out outside those hours.

3.2 The Supplier shall measure availability with automated monitoring and provide the Customer with a monthly report within five (5) Business Days after each month end.

## Part D. Service credits

4.1 If availability in a month is below 99.9% but at least 99.0%, the service credit is five per cent (5%) of the support and managed service fee for that month. If availability is below 99.0% but at least 98.0%, the credit is ten per cent (10%). If availability is below 98.0%, the credit is twenty per cent (20%).

4.2 If the Supplier misses the resolution time for a severity 1 incident, the service credit is a further five per cent (5%) of that month's fee for each additional four (4) hours, up to a total of twenty-five per cent (25%) for the month.

4.3 The Supplier shall apply service credits to the next invoice without the Customer having to claim them.
