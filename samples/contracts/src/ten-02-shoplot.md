# TENANCY AGREEMENT (SHOP LOT)

This Tenancy Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 20 October 2026.

**BETWEEN**

(1) **TAMAN HARMONI PROPERTI SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1401), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 3, Jalan Contoh 14/8, Seksyen 14, 46100 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia (the "**Landlord**"); and

(2) **KOPI SENJA ENTERPRISE SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1402), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 18, Jalan Contoh 3/2, Taman Contoh, 47300 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia (the "**Tenant**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Landlord is the registered proprietor of a three-storey shop-lot building at Lot 12, Jalan Contoh 14/8, Seksyen 14, 46100 Petaling Jaya, Selangor (the "**Building**").

(B) The Tenant operates a cafe and wishes to rent the ground floor of the Building, with an area of about 1,600 square feet (the "**Premises**"), and the Landlord has agreed to let the Premises to the Tenant on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. TERM

1.1 The Landlord lets the Premises to the Tenant for a term of three (3) years, starting on 1 December 2026 and ending on 30 November 2029 (the "**Term**").

1.2 The Landlord shall hand over the Premises on 1 December 2026 in the condition seen by the Tenant. The Tenant has a rent-free fit-out period of thirty (30) days from the handover.

## 2. USE OF THE PREMISES

2.1 The Tenant shall use the Premises only to run a cafe selling food, beverages and related items, under the trading name "Kopi Senja", and for no other purpose without the Landlord's written consent.

2.2 The Tenant shall obtain and keep in force at its own cost all licences and permits needed for its business, including the premises licence from the local authority and the food premises registration, and shall give the Landlord a copy on request.

2.3 The Tenant may put up a shop sign on the front of the Building in the place and of the size approved by the Landlord in writing, which approval shall not be unreasonably withheld. The Tenant shall remove the sign at the end of the Term and make good any damage.

2.4 The Tenant shall not cause a nuisance to the other occupiers of the Building, shall keep the five-foot way in front of the Premises clear, and shall dispose of its rubbish and kitchen waste in the way required by the local authority.

## 3. RENT AND PAYMENT

3.1 The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of RM6,800 (the "**Rent**"), in advance, on or before the fifth (5th) day of each month, by bank transfer to the account the Landlord notifies in writing. The Rent is exclusive of any sales and service tax, which the Tenant shall pay in addition if it applies.

3.2 The Tenant shall also pay the electricity and water used at the Premises, the Tenant's own telephone and internet, and the service charge for the Building's common areas of RM350 a month.

3.3 If any sum is not paid within seven (7) days after the due date, the Tenant shall pay interest on the late amount at one per cent (1%) a month until payment.

## 4. SECURITY DEPOSIT

4.1 On signing this Agreement, the Tenant shall pay the Landlord a security deposit of RM20,400, equal to three (3) months' Rent (the "**Deposit**"), and a utility deposit of RM3,000.

4.2 The Deposit is held as security for the Tenant's obligations under this Agreement. The Security Deposit shall be non-refundable at the Landlord's discretion on the expiry or earlier termination of this Agreement, and the Landlord is not required to give reasons or to account for its decision.

4.3 The utility deposit shall be returned within thirty (30) days after the Tenant has paid the final utility bills.

## 5. RENT REVIEW

5.1 The Rent for the Term shall be reviewed each year on the anniversary of the start of the Term. The Landlord may, in its sole discretion, increase the Rent by any amount and at any time, by giving the Tenant fourteen (14) days' notice, and the Tenant shall pay the new Rent from the date stated in the notice.

5.2 The Tenant has no right to refuse the new Rent or to end this Agreement because of an increase.

## 6. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE

6.1 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises, its fittings and signage in good condition, and shall be responsible for the cost of all repairs, maintenance and replacements of every kind to the Premises and the Building, including structural repairs to the roof, external walls, foundation, columns and main drainage, whether or not the need for repair was caused by the Tenant.

6.2 The Landlord may enter the Premises at reasonable times on twenty-four (24) hours' notice to inspect their condition.

6.3 The Tenant shall carry out its fit-out at its own cost, after the Landlord has approved the plans in writing, and shall comply with the requirements of the local authority and the fire department.

## 7. INSURANCE

7.1 The Landlord shall insure the Building against fire and other usual risks. The Tenant shall insure its own stock, equipment and fit-out, and shall carry public liability insurance of at least RM1,000,000 for each claim, and shall give the Landlord a copy of the certificate on request.

## 8. DEFAULT AND RE-ENTRY

8.1 If the Rent or any other sum due under this Agreement is unpaid for seven (7) days after the due date, the Landlord may, without notice and without a court order, re-enter the Premises and change the locks, and may keep the Tenant's goods in the Premises until all arrears are paid. This Agreement shall then end, without affecting the Landlord's right to claim all sums due.

8.2 If the Tenant breaks any other term and does not fix the breach within fourteen (14) days of the Landlord's written notice, the Landlord may end this Agreement by written notice.

## 9. EARLY TERMINATION BY THE TENANT

9.1 The Tenant may not end this Agreement before the end of the Term except under clause 9.2.

9.2 After the first twenty-four (24) months of the Term, the Tenant may end this Agreement by giving the Landlord at least three (3) months' written notice. If the Tenant ends this Agreement early in any other way, the Tenant shall pay the Landlord all Rent for the remainder of the Term, as agreed compensation.

## 10. RENEWAL

10.1 At the end of the Term, the Tenant may ask the Landlord in writing, at least three (3) months before the end of the Term, to renew this Agreement. The Landlord shall consider the request, and any renewal shall be on terms agreed in writing. This Agreement does not renew automatically.

## 11. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

11.1 The Tenant shall not assign or sublet the Premises, or part of them, without the Landlord's written consent, which the Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold or delay.

11.2 If the Landlord sells the Building, the Tenant's rights under this Agreement continue against the buyer, and the Landlord shall pay the Deposit and the utility deposit to the buyer.

## 12. STAMP DUTY

12.1 The Tenant shall bear the stamp duty on this Agreement and shall have it stamped within thirty (30) days of signing. The Landlord shall bear the stamp duty on the counterpart.

## 13. GENERAL

13.1 Notices shall be in writing and sent to the addresses above, and are treated as received two (2) days after posting.

13.2 This Agreement is the whole agreement between the Parties about the Premises. Any change must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

13.3 This Agreement is governed by the law of Malaysia. The courts of Kuala Lumpur shall have jurisdiction over any dispute.
