## DEFINITIONS AND NOTES

A. In this Agreement, "**Term**" means the period of eighty-four (84) months over which the instalments are payable, "**Schedule**" means the Schedule at the end of this Agreement, and "**Act**" means the Hire-Purchase Act 1967. Headings are for convenience only and do not change the meaning of any clause.

B. The Hirer confirms that the Hirer has read this Agreement, that the Dealer has explained it to the Hirer in a language the Hirer understands and that the Hirer has been given a copy. The Hirer was given the chance to ask questions before signing.

C. The Hirer confirms that the information given in the application form is true and complete. The Owner may rely on it, and may end this Agreement if any of it was untrue.

D. The Owner is a finance company that buys vehicles chosen by customers through its dealer network. The Dealer is a separate business and is not employed by the Owner.

E. All sums are in Malaysian ringgit. A reference to a month is a calendar month. If a due date is not a working day, payment is due on the next working day.

## 1. THE VEHICLE AND THE PRICE

1.1 The Owner lets and the Hirer takes on hire-purchase the used motor vehicle described in the Schedule (the "**Vehicle**"), which was found and arranged for the Hirer by Pasar Contoh Motors (the "**Dealer**"). The Vehicle is for the Hirer's personal use.

1.2 The Hirer has inspected the Vehicle and takes it in its present condition.

1.3 The key figures are:

(a) cash price of the Vehicle: RM 46,000.00;

(b) deposit paid by the Hirer: RM 8,000.00;

(c) amount financed: RM 38,000.00;

(d) number of monthly instalments (the "**Term**"): 84, each of RM 636.05, due on the 3rd day of each month; and

(e) total amount payable under the instalments: RM 53,428.00.

## 2. INTEREST

2.1 The Hirer shall pay interest at a flat rate of 5.8% a year on the amount financed for the whole Term. The interest is added to the amount financed and the total is divided into equal monthly instalments.

2.2 The interest is calculated on the original amount financed for the whole Term, whatever the Hirer has already paid.

## 3. PAYMENT AND FEES

3.1 The Hirer shall pay each instalment on its due date to the Owner or to the Dealer. A payment made to the Dealer discharges the Hirer only when the Owner receives it.

3.2 The Hirer shall also pay on the Agreement Date an administration fee of RM 600.00 and a document processing fee of RM 350.00 to the Dealer. These fees are not refundable.

3.3 If an instalment is paid late, the Hirer shall pay a late charge of RM 50.00 for each late instalment and interest on the overdue amount at 1% a month.

## 4. INSURANCE

4.1 The Hirer must insure the Vehicle throughout the Term only with Bintang Cover Berhad (the "**Dealer's Insurer**"), through the Dealer, with comprehensive cover and the Owner noted as interested party. The Hirer may not insure the Vehicle with any other insurer, and the Owner may refuse any other policy.

4.2 The premium is set by the Dealer's Insurer from year to year, and is estimated at RM 2,850.00 for the first year. The Hirer shall pay each premium to the Dealer on the date the Dealer asks. If the Hirer does not, the Owner may pay the premium and add it to the amount owed, together with a handling charge of RM 100.00.

4.3 Premiums are not refunded if the Hirer settles this Agreement early or the Vehicle is sold.

## 5. CONDITION OF THE VEHICLE

5.1 The Vehicle is sold and let as seen. The Owner and the Dealer give no warranty about its condition, its mileage, its history or its fitness for any purpose, and the Hirer waives any claim in respect of them.

5.2 Neither the Owner nor the Dealer is responsible for anything the Dealer or its staff said about the Vehicle before the Hirer signed this Agreement. The Hirer must keep paying the instalments even if the Vehicle is faulty, off the road or not delivered.

## 6. DEFAULT AND REPOSSESSION

6.1 The Owner may take possession of the Vehicle immediately and without notice if the Hirer misses any one (1) instalment, or if the Hirer breaches any other term of this Agreement. The Hirer authorises the Owner and its agents to enter any premises where the Vehicle is kept for that purpose and to take it away, and the Owner is not responsible for any loss caused by doing so.

6.2 If the Owner takes possession, the Owner may sell the Vehicle at once at any price it considers fair and keep the proceeds. The Hirer remains liable for all instalments that would have fallen due, together with all costs of repossession, storage and sale and the Owner's legal costs on a full indemnity basis, less only the sale price actually received.

6.3 All the remaining instalments become due immediately if the Hirer is late with any payment, if the Hirer is declared bankrupt or if the Hirer changes address without telling the Owner.

## 7. EARLY SETTLEMENT AND ENDING THE AGREEMENT

7.1 The Hirer may settle this Agreement early only with the Owner's written consent, and must pay all the instalments that remain unpaid under this Agreement. Because the interest is fixed for the whole Term, no rebate of terms charges will be given for early settlement, and no part of the interest already added to the instalments is refundable.

7.2 The Hirer may not end this Agreement by returning the Vehicle. Any return of the Vehicle is a default and does not reduce the sums owed.

## 8. OWNERSHIP

8.1 The Vehicle remains the Owner's property until every sum due has been paid. When that happens, the Owner shall transfer the Vehicle to the Hirer, who shall pay the Owner's transfer costs.

## 9. GUARANTOR

9.1 No guarantor is required for this Agreement.

## 10. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Malaysia. Any dispute shall be decided by a single arbitrator chosen by the Owner, sitting in Klang, and the Hirer waives any right to bring proceedings in court. The Hirer must start any claim against the Owner within three (3) months of the event.
