# DIGITAL MARKETING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Digital Marketing Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 20 August 2026 (the "**Effective Date**").

**PARTIES**

(1) **LANTERN & LOOM MEDIA PTE. LTD.** (UEN SAMPLE-SG-1001), a company incorporated in Singapore with its registered office at 8 Sample Creative Lane, #02-04, Singapore 001001 (the "**Agency**"); and

(2) **SENJA REKA STUDIO SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-1002), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its registered office at No. 9, Jalan Contoh 7/12, Mont Kiara, 50480 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (the "**Client**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Agency provides social media, paid advertising and content marketing services.

(B) The Client is an interior design studio and wishes to engage the Agency to run its digital marketing for a monthly retainer.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Services**" means the social media management, paid advertising, content production and monthly reporting described in Schedule 1.

"**Retainer**" means the monthly fee of RM 6,500 for the Services, as set out in Schedule 2.

"**Campaign Creatives**" means all advertisements, images, videos, copy, captions, graphics, landing pages and other materials produced by the Agency for the Client under this Agreement.

"**Ad Accounts**" means the advertising, analytics and social media accounts, pages and pixels set up or used by the Agency to run the Client's campaigns.

"**Ad Spend**" means the amounts paid to advertising platforms for running the Client's campaigns, which are charged to the Client at cost in addition to the Retainer.

## 2. THE SERVICES

2.1 The Agency shall provide the Services from the Effective Date with reasonable skill and care, using suitably experienced staff.

2.2 The Agency shall send the Client a performance report by the fifth (5th) day of each month and shall meet the Client at least once a month to review results.

2.3 The Client shall give the Agency timely access to information, photographs of completed projects and approvals. The Client shall approve or comment on draft content within three (3) days. Content not rejected within that time is treated as approved.

2.4 The Agency does not guarantee any particular number of enquiries, followers or sales.

## 3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 The Agency shall invoice the Retainer monthly in advance and the Ad Spend monthly in arrears. The Client shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

3.2 Fees are exclusive of taxes. Late payments carry interest at one per cent (1%) a month on amounts that are not disputed in good faith.

3.3 The Client may hold back the disputed part of an invoice while the parties resolve the dispute, if it pays the undisputed part and explains the dispute in writing.

## 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

4.1 The Client keeps all rights in its trade marks, logos, photographs of its projects and the materials it supplies to the Agency (the "**Client Materials**"), and grants the Agency a licence to use them only to perform the Services.

4.2 All intellectual property rights in the Campaign Creatives shall vest in and remain with the Agency. The Agency grants the Client a non-exclusive licence to use the Campaign Creatives for the Client's own marketing only for as long as this Agreement continues.

4.3 The Agency owns and controls the Ad Accounts. The Client has no right to transfer, access or take over the Ad Accounts, the audience data or the campaign history held in them, whether during or after this Agreement.

4.4 On termination for any reason, the licence in Clause 4.2 ends and the Client shall stop using the Campaign Creatives.

## 5. TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for a minimum term of twelve (12) months (the "**Minimum Term**"). After the Minimum Term it continues month to month until either party ends it by giving thirty (30) days' written notice.

5.2 The Client may not terminate this Agreement for convenience during the Minimum Term. If the Client ends this Agreement for any reason other than the Agency's uncured material breach before the end of the Minimum Term, the Client shall pay the Agency, as an early exit fee, an amount equal to all Retainers that would have been payable for the remainder of the Minimum Term.

5.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within fourteen (14) days of written notice, or becomes insolvent.

5.4 On termination the Client shall pay all Fees due up to the effective date of termination, and the Agency shall hand over the Client Materials it holds.

## 6. NON-SOLICITATION

6.1 During this Agreement and for twenty-four (24) months afterwards, the Client shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit or employ any person who is or was an employee or freelancer of the Agency and who worked on the Client's account, without the Agency's prior written consent.

6.2 If the Client breaches Clause 6.1, it shall pay the Agency an amount equal to twelve (12) months of that person's salary or fees at the Agency.

## 7. WARRANTIES AND CLIENT MATERIALS

7.1 The Agency warrants that the Campaign Creatives will be original and, to the best of its knowledge, will not infringe the rights of any third party.

7.2 The Client warrants that it has the right to give the Agency the Client Materials.

## 8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.1 Neither party shall be liable to the other for loss of profits, revenue or goodwill or for any indirect or consequential loss.

8.2 Each party's total liability under this Agreement in any twelve (12) month period shall not exceed the Retainers paid or payable in the twelve (12) months before the claim, except for the Client's obligation to pay fees and for liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 9. DATA PROTECTION

9.1 The Agency shall process any personal data it receives from the Client (including lead and customer lists) only on the Client's instructions and for the Services, shall keep it secure and shall comply with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia and the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 of Singapore, where they apply.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 Each party shall keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement, except for information that is public, already known to the recipient, lawfully received from a third party or required by law to be disclosed. This obligation continues for three (3) years after termination.

## 11. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Malaysia.

11.2 The parties shall first try to resolve any dispute by discussion between senior management for thirty (30) days. Any unresolved dispute shall be decided by the courts of Kuala Lumpur.

## 12. GENERAL

12.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent.

12.2 This Agreement and its Schedules are the entire agreement between the parties. Any variation must be in writing and signed by both parties.

12.3 Notices shall be sent by email or courier to the addresses stated above.
