# NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is dated 2 September 2026 (the "**Effective Date**").

**PARTIES**

(1) **QUILLSTONE FOOD PLATFORMS PTE. LTD.** (UEN SAMPLE-SG-0201), a company incorporated in Singapore whose registered office is at 7 Sample Boulevard, #12-01, Singapore 000201 (the "**Discloser**"); and

(2) **CENDANA CRAFT BAKERY SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-0202), a company incorporated in Malaysia whose registered office is at No. 21, Jalan Contoh 7/2, Taman Contoh, 47300 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia (the "**Recipient**").

**RECITALS**

(A) The Discloser operates an online ordering and delivery platform for food and beverage outlets in Singapore and Malaysia, together with central kitchens and a private-label product range.

(B) The Recipient operates artisan bakeries in the Klang Valley and supplies baked goods to cafes and hotels.

(C) The Discloser is considering appointing the Recipient as a supplier of baked goods for the Discloser's private-label range and for outlets listed on its platform, and the Recipient wishes to tender for that appointment (the "**Proposed Arrangement**").

(D) For the Recipient to prepare its tender, the Discloser intends to share commercial, operational and technical information with the Recipient. The Discloser requires that information to be protected on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context requires otherwise:

(a) "**Affiliate**" means, in relation to the Discloser, any company that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with the Discloser;

(b) "**Purpose**" means the Recipient's evaluation of, and preparation of a tender for, the Proposed Arrangement;

(c) "**Representatives**" means the Recipient's directors and employees who are directly involved in the Purpose; and

(d) a reference to a "person" includes an individual, company, partnership or other body.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement do not affect its interpretation. Words in the singular include the plural and vice versa.

1.3 This Agreement is to be read as a whole. Where a provision of this Agreement appears to conflict with any other document exchanged between the parties, this Agreement prevails.

## 2. PROVISION OF INFORMATION

2.1 The Discloser may, in its sole discretion, provide information to the Recipient in any form, including in documents, electronic files, presentations, site visits to the Discloser's central kitchens, tastings, and discussions with the Discloser's staff.

2.2 The Discloser is not obliged to provide any information, and may stop providing information at any time without giving a reason.

2.3 The Recipient shall nominate one Representative as its single point of contact for the Purpose and shall notify the Discloser of that person's name and contact details.

## 3. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

3.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all information of any kind whatsoever, in any form or medium, whether or not marked as confidential, that is disclosed to or obtained by the Recipient or its Representatives in connection with the Purpose or the Proposed Arrangement, including recipes, product specifications, supplier lists, pricing, volumes, sales data, outlet information, business plans, and any information that the Recipient already holds or that is in the public domain, together with all notes, analyses and other materials prepared by the Recipient that contain or reflect any such information.

3.2 For the avoidance of doubt, information that is already known to the Recipient, or that the Recipient obtains from a third party, falls within the definition of Confidential Information if it relates in any way to the Discloser, its Affiliates or the Proposed Arrangement.

## 4. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECIPIENT

4.1 The Recipient shall:

(a) keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential;

(b) use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;

(c) not disclose the Confidential Information to any person other than its Representatives, and not to its professional advisers, contractors or financiers, without the prior written consent of the Discloser;

(d) not copy or reproduce any Confidential Information except as strictly necessary for the Purpose; and

(e) be liable for any act or omission of its Representatives as if it were the act or omission of the Recipient.

4.2 The Recipient's obligations under this Agreement apply without exception, irrespective of whether any Confidential Information is or becomes publicly available, was already known to the Recipient, or is required to be disclosed by any law, regulation or order of any court or authority.

4.3 The Discloser gives no undertaking of confidentiality in respect of any information provided to it by the Recipient, including the Recipient's tender, pricing and product samples, and may use and share such information with its Affiliates and other suppliers as it sees fit.

## 5. DURATION

5.1 This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date. The Recipient's obligations under this Agreement shall continue in perpetuity and shall survive the completion or abandonment of the Proposed Arrangement and any termination of this Agreement.

5.2 Only the Discloser may terminate this Agreement, by written notice to the Recipient at any time. Termination shall not release the Recipient from any of its obligations under this Agreement.

## 6. RETURN OF MATERIALS

6.1 Within five (5) days of a written request from the Discloser, the Recipient shall return to the Discloser, or at the Discloser's option destroy, all documents and materials containing Confidential Information, including all copies held electronically and in back-up systems, and shall deliver to the Discloser a statutory declaration made by a director of the Recipient confirming that it has done so.

## 7. NO RIGHTS GRANTED

7.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. No licence or other right is granted to the Recipient in respect of any Confidential Information.

7.2 Nothing in this Agreement obliges the Discloser to appoint the Recipient as a supplier or to enter into any further agreement.

## 8. REMEDIES

8.1 The Recipient acknowledges that damages may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this Agreement and that the Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedy.

8.2 The Recipient shall indemnify the Discloser and its Affiliates against all losses, costs (including legal costs on a full indemnity basis) and expenses arising from any breach of this Agreement by the Recipient or its Representatives.

## 9. GENERAL

9.1 The Discloser may assign this Agreement or any of its rights under it to any of its Affiliates or to any purchaser of its business. The Recipient may not assign this Agreement.

9.2 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties relating to its subject matter.

9.3 No variation of this Agreement is effective unless it is in writing and signed by both parties.

9.4 Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, courier or email to the addresses set out above.

## 10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Singapore.
