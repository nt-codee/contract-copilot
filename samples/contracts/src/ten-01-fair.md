# TENANCY AGREEMENT (RESIDENTIAL)

This Tenancy Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 15 October 2026.

**BETWEEN**

(1) **MS LIM SOON HUAT** (NRIC No. SAMPLE-SG-0101), of 14 Contoh Walk, #05-12 Marina Sample Residences, Singapore 018900 (the "**Landlord**"); and

(2) **MR DANIEL RAJENDRAN** (Passport No. SAMPLE-SG-0102), currently residing at 22 Contoh Lane, Singapore 238800 (the "**Tenant**").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Landlord is the owner of a two-bedroom private condominium apartment at 14 Contoh Walk, #05-12 Marina Sample Residences, Singapore 018900 (the "**Premises**"), together with the furniture and appliances listed in the inventory in Schedule 1 (the "**Inventory**").

(B) The Tenant wishes to rent the Premises as his home, and the Landlord has agreed to let the Premises on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. TERM

1.1 The Landlord lets the Premises to the Tenant for a fixed term of twenty-four (24) months, starting on 1 November 2026 and ending on 31 October 2028 (the "**Term**").

1.2 On the first day of the Term the Landlord shall hand over the Premises and the keys. The Parties shall sign the handover checklist in Schedule 1, with dated photographs, to record the condition of the Premises and the Inventory at handover.

## 2. RENT AND OTHER PAYMENTS

2.1 The Tenant shall pay rent of S$3,800 a month (the "**Rent**"), in advance, on or before the first day of each month, by bank transfer to the account named in Schedule 2. The Landlord shall give a receipt for each payment on request.

2.2 The Rent is fixed for the whole Term and shall not be increased during the Term.

2.3 The Tenant shall pay for electricity, gas, water, telephone and internet used at the Premises, directly to the providers. The Landlord shall pay the condominium's maintenance and sinking fund contributions and the property tax.

2.4 If any Rent is not paid within seven (7) days after the due date and the Landlord has sent the Tenant a written reminder, the Tenant shall pay interest on the late amount at one per cent (1%) a month until it is paid.

## 3. SECURITY DEPOSIT

3.1 On signing, the Tenant shall pay the Landlord a security deposit of S$7,600, equal to two (2) months' Rent (the "**Deposit**"). The Landlord holds the Deposit as security for unpaid Rent, unpaid utilities and damage to the Premises or the Inventory beyond fair wear and tear.

3.2 The Tenant shall not treat the Deposit as payment of Rent for the last month of the Term unless the Landlord agrees in writing.

3.3 The Landlord shall return the Deposit within fourteen (14) days after the Tenant gives back the keys and the Parties have checked the Premises against the handover checklist. The Landlord may deduct only the amounts described in clause 3.1 and shall give the Tenant a written list of each deduction with receipts or quotations.

3.4 If the Landlord sells the Premises during the Term, the Landlord shall transfer the Deposit to the buyer or refund it to the Tenant, and shall tell the Tenant in writing.

## 4. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE

4.1 The Landlord is responsible for the structure of the Premises, the main water pipes, wiring and drains, the air-conditioning units and the other appliances in the Inventory, except where the Tenant caused the fault. The Landlord shall carry out each repair within fourteen (14) days of the Tenant's notice, or sooner if the problem makes part of the Premises unusable.

4.2 The Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and in good order, shall replace light bulbs and filters, and shall pay for minor repairs of up to S$200 for each repair. The Tenant shall pay for damage caused by the Tenant or his guests.

4.3 The Landlord may enter the Premises at reasonable times to inspect or repair, after giving the Tenant at least forty-eight (48) hours' notice, except in an emergency.

## 5. USE OF THE PREMISES

5.1 The Tenant shall use the Premises only as a private home for himself and his immediate family, with no more than four (4) occupants.

5.2 The Tenant shall not carry on a business at the Premises, shall not make structural changes, and shall comply with the condominium's house rules, a copy of which the Landlord has given to the Tenant.

## 6. INSURANCE

6.1 The Landlord shall insure the building and the Inventory against fire and similar risks. The Tenant shall insure his own belongings if he wishes. The Tenant is not responsible for loss or damage caused by fire or other events outside his control, unless he or his guests caused it.

## 7. EARLY TERMINATION BY THE TENANT

7.1 After the first twelve (12) months of the Term, the Tenant may end this Agreement early by giving the Landlord at least two (2) months' written notice, and paying one (1) month's Rent as a fee for leaving early. The Landlord shall try to find a new tenant, and shall return the Deposit under clause 3.3.

7.2 If the Tenant is posted out of Singapore by his employer before the end of the Term, he may end this Agreement on two (2) months' written notice with a letter from his employer, with no fee for leaving early.

## 8. END OF THE AGREEMENT AND DEFAULT

8.1 This Agreement ends on 31 October 2028. If the Parties wish to continue, the Landlord shall propose renewal terms in writing at least two (2) months before the end of the Term, and the Tenant may accept or decline. The Agreement does not renew automatically.

8.2 If the Tenant fails to pay Rent when due, or breaks another term of this Agreement, the Landlord may give the Tenant a written notice describing the breach and allowing fourteen (14) days to remedy it. If the breach is not remedied, the Landlord may end this Agreement by further written notice.

8.3 If the Tenant does not leave after this Agreement ends, the Landlord shall apply to the court for an order for possession. The Landlord shall not change the locks or remove the Tenant's belongings without a court order.

## 9. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

9.1 The Tenant shall not assign or sublet the Premises without the Landlord's written consent, which the Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold or delay (and shall decide within fourteen (14) days of the request).

9.2 If the Landlord sells or transfers the Premises, the Tenant's rights under this Agreement continue against the new owner.

## 10. STAMP DUTY

10.1 The Parties shall share the stamp duty on this Agreement equally. The Tenant shall arrange stamping within the time required by law and shall give the Landlord a copy of the stamp certificate.

## 11. GENERAL

11.1 Notices shall be in writing and sent to the addresses above or to the email addresses in Schedule 2, and are treated as received two (2) days after posting or on the next working day after email.

11.2 This Agreement and its Schedules are the whole agreement between the Parties about the Premises. Any change must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

11.3 This Agreement is governed by the law of Singapore. The Parties shall first try to settle any dispute by talking to each other, and then by mediation, before starting court proceedings in the courts of Singapore.
