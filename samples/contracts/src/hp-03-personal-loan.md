# PERSONAL LOAN AGREEMENT WITH GUARANTEE

This Loan Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is made on 20 September 2026 (the "**Agreement Date**"). This is a sample consumer document with invented parties and figures.

**BETWEEN**

(1) **KILAT KREDIT SDN. BHD.** (Company No. SAMPLE-MY-0803), a licensed moneylender (licence number SAMPLE-ML-0803) with its registered office at No. 3, Jalan Contoh Wang 4, 80000 Johor Bahru, Johor, Malaysia (the "**Lender**");

(2) **TAN WEI LING** (NRIC No. SAMPLE-000000-00-0002), an individual residing at No. 15, Jalan Sampel Harmoni 2, 81100 Johor Bahru, Johor, Malaysia (the "**Borrower**"); and

(3) **TAN KOK HENG** (NRIC No. SAMPLE-000000-00-0003), an individual residing at No. 15, Jalan Sampel Harmoni 2, 81100 Johor Bahru, Johor, Malaysia (the "**Guarantor**").

## DEFINITIONS AND NOTES

A. In this Agreement, "**Loan**" means the sum lent under Clause 1, "**instalment**" means a monthly payment under Clause 1.3, "**business day**" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Johor, and "**moneylending agreement**" has the meaning given in the Moneylenders Act 1951.

B. The Lender is licensed under the Moneylenders Act 1951 and shall show its licence on request. The Lender's licence conditions require this Agreement to be in writing, to be explained to the Borrower and to be signed by the Borrower.

C. The Borrower confirms that the information given in the application is true and complete, that the Loan is for the Borrower's own use and that the Borrower has been given a copy of this Agreement and a repayment schedule. The Borrower had the chance to ask questions before signing.

D. All sums are in Malaysian ringgit. If a due date is not a business day, payment is due on the next business day. Headings are for convenience and do not change the meaning of any clause.

E. The Guarantor is a family member of the Borrower who has agreed to guarantee the Loan. The Guarantor is not a borrower and receives no part of the Loan.

## 1. THE LOAN

1.1 The Lender shall lend the Borrower RM 20,000.00 (the "**Loan**") as an unsecured personal loan for the Borrower's personal use.

1.2 The Lender shall pay the Loan to the Borrower's bank account on the day after the Borrower returns this signed Agreement, less the fees in Clause 3.

1.3 The Borrower shall repay the Loan in thirty-six (36) equal monthly instalments of RM 703.14 each, the first due on 20 October 2026. The total of the instalments is RM 25,313.04.

## 2. INTEREST

2.1 Interest is charged at 16% a year on the reducing balance (the amount of the Loan still owed). Each instalment is made up of interest and a repayment of the Loan, and the Lender has given the Borrower a repayment schedule showing the split for every instalment.

2.2 The rate in Clause 2.1 is the only interest charged under this Agreement before default.

## 3. FEES

3.1 The Lender shall deduct from the Loan before payment a processing fee of RM 400.00 and stamp duty. The Borrower acknowledges that interest is charged on the full RM 20,000.00.

3.2 The Borrower shall pay no other fee before default.

## 4. LATE PAYMENT

4.1 If an instalment is not paid when due, a late fee of 0.5% of the overdue amount is charged for each day it remains unpaid. The late fee for each day is added to the overdue amount at the end of that day, and the late fee for the following day is calculated on the new total, so that the late fee compounds daily until the overdue amount and all late fees are paid in full.

4.2 The Lender may also charge a collection fee of RM 150.00 for each reminder letter or call and may increase the rate of interest in Clause 2.1 to 24% a year on the whole of the Loan still owed from the date of the first default.

## 5. EARLY REPAYMENT

5.1 The Borrower may repay the Loan early at any time by giving fourteen (14) days' written notice and paying the Loan still owed, with interest to the date of payment. Interest for the unexpired part of the term is not payable. The Lender shall give a written settlement figure within five (5) working days of a request. No early repayment fee is charged.

## 6. DEFAULT

6.1 If the Borrower misses two (2) consecutive instalments, the Lender may by written notice require immediate payment of the Loan still owed, with interest and any late fees. The notice shall give the Borrower fourteen (14) days to bring the account up to date.

6.2 The Lender may report the default to a credit reporting agency, after giving the Borrower seven (7) days' written warning.

## 7. GUARANTEE

7.1 In consideration of the Lender making the Loan, the Guarantor guarantees to the Lender the payment of everything the Borrower owes under this Agreement and any other debt the Borrower may owe the Lender now or in future.

7.2 The Guarantor shall be liable for all costs, charges and expenses of every kind that the Lender incurs in connection with the Loan, this Agreement or the guarantee, including legal costs on a full indemnity basis, collection costs and the cost of tracing the Borrower, whether or not they relate to the Loan still owed. The Guarantor's liability is not limited to the amount of the Loan.

7.3 The Guarantor's liability continues even if the Lender gives the Borrower more time, changes the terms of the Loan or increases the amount of the Loan, with or without telling the Guarantor. The Lender may demand payment from the Guarantor first, without first asking the Borrower for payment or suing the Borrower.

7.4 The Lender need not tell the Guarantor when the Borrower is in default. The Guarantor waives any right to receive a copy of the statements of the Borrower's account.

7.5 The Guarantor confirms that the Guarantor has read this Agreement and has had the chance to take independent advice.

## 8. INSURANCE

8.1 The Borrower is not required to take out any insurance in connection with the Loan.

## 9. USE OF PERSONAL DATA

9.1 The Lender may use the Borrower's and the Guarantor's personal data to manage the Loan and to comply with the law. The Lender may share it with credit reporting agencies and with its debt collectors, and shall keep it secure in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

## 10. COMPLAINTS AND DISPUTES

10.1 The Borrower may complain to the Lender in writing, and the Lender shall reply within fourteen (14) days. The Borrower and the Guarantor may also complain to the Ministry that licenses moneylenders. Nothing in this Agreement stops either of them from going to court.

10.2 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Malaysia and the courts of Malaysia have jurisdiction.

## 11. GENERAL

11.1 This Agreement may be changed only in writing signed by the Lender and the Borrower. A change that makes the Guarantor's position worse needs the Guarantor's written consent only if the Lender chooses to ask for it.
