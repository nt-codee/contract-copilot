# Roadmap

Contract types are **packs**: a playbook of standard terms (`knowledge/playbook/<type>/`), the relevant
law entries (`knowledge/law/`), and synthetic samples with answer keys (`samples/contracts/`). The review loop,
quote and citation verification, the document reader and the negotiation step are shared, so a new type
is mostly content.

| Stage | Contract types | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Today | NDAs, service agreements, employment and freelance, tenancy, hire purchase | Malaysian and Singapore law; English and Bahasa Malaysia; synthetic samples and answer keys |
| Next: reliability and evidence | Existing five types | Expand output-quality checks and source coverage; durable background review/resume is planned, not currently guaranteed |
| Later: more commercial types | Supplier and distribution agreements, additional lease scenarios | New standard terms, law sources and answer keys before promising coverage |
| Research only | Other jurisdictions and regulated consumer documents | Require source rights, local expertise and a clear boundary between information and professional advice |

Google sign-in and per-company workspaces (roles, invites, an audit trail) are already built. Platform items that come
with scale: licensed access to full judgments (MLJ/CLJ, Supreme Court permission for commercial
reuse); more jurisdictions (Indonesia, Thailand, Philippines, Vietnam), with the BM support pattern extended to their
languages; and integrations with e-signature and document storage tools.
