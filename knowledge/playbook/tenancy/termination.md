# Termination, default and re-entry (tenancy)

clause_type: `termination` · contract_type: `tenancy`

## In plain words
How and when either side can end the tenancy for breach, and what the landlord may do if rent is unpaid. Ending a tenancy normally needs notice, time to fix the breach and, if the tenant stays, a court order for possession.

## What balanced looks like
- A **written notice** of breach describing the breach, with **14 to 30 days** to fix it (14 days for unpaid rent is common).
- Termination for default only for **material** breach (unpaid rent, serious damage, illegal use), not for small breaches.
- If the tenant does not leave, the landlord **applies to court** for possession; the agreement does not give a right to change the locks or remove the tenant's goods.
- Either side can end for the other's **serious** breach, and the tenant may end if the premises become unusable.
- The landlord does not seize or hold the tenant's goods for rent except through the court process (Malaysia: Distress Act 1951; Singapore: Distress Act 1934).
- Rent stops when the tenancy ends and the keys are returned.

## Red flags
- **High:** The landlord may **re-enter, change the locks or remove the tenant's goods** after **a few days' arrears** (for example 7 days), without notice or a court order.
- **High:** Any breach, however small, lets the landlord end the tenancy at once, while the tenant has no matching right.
- **High:** The landlord may **hold or sell the tenant's goods** for arrears without a court warrant.
- **Medium:** Termination is allowed on **less than 14 days'** notice for rent default.
- **Medium:** The tenant stays liable for rent for the remainder of the term after the landlord ends the tenancy, with no duty to re-let.
- **Low:** A reasonable cure period and a notice method (email and registered post).

## Suggested ask
"Please add a written notice of breach with 14 days to remedy, and make clear that if the tenant does not leave the landlord will apply to court for possession rather than re-enter or change the locks."
