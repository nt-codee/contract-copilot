# Automatic renewal of the tenancy (tenancy)

clause_type: `auto_renewal` · contract_type: `tenancy`

## In plain words
Whether the tenancy rolls into a new term unless someone gives notice. This is convenient, but a long renewal period or a long notice window can lock the tenant in.

## What balanced looks like
- The tenancy ends on the end date, with a **renewal on new written terms** or by the tenant's **option** on stated terms and rent.
- If it renews automatically, the renewal is for **the same or a shorter period**, or month to month, with rent increases capped and announced early.
- The **notice to stop renewal is 30 to 60 days**, and the landlord **reminds** the tenant before the deadline.
- After renewal the tenant may still leave on **1 to 2 months' notice**.
- A shop-lot **option to renew** is clear on the rent and the notice date.

## Red flags
- **High:** An **automatic renewal for 2 years or more**, with a notice window of **90 days or more** and notice only by registered post.
- **High:** Renewal at a rent chosen by the landlord (see `rent_review.md`) with no right to leave.
- **Medium:** Only the landlord may decide not to renew.
- **Medium:** Renewal triggered simply by the tenant staying on, at a higher holding-over rent such as double.
- **Low:** A 60-day notice window with a reminder; acceptable but worth diarising.

## Suggested ask
"Please change renewal to 12-month periods with 30 days' notice to stop, and ask the landlord to send a written reminder 60 days before the end of the term."
