# Assignment, subletting and change of landlord (tenancy)

clause_type: `assignment` · contract_type: `tenancy`

## In plain words
Whether the tenant can pass the tenancy to someone else or sublet part of the premises, and what happens if the landlord sells. The tenant needs a workable way to leave and protection if the property changes hands.

## What balanced looks like
- The tenant may **assign or sublet with the landlord's written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld**, particularly in a shop-lot tenancy.
- The landlord's consent is decided in a **reasonable time** (for example 14 days) and any fee is modest and stated.
- If the landlord **sells**, the tenancy and the deposit **continue** with the buyer, and the tenant is told in writing.
- Roommates in a residential room rental are listed, and the tenant can replace one on the landlord's reasonable approval.

## Red flags
- **High:** The tenant may **never** assign, sublet or take in a roommate, while the landlord may transfer the property or the tenancy at will with no protection for the tenant's deposit.
- **Medium:** The landlord can withhold consent for any reason, or charge a large fee for consent.
- **Medium:** For a company tenant, any change in its shareholding is treated as an assignment.
- **Low:** A requirement to give notice of new occupants in a residential let; reasonable if the landlord must respond.

## Suggested ask
"Please allow the tenant to assign or sublet with the landlord's written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, and confirm that the deposit passes to any buyer of the property."
