# Rent, charges and late payment (tenancy)

clause_type: `payment` · contract_type: `tenancy`

## In plain words
How much rent is due, when and how it is paid, what other charges the tenant must pay (service charge, utilities, assessment, SST on commercial rent) and what happens when a payment is late.

## What balanced looks like
- Rent is due **monthly in advance**, usually by the **1st to the 7th** of the month, with the bank account named in the agreement.
- **All other charges** the tenant pays are listed (utilities, service charge, quit rent and assessment if passed on, sales and service tax where it applies to commercial rent).
- **Late interest at a modest rate** (roughly **0.5% to 1% a month**, or a fixed annual rate around 8% or less), starting only after a short grace period and a reminder.
- The landlord gives a **receipt** for each payment.
- Arrears are pursued by notice and, if needed, by court; the agreement does not promise self-help (see `termination.md`).

## Red flags
- **High:** Rent can be increased or extra charges added by the landlord at its **sole discretion** during the term (see `rent_review.md`).
- **High:** Late charges of **more than 2% a month**, or a fixed late fee on top of interest, with immediate default after 1 to 3 days.
- **Medium:** 'Other charges' are not listed, so the tenant cannot tell what it is agreeing to pay.
- **Medium:** The tenant pays property tax, quit rent or the landlord's income tax.
- **Low:** Rent payable by a particular channel only (cash, post-dated cheques); acceptable if receipts are issued.

## Suggested ask
"Please list every charge the tenant pays, set late interest at no more than 1% a month after a 7-day grace period and a written reminder, and confirm a receipt will be given for every payment."
