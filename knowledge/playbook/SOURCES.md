# Baseline sources and licences

The playbook files in this folder are original writing, in plain English, based on general market
practice for NDAs and service agreements between small and mid-sized businesses in Malaysia and
Singapore. No text is copied from any source. They describe what is common, not what any particular
business should do, and they are not legal advice.

Two openly licensed standard forms were consulted for **structure only** (which topics a balanced
NDA or services agreement covers, and in what order). Their licences were checked on 2026-10-18 with
`web_fetch` before use.

| Source | URL checked | Licence found | How it was used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common Paper Mutual NDA v1.0 | https://commonpaper.com/standards/mutual-nda/ | CC BY 4.0 ("Common Paper agreements are free to use and modify under CC BY 4.0") | Topic checklist for the NDA files (fixed vs unlimited confidentiality term; their FAQ notes two years as the most common fixed term) |
| Common Paper Professional Services Agreement v1.1 | https://commonpaper.com/standards/professional-services-agreement/ | CC BY 4.0 (same statement on the page) | Topic checklist for the service agreement files (deliverables, time of IP assignment, acceptance) |
| Bonterms Mutual NDA v1.0 | https://bonterms.com/forms/bonterms-mutual-nda-v1/ and https://github.com/Bonterms/Mutual-NDA | CC BY 4.0 (form footer "Free to use under CC BY 4.0"; repo README says the same) | Topic checklist for the NDA files (exceptions, permitted disclosures, return or destruction, no licence, assignment on merger) |

Not consulted: any real negotiated contract, any law firm template, any paid precedent bank.

Malaysian and Singapore law points mentioned in the files (restraint of trade, how courts treat
fixed damages sums, the two Personal Data Protection Acts, AIAC and SIAC) are stated at a general
level from common knowledge and should be checked by a lawyer before anyone relies on them.
