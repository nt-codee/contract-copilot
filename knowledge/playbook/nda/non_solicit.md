# Non-solicitation of staff (NDA)

clause_type: `non_solicit` · contract_type: `nda`

## In plain words
A promise not to approach the other side's employees to hire them away. It is common in NDAs and
usually acceptable when it is narrow.

## What balanced looks like
- **Mutual**: both sides make the same promise.
- **Short**: 6 to 12 months after the NDA ends.
- **Narrow**: covers only employees the other side actually met or worked with during the
  discussions.
- **Carve-out for general hiring**: job adverts and recruiters not aimed at the other side's staff do
  not count, and neither does hiring someone who applies on their own initiative.

## Red flags
- **Medium:** one-way, protecting only the discloser.
- **Medium:** lasting 24 months or longer, or covering all employees of all group companies.
- **Medium:** extending to customers, suppliers or "business partners" (this starts to look like a
  non-compete; see `non_compete.md`).
- **Low:** no carve-out for general job adverts.

## Suggested ask
"Please make the non-solicitation mutual, limit it to 12 months and to staff involved in our
discussions, and exclude general job adverts and unsolicited applications."
