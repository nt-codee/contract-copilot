# Liability and remedies, including fixed damages (NDA)

clause_type: `liability` · contract_type: `nda`

## In plain words
What happens if someone breaks the NDA: what they must pay, and what the other side can ask a court
to do.

## What balanced looks like
- **Ordinary remedies**: the injured side can claim the loss it can actually prove.
- **Injunction**: both sides accept that a court may order a stop to a breach quickly, because money
  may not fix leaked secrets. This is standard and fair.
- **No fixed damages sum.** Most standard NDAs do not set a pre-agreed amount payable per breach.
- **The same rules for both sides.**
- Often no cap on liability for misuse of confidential information. That is normal in an NDA because
  each side controls its own risk by not leaking.

## Background (general, not advice)
- A fixed sum payable on breach is called **liquidated damages**. In **Malaysia**, section 75 of the
  Contracts Act 1950 lets a court award reasonable compensation up to the stated sum, not
  automatically the whole sum. In **Singapore**, a sum that is a punishment rather than a genuine
  pre-estimate of loss is generally unenforceable (the penalty rule).
- Even so, a large stated sum shifts negotiating power and may be enforceable under a foreign law.

## Red flags
- **High:** a fixed sum payable "for each breach" with no overall limit, or a sum far above any
  realistic loss.
- **High:** the receiver must pay the discloser's legal costs on an indemnity basis, plus damages,
  for any alleged breach, without the discloser having to prove loss.
- **High:** the receiver's liability for misusing or leaking the information is excluded altogether,
  while the discloser still gives indemnities. The NDA then gives no remedy for its own breach. In
  Malaysia a clause that leaves no remedy at all can be void under Contracts Act section 29 (library
  entry `my-case-cimb-v-bourke`), but nobody should have to litigate that to be protected.
- **Medium:** only one side is liable; the other side's breaches have no consequence.
- **Medium:** the receiver indemnifies the discloser for "all losses" connected with the NDA.

## Suggested ask
"Please remove the liquidated damages clause and rely on ordinary damages and injunctions, which
apply equally to both parties."
