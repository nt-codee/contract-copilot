# Dispute resolution: courts, mediation or arbitration (NDA)

clause_type: `dispute_resolution` · contract_type: `nda`

## In plain words
How a disagreement gets settled: talking first, then a mediator, then a court or an arbitrator
(a private judge whose decision is binding).

## What balanced looks like
- A short step of **good-faith talks** between senior people, for example 14 to 30 days.
- Then **courts** in the governing-law country, or **arbitration** in the region: the Asian
  International Arbitration Centre (**AIAC**, Kuala Lumpur) or the Singapore International
  Arbitration Centre (**SIAC**). Mediation centres in both cities are a cheaper first step.
- A single arbitrator for small claims, to keep cost down.
- Either side may still go to court for an urgent injunction.
- Each side pays its own costs unless the tribunal or court decides otherwise.

## Red flags
- **High:** arbitration or courts in a distant city with rules that make even a small claim cost more
  than it is worth.
- **Medium:** only one side may choose between court and arbitration.
- **Medium:** the receiver must pay the discloser's legal costs whatever the outcome.
- **Medium:** the receiver waives its right to go to court or to appeal, while the discloser keeps it.
- **Low:** no dispute clause at all; the courts of the governing-law country then apply by default.

## Suggested ask
"Please provide for talks first, then mediation or arbitration in Kuala Lumpur (AIAC) or Singapore
(SIAC) with one arbitrator, and let each party bear its own costs."
