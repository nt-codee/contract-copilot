# Confidentiality: what counts as secret, and the exceptions (NDA)

clause_type: `confidentiality` · contract_type: `nda`

## In plain words
This is the heart of an NDA. It says what information is protected, what the receiving side may do
with it, who else may see it, and which information is never treated as secret.

## What balanced looks like
- **Mutual** when both sides will share information. A one-way NDA is fine only when one side
  genuinely shares nothing.
- **A clear definition.** Information counts as confidential if it is marked confidential, or if a
  sensible person would understand it to be confidential from its nature.
- **Use limited to the purpose.** The receiver may use the information only to evaluate or carry out
  the stated deal.
- **Need-to-know sharing.** The receiver may share with its staff, advisers and contractors who need
  it for the purpose and who are bound by similar confidentiality duties.
- **Reasonable care.** Protect it at least as carefully as your own similar information.
- **The standard exceptions ("carve-outs").** The duty does not cover information that:
  1. is or becomes public without the receiver's fault;
  2. the receiver already knew before it was disclosed;
  3. the receiver gets lawfully from someone else who owed no duty of secrecy;
  4. the receiver develops independently, without using the disclosed information.
- **Compelled disclosure.** The receiver may disclose if a law, regulator or court requires it,
  after telling the discloser first where allowed, so the discloser can object.

## Red flags
- **High:** no carve-outs at all, or wording that the duty applies "without exception".
- **High:** a definition covering "all information of any kind", including information the receiver
  already had or that is public.
- **High:** no right to disclose when the law requires it, which can put the receiver in breach of
  either the contract or the law.
- **Medium:** one-way obligations when both sides will in fact share information.
- **Medium:** the receiver may not share even with its own lawyers, accountants or staff.
- **Low:** oral disclosures must be confirmed in writing within a short window (common, but note it).

## Suggested ask
"Please make the obligations mutual, limit confidential information to what is marked or obviously
confidential, and add the four standard exceptions plus a right to disclose where the law requires."
