# Termination, and return or destruction of information (NDA)

clause_type: `termination` · contract_type: `nda`

## In plain words
How either side can end the NDA, and what happens to the shared information when it ends.

## What balanced looks like
- **Either side may end** the NDA by written notice at any time. Ending it stops new disclosures; the
  secrecy duty for information already shared continues for the agreed period.
- **On request or on termination**, the receiver returns or destroys the information and, if asked,
  confirms this in writing.
- **Practical exception:** copies kept in automatic backups, or kept because a law requires it, need
  not be hunted down, but they stay confidential.
- No fee or penalty for walking away from the discussions.

## Red flags
- **High:** only the discloser may end the NDA, while the receiver stays bound with no end date.
- **Medium:** the receiver must certify destruction of every copy, including backups, within a very
  short time (for example 48 hours).
- **Medium:** walking away from the deal triggers a payment or a "break fee".
- **Low:** no written confirmation process; usually harmless.

## Suggested ask
"Please let either party end the NDA by written notice, and allow copies held in routine backups or
kept by law to be retained, provided they stay confidential."
