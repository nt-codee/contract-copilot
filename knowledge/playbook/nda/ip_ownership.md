# Ownership of ideas and no licence (NDA)

clause_type: `ip_ownership` · contract_type: `nda`

## In plain words
Who owns the information and any ideas that come up during the discussions. ("IP" means intellectual
property: inventions, designs, software, brands and know-how.)

## What balanced looks like
- Each side **keeps ownership** of what it discloses. Sharing it gives no licence (permission) to use
  it beyond the purpose.
- Each side **keeps the right to develop its own products**, including similar ones, provided it does
  not misuse the other's information.
- No transfer of ownership in anything either side creates on its own.
- Feedback or suggestions given in passing are either left out, or may be used freely by the side
  that receives them, without passing ownership of the giver's own IP.

## Red flags
- **High:** anything the receiver creates, improves or "derives" during the discussions becomes the
  discloser's property.
- **High:** a "residuals" clause that lets the receiver freely use whatever its staff remember. This
  quietly undoes the NDA for the discloser.
- **Medium:** the receiver must assign ownership of feedback, suggestions or improvements to the
  discloser.
- **Low:** silence on ownership; the default (each keeps its own) usually applies.

## Suggested ask
"Please confirm that each party keeps ownership of its own information and anything it develops
independently, and remove any transfer of ownership of improvements or feedback."
