# Assignment: passing the contract to someone else (NDA)

clause_type: `assignment` · contract_type: `nda`

## In plain words
Whether a party may transfer its rights or duties under the NDA to another company, for example a
buyer of its business.

## What balanced looks like
- **Neither side** may assign without the other's written consent.
- **Exception:** either side may assign to a company that takes over all or most of its business in a
  merger or sale, without needing consent.
- Consent is not to be unreasonably withheld.

## Red flags
- **Medium:** the discloser may assign to anyone, including a competitor of the receiver, while the
  receiver may never assign.
- **Medium:** the discloser may share the receiver's information across its whole group of companies
  without the same duties applying to each of them.
- **Low:** no assignment clause; usually harmless in an NDA.

## Suggested ask
"Please make the assignment rule mutual: no assignment without consent, except to a successor in a
merger or sale of the business."
