# Dispute resolution (service agreement)

clause_type: `dispute_resolution` · contract_type: `service_agreement`

## In plain words
The steps for settling a disagreement before it becomes a costly case.

## What balanced looks like
- **Escalation:** the project managers talk first, then senior managers, within fixed short periods
  (for example 10 business days each).
- **Mediation** at a regional centre (for example the AIAC in Kuala Lumpur or the Singapore
  International Mediation Centre) if talks fail; it is quick and cheap.
- Then **courts** of the governing-law country, or **arbitration** at the AIAC or SIAC with one
  arbitrator for smaller disputes.
- **Service continues** during a dispute; the vendor may not suspend while a good-faith dispute is
  being worked through, provided undisputed amounts are paid.
- Either side may seek urgent relief from a court at any time.

## Red flags
- **High:** the vendor may suspend the whole service during any dispute.
- **High:** arbitration in a distant city with three arbitrators for any claim, making small claims
  pointless.
- **Medium:** only the vendor may start court proceedings; the customer must arbitrate.
- **Medium:** the losing side always pays the winner's costs on a full indemnity basis, which deters
  the smaller side from raising genuine complaints.
- **Low:** no escalation step; ask for one.

## Suggested ask
"Please add an escalation and mediation step before any proceedings, keep the service running
during a good-faith dispute, and use courts or arbitration in Kuala Lumpur or Singapore."
