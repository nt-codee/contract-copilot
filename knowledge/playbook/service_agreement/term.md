# Term: how long the agreement runs (service agreement)

clause_type: `term` · contract_type: `service_agreement`

## In plain words
The start date, the first fixed period (the "initial term") and what happens at the end of it.
Renewal is covered separately in `auto_renewal.md`.

## What balanced looks like
- An initial term of **12 months** for subscriptions and ongoing services; up to **24-36 months** only
  when the customer gets a real price discount in return.
- Project work ends when the project is delivered and accepted, or on a stated end date.
- The start date is clear, and paid service starts only when the service is actually available
  ("go-live"), not on signature months earlier.
- Clauses that should outlive the contract (confidentiality, payment of amounts already due,
  liability) are listed as surviving.

## Red flags
- **High:** a long initial term (3 years or more) that the customer cannot leave early, with all fees
  for the whole term payable even if the service is poor.
- **Medium:** fees start on signature although set-up takes months.
- **Medium:** the vendor may extend the term on its own by issuing a new order or "service schedule".
- **Low:** no stated end date for project work; ask for one.

## Suggested ask
"Please set a 12-month initial term starting on go-live, and confirm the customer may end it early
as set out in the termination clause."
