# Auto-renewal and price increases on renewal (service agreement)

clause_type: `auto_renewal` · contract_type: `service_agreement`

## In plain words
An auto-renewal clause rolls the contract into a new period unless someone gives notice in time. It
is convenient, but a long notice window or a long renewal period can lock a business in by accident.
Price rises are covered in `payment.md`; a rise tied to renewal is flagged here as well.

## What balanced looks like
- Renewal for **the same or a shorter period**, usually 12 months or month-to-month.
- **Notice to stop renewal of 30 days** before the end date (60 days at most).
- The vendor **sends a reminder** 30-60 days before the notice deadline.
- After renewal, the customer may still leave on reasonable notice, or renewal is month-to-month.
- Any price change on renewal is announced at least 60 days ahead and capped (see `payment.md`).

## Red flags
- **High:** a notice window of **90 days or more**, especially combined with a renewal period longer
  than the original term (for example 24 months).
- **High:** renewal plus an automatic price increase well above inflation.
- **Medium:** notice must be given by a special method (registered post only, a named person only)
  that is easy to get wrong.
- **Medium:** only the vendor may decide not to renew.
- **Low:** 60-day notice window with a reminder; acceptable but worth diarising.

## Suggested ask
"Please change renewal to 12-month periods with 30 days' notice to stop renewal, and have the vendor
send a written reminder 60 days before the renewal date."
