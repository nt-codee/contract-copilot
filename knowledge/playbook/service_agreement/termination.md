# Termination: how either side can end the agreement (service agreement)

clause_type: `termination` · contract_type: `service_agreement`

## In plain words
When and how the contract can be ended before its natural end, and what each side owes afterwards.

## What balanced looks like
- **For cause, both sides:** either side may end it if the other commits a serious breach and does
  not fix it within **30 days** of written notice, or becomes insolvent.
- **For convenience:** the customer (and often both sides) may end it without a reason on **30 to 90
  days'** notice, paying for work done and committed costs up to that date.
- **The same notice periods for both sides**, or a longer period for the vendor (so the customer has
  time to switch).
- **Exit help:** the customer gets its data back in a usable format within about 30 days, and
  reasonable transition help at normal rates.
- Prepaid fees for services not yet delivered are refunded when the vendor is at fault.

## Red flags
- **High:** the customer may not end early for any reason, or must pay all remaining fees for the
  whole term as an "early termination fee".
- **Medium:** the vendor may end on very short notice (for example 7 days) without cause, while the
  customer needs months.
- **Medium:** no cure period: one minor late payment lets the vendor end the contract at once.
- **Medium:** the customer's data is deleted on termination with no export window.
- **Low:** no exit-assistance wording; ask for a data export at minimum.

## Suggested ask
"Please give both parties the same notice period, allow 30 days to fix a breach, and add a 30-day
window to export our data on termination."
