# Confidentiality (service agreement)

clause_type: `confidentiality` · contract_type: `service_agreement`

## In plain words
Both sides will see each other's business information while the service runs. This clause keeps it
secret and limits how it is used.

## What balanced looks like
- **Mutual** obligations.
- Confidential information is what is marked, or obviously, confidential, and includes the
  customer's data and the contract's pricing.
- Use only to perform the agreement; share only with staff and subcontractors who need it and are
  bound by similar duties.
- The standard exceptions: public information, already known, received lawfully from a third party,
  independently developed, and disclosure required by law with prior notice where allowed.
- Lasts for the contract plus **2 to 5 years**, and for trade secrets and personal data as long as
  they remain so.

## Red flags
- **High:** only the customer is bound, while the vendor holds the customer's business data with no
  duty of secrecy.
- **Medium:** the vendor may publicise the customer as a reference, or publish case studies, without
  consent.
- **Medium:** no exceptions, or no right to disclose where the law requires.
- **Low:** a 1-year survival period; short, but not dangerous for ordinary information.

## Suggested ask
"Please make the confidentiality obligations mutual, with the standard exceptions, lasting at least
three years after the agreement ends, and require our consent before naming us publicly."
