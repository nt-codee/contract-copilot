# Ownership of work product and IP (service agreement)

clause_type: `ip_ownership` · contract_type: `service_agreement`

## In plain words
Who owns what is created under the contract. ("IP", intellectual property, covers software code,
designs, documents, data models and brands.)

## What balanced looks like
- **Custom deliverables belong to the customer** once paid for: work made specifically for the
  customer (a custom app, a design, a report) transfers to the customer on payment.
- **The vendor keeps its existing tools and platform** ("background IP") and general know-how, and
  gives the customer a **licence** (permission) to use whatever is built into the deliverables, for as
  long as the customer uses them.
- **The customer keeps all its own data** and content. The vendor may use it only to provide the
  service.
- Source code for custom software is handed over, or held in escrow, if the customer will need to
  maintain it.

## Red flags
- **High:** the vendor owns custom work the customer paid to have built, and the customer gets only a
  licence that ends when the contract ends.
- **High:** the vendor owns or may freely reuse the customer's data, or anything "derived" from it.
- **Medium:** ownership passes only after every fee under the whole agreement is paid, not per
  deliverable.
- **Medium:** no source code delivery for custom software the customer depends on.
- **Low:** the vendor may reuse general know-how and non-customer-specific components; normal.

## Suggested ask
"Please confirm that all deliverables created specifically for us belong to us on payment, with a
continuing licence to any vendor tools built into them, and that our data remains ours."
