# Leave (employment)

clause_type: `leave` · contract_type: `employment`

## In plain words
How much paid time off you get: annual leave, sick leave, hospitalisation, maternity and paternity
leave, and what happens to unused days.

## What balanced looks like
- Every type of leave **at or above the statutory floor**, stated in days.
- Annual leave rising with years of service; a fair rule for carrying unused days forward or being
  paid for them on exit.
- Sick leave with a medical certificate, plus separate hospitalisation leave.
- Maternity and paternity leave at least as the law requires, with pay.

## Background (general, not advice)
- In **Malaysia**, the Employment Act 1955 sets minimums: annual leave of 8, 12 or 16 days (under 2
  years, 2 to under 5 years, 5 years and more); sick leave of 14, 18 or 22 days on the same bands,
  not counting hospitalisation; **98 days' maternity leave**; and **7 consecutive days' paid
  paternity leave** for a married male employee with 12 months' service.
- In **Singapore**, the Employment Act and related laws set baseline leave for many employees.

## Red flags
- **High:** leave below the statutory minimum, or "leave at management's discretion".
- **High:** no maternity or paternity leave, or unpaid only.
- **Medium:** unused annual leave forfeited with no chance to take it.
- **Medium:** sick leave counted against annual leave.
- **Low:** leave entitlement set out only in a handbook you have not seen.

## Suggested ask
"Please state my annual, sick, hospitalisation, maternity and paternity leave in days, each at or
above the statutory minimum, and attach the leave policy."
