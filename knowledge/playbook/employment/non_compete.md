# Non-compete after you leave (employment)

clause_type: `non_compete` · contract_type: `employment`

## In plain words
A non-compete stops you working for a competitor, or setting up your own business, for a period after
you leave. It can block you from earning a living in your own field.

## What balanced looks like
- **Best: no post-employment non-compete.** Confidentiality and non-solicitation clauses already
  protect the employer's secrets and clients.
- If one is truly needed: limited to **named competitors or a specific product**, a short period
  (**6-12 months at most**), a narrow area, and ideally **paid garden leave** for that time.

## Background (general, not advice)
- This is general information, not legal advice.
- In **Malaysia**, section 28 of the Contracts Act 1950 makes an agreement restraining a lawful
  profession, trade or business void to that extent, so a non-compete after employment is likely
  void.
- In **Singapore**, a restraint is enforced only if it protects a legitimate interest (such as trade
  secrets or client connections) and goes no further than necessary in time, area and activity
  (*Man Financial v Wong* [2007] SGCA 53). A South-East Asia-wide ban was held too wide for an
  employee whose work was only in Singapore (*MoneySmart Singapore v Artem Musienko* [2024] SGHC 94). A
  restraint with no time limit was held void, and the court did not rewrite it to add one (*Smile Inc Dental Surgeons
  v Lui Andrew Stewart* [2012] SGCA 39).
- A void clause can still deter you or cost money to fight.

## Red flags
- **High:** 2 years or more, or across "Southeast Asia" or the whole industry.
- **High:** no time limit at all.
- **Medium:** covers any business "similar to" the employer's, not named competitors.
- **Low:** a short, narrow restraint paid as garden leave.

## Suggested ask
"Please remove the non-compete, or limit it to named direct competitors for no more than 6 months,
with my salary paid as garden leave for that period."
