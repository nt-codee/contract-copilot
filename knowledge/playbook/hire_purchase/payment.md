# Payment, deposit and how payments are applied (hire purchase)

clause_type: `payment` · contract_type: `hire_purchase`

## In plain words
How much you pay up front and each month, where and how to pay, and what the lender does with each payment.

## What balanced looks like
- **A clear deposit** that is shown as paid, with a receipt. Booking fees, if any, are small and refundable on the terms the law sets.
- **Payment only to the lender or a named, authorised collector**, with a receipt for every payment.
- **Payments applied in a stated order** (late interest, then arrears, then current instalment), and shown on a statement on request.
- **Late payment** triggers a reminder first, and the late charge is simple interest on the late amount only.

## Red flags
- **High:** payments can be demanded in cash to the dealer or a collector with no receipt or a collector not named in the agreement.
- **Medium:** the lender may apply your payments to fees and insurance first, so you stay in arrears.
- **Medium:** extra charges in the agreement that are not on the list of permitted payments in the statute.
- **Medium:** the deposit is taken but the car is not delivered by a fixed date, and there is no refund wording.
- **Low:** payment by standing instruction only, with no right to pay another way.

## Suggested ask
"Please confirm all payments are made to the lender (or a named authorised agent) against a receipt, and that payments are applied to the current instalment before fees."
