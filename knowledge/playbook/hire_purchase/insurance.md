# Insurance tie-in (hire purchase)

clause_type: `insurance` · contract_type: `hire_purchase`

## In plain words
Who arranges the car's insurance, who pays, and whether you must use the lender's or dealer's insurer.

## What balanced looks like
- **You may choose your insurer**, as long as the cover meets a stated minimum (comprehensive cover, the lender noted as interested party). In Malaysia the owner cannot require a particular insurer (s 26(3)).
- **The premium is shown separately** in the agreement and in the total amount payable.
- **A refund of unused premium** if you settle early or cancel the policy.
- **Commissions and rebates** from the insurer pass to you where the law says so.
- **Renewal** is your decision, with a reminder from the lender and a right to renew elsewhere.

## Red flags
- **High:** you must insure through the dealer's or lender's named insurer for the whole term, with no right to switch.
- **Medium:** the premium is much higher than the open-market price and is not shown separately.
- **Medium:** no refund of premium on early settlement.
- **Medium:** the lender may force-place insurance and add the cost to your balance without warning.
- **Low:** cover amount (agreed value) is not stated.

## Suggested ask
"Please confirm I may arrange comprehensive insurance with an insurer of my choice, show the premium separately, and refund the unused premium if I settle early."
