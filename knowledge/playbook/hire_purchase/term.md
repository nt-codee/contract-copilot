# Term and instalment schedule (hire purchase)

clause_type: `term` · contract_type: `hire_purchase`

## In plain words
How long the agreement runs, how many instalments, and when they fall due.

## What balanced looks like
- **Tenure stated in months**, with the number of instalments, the amount of each, and the due date every month.
- **A payment schedule** (or a clear way to get one) showing, for each instalment, how much is interest and how much is principal.
- **A tenure that matches the car's age and your plans**: a used car on a very long tenure can leave you owing more than the car is worth.
- **Ownership passes to you** when the last instalment and any lawful sums are paid, and the lender releases its interest in the registration.

## Red flags
- **Medium:** the instalment amount or number of instalments is left blank, or says 'as notified by the Owner'.
- **Medium:** no schedule, and no statement of the total amount payable over the whole term.
- **Medium:** a balloon (large last) payment that is not shown on the first page.
- **Low:** the due date is not a fixed day of the month.

## Suggested ask
"Please attach the full repayment schedule, state the total amount payable over the term, and confirm there is no balloon payment."
