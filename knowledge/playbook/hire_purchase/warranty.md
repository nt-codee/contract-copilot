# Condition of the goods and the dealer's promises (hire purchase)

clause_type: `warranty` · contract_type: `hire_purchase`

## In plain words
What you are promised about the car, and who is responsible when it turns out to be faulty.

## What balanced looks like
- **The car matches the description**, with its registration, mileage and any prior accident or modification disclosed in writing.
- **A fair warranty** from the dealer or manufacturer, and clarity on who to call.
- **Implied terms stay**: the owner stands behind the car being the owner's to hire and fit for its purpose, and cannot hide behind the dealer.
- **Misrepresentation by the dealer** is the owner's responsibility when the dealer arranged the deal.
- **Right to inspect** or have a test drive before signing.

## Red flags
- **High:** 'sold as seen, no warranty', with the owner and dealer both disclaiming all responsibility.
- **High:** the owner is not liable for what the dealer said, even though the dealer arranged the finance.
- **Medium:** you must keep paying even if the car is not delivered or is unroadworthy.
- **Medium:** the car's history (accident, flood, odometer) is not disclosed.
- **Low:** warranty period is short for a used car.

## Suggested ask
"Please confirm in writing the car's condition, mileage and accident history, and that the finance company is responsible for the dealer's statements made while arranging this agreement."
