# Early settlement and early termination (hire purchase and personal loans)

clause_type: `early_termination` · contract_type: `hire_purchase`

## In plain words
Your right to pay off the debt early or to hand the car back, and what that costs.

## What balanced looks like
- **A right to settle early at any time**, on written notice, paying the outstanding balance with a fair rebate of future interest.
- **A settlement figure within a few working days** of asking, showing how it was worked out.
- **A right to end the hire purchase by returning the goods** (in Malaysia, under the Hire-Purchase Act), with the goods valued fairly and the difference settled.
- **No separate early-settlement penalty** on top of the rebate calculation, or only a small, stated one that the law allows.
- **Singapore:** the Hire-Purchase Act 1969 also gives hirers a right to settle early with a rebate. Check the Act for the formula.

## Red flags
- **High:** 'no rebate' on early settlement, or a promise that all interest for the whole term is payable whatever the settlement date.
- **High:** a clause that takes away the right to hand the goods back, or makes you pay more on ending than the statute allows (void in Malaysia under s 34 of the Act).
- **Medium:** the Rule of 78 or a similar method that front-loads interest, so early settlement saves little. (Malaysia moved away from this for new agreements from 1 June 2026.)
- **Medium:** a large 'early termination fee' stated as a percentage of the balance.
- **Low:** settlement requires notice by registered post only.

## Suggested ask
"Please confirm I may settle early at any time with a fair rebate of unearned interest, give me the settlement formula in the agreement, and delete the no-rebate wording."
