# Disputes and complaints (hire purchase and personal loans)

clause_type: `dispute_resolution` · contract_type: `hire_purchase`

## In plain words
Where and how a dispute is settled, and your route to complain.

## What balanced looks like
- **Courts of your home country**, in the court that is practical for you (in Malaysia, the Magistrate's Court or the Tribunal for Consumer Claims where it applies).
- **A complaints route** first: the lender's complaints office, then a regulator or an independent scheme (in Malaysia, Bank Negara Malaysia's complaints channels and the Ministry's Hire-Purchase Controller; in Singapore, the Financial Industry Disputes Resolution Centre for regulated lenders).
- **No clause stopping you from going to court** or limiting the time to complain.
- **Each side pays its own costs** unless the court orders otherwise.

## Red flags
- **High:** compulsory arbitration or a foreign court that is costly for an individual.
- **Medium:** the lender chooses the forum, the law, or the lawyers at its option.
- **Medium:** a very short period to make any claim against the lender.
- **Low:** notices of dispute must be in a fixed form.

## Suggested ask
"Please let disputes be heard in the courts of the country whose law governs this agreement, delete the time limit for bringing a claim, and confirm that nothing in the agreement stops me from using the lender's complaints process or a regulator's complaints channel."
