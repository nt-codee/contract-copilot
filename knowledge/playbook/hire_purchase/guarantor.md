# Guarantor's liability (hire purchase and personal loans)

clause_type: `guarantor` · contract_type: `hire_purchase`

## In plain words
A guarantor promises to pay if the borrower does not. The question is how far that promise goes.

## What balanced looks like
- **The guarantor owes no more than the borrower does.** In Malaysia a hire-purchase guarantor cannot be made liable beyond the hirer (s 21).
- **A stated cap**: the guarantee is limited to the balance of this agreement, not 'all sums, present and future'.
- **Notice**: the lender tells the guarantor when the borrower is in arrears and before it takes steps against the guarantor.
- **The right to pay off and take over**: the guarantor may settle the debt and then claim against the borrower.
- **Independent advice**: the guarantor is told to read the agreement and may take advice. Larger Malaysian hire-purchase guarantees need signing before a Magistrate, Commissioner for Oaths or Notary Public (s 22).

## Red flags
- **High:** the guarantor is liable for 'all costs, charges and expenses' without limit, including the lender's legal costs on a full indemnity basis.
- **High:** the guarantee covers other debts or other goods the borrower may owe, now or later.
- **High:** the guarantor stays liable even if the lender changes the terms or gives the borrower more time, without the guarantor agreeing.
- **Medium:** the lender may go after the guarantor first, without asking the borrower.
- **Medium:** no notice of default to the guarantor.
- **Low:** a guarantor's address for notices is not recorded.

## Suggested ask
"Please limit the guarantor's liability to the outstanding balance under this agreement, cap legal costs to those reasonably incurred, give the guarantor written notice of any default, and confirm the guarantee ends if the terms are changed without the guarantor's written consent."
