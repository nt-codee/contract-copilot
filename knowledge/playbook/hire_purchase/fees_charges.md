# Fees, charges and costs (hire purchase and personal loans)

clause_type: `fees_charges` · contract_type: `hire_purchase`

## In plain words
Every charge other than the instalment itself: processing, stamp duty, admin, late, collection, legal and repossession costs.

## What balanced looks like
- **One list of all fees** with amounts, so the total cost is known before signing.
- **Only fees the law allows.** In Malaysia, a hire-purchase owner or dealer may collect only the payments the Act permits.
- **Late fees are modest and not stacked**: one late charge per late instalment, not a daily fee on top of interest on top of a penalty.
- **Legal and recovery costs** only if actually and reasonably incurred, and not for hirers who return the goods within the notice period.
- **No fee for settling early** beyond what the law allows.

## Red flags
- **High:** a late fee that compounds daily, or a 'penalty' stated as a percentage of the whole loan.
- **High:** 'all costs on a full indemnity basis' with no cap, or costs charged to a guarantor beyond what the hirer owes.
- **Medium:** fees are described as 'as determined by the Owner from time to time'.
- **Medium:** processing or admin fees deducted from the loan amount before you receive it, so you pay interest on money you never got.
- **Low:** a small fee for a statement or for a settlement figure.

## Suggested ask
"Please list every fee and charge with its amount, remove the daily compounding late fee in favour of simple interest on the overdue amount, and limit legal costs to those actually and reasonably incurred."
