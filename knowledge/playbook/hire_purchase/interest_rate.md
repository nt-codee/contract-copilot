# Interest rate and the true cost of financing (hire purchase and personal loans)

clause_type: `interest_rate` · contract_type: `hire_purchase`

## In plain words
The price of the credit: how the interest is worked out and what it really costs per year.

## What balanced looks like
- **The effective interest rate (EIR) is stated**, worked out on the reducing balance (interest charged on what you still owe), not only a 'flat' rate on the original amount.
- **The total interest and the total amount payable are shown in ringgit or dollars**, so you can check them against the rate.
- **Variable rate:** tied to a published reference, with notice before any change and a right to see how it was worked out.
- **Default interest** is a modest simple rate on the late amount only, with no compounding.
- **Personal loans from a licensed moneylender in Malaysia** stay within the Moneylenders Act caps (12% a year secured, 18% unsecured), with no compound interest. Singapore licensed moneylenders are also capped by the Moneylenders Act 2008; check the current caps.
- **Malaysia hire purchase:** since 1 June 2026 the amended Hire-Purchase Act moves new agreements to EIR on a reducing balance (with a transition period to 31 March 2027 for providers). A flat-rate-only quote on a new agreement is worth challenging.

## Red flags
- **High:** the agreement shows only a 'flat rate' (for example 2.8% a year) and no EIR. A flat rate on a 5-7 year loan can be roughly double the true yearly cost.
- **High:** interest that compounds, or a rate that rises because you are late (for a licensed Malaysian moneylender this is illegal beyond a simple 8% a year default charge).
- **High:** a stated rate above the statutory cap for the lender type, or fees that push the real yearly cost over the cap.
- **Medium:** the rate or the lender's 'base' rate can be changed by the lender alone at any time.
- **Medium:** interest calculated on the full original amount even after you have paid part of it.
- **Low:** rounding or day-count wording is unclear.

## Suggested ask
"Please state the effective interest rate on a reducing balance, the total interest and the total amount payable, and confirm that late payment attracts simple interest on the overdue amount only, with no compounding."
