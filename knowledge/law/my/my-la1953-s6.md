## Text

"6. (1) Save as hereinafter provided the following actions shall not be brought after the expiration of six years from the date on which the cause of action accrued, that is to say— (a) actions founded on a contract or on tort; (b) actions to enforce a recognisance; (c) actions to enforce an award; (d) actions to recover any sum recoverable by virtue of any written law other than a penalty or forfeiture or of a sum by way of penalty or forfeiture."

## In plain words

In Malaysia, a claim for breach of contract must normally be started within six years from when the claim arose, usually the date of the breach. After that, the claim is time-barred.

## When it matters in an NDA or service agreement

This is the baseline against which to judge a vendor clause such as "any claim must be brought within 12 months". Under Malaysian law a contract term that shortens the time to enforce rights is void to that extent (my-ca1950-s29), so the six-year period normally still applies. Confidentiality breaches are often discovered late, which makes the full period valuable.
