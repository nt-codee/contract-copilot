## Text

Para 5(1), rates table from 1 August 2025 (extracted text, not a quotation): monthly RM1,700, hourly RM8.72, daily RM65.38 / RM78.46 / RM98.08 for a 6 / 5 / 4 day working week.

Para 5(2) (piece-rate and commission workers, from 1 August 2025): 

"(2) In relation to an employee who is not paid basic wages but is paid wages based only on piece rate, tonnage, task, trip or commission, the rate of monthly wages payable to that employee with effect from 1 August 2025 shall not be less than RM1,700.00."

Para 2: "This Order shall not apply to a domestic servant as defined under subsection 2(1) of the Employment Act 1955 [Act 265]" (the paragraph continues with the Sabah and Sarawak labour ordinances).

Para 6: "The Minimum Wages Order 2022 [P.U. (A) 140/2022] is revoked."

## In plain words

The national minimum wage is RM1,700 a month, or RM8.72 an hour, set under the Minimum Wages Order 2024. It applied from 1 February 2025 to employers with five or more employees and to employers doing professional activities, and from 1 August 2025 to all remaining employers (the interim rate for small employers was RM1,500). Someone paid only by piece rate, task, trip or commission is also guaranteed a monthly floor of RM1,700. Domestic workers are not covered by this Order.

## When it matters in an NDA or service agreement

Check the salary or fee in any Malaysian employment contract, and in a 'freelance' contract that is really employment (see the s 101C entry), against RM1,700 a month, and ask whether commission-only pay is allowed for the role. A salary that falls below the floor after unlawful deductions is a particular risk. The rate may change if the Order is replaced, so confirm the current figure.
