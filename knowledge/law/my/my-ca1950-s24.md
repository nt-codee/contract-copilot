## Text

"24. The consideration or object of an agreement is lawful, unless— (a) it is forbidden by a law; (b) it is of such a nature that, if permitted, it would defeat any law; (c) it is fraudulent; (d) it involves or implies injury to the person or property of another; or (e) the court regards it as immoral, or opposed to public policy.

In each of the above cases, the consideration or object of an agreement is said to be unlawful. Every agreement of which the object or consideration is unlawful is void."

## In plain words

A contract is void if what it is for, or what is being paid for, is illegal, fraudulent, harms someone, or goes against public policy. A void agreement cannot be enforced by either side.

## When it matters in an NDA or service agreement

Rarely the main issue, but it is the backstop when a clause asks a party to do something unlawful, for example an NDA term that forbids reporting wrongdoing to a regulator, or a services scope that would breach licensing law. Flag such terms as void or unenforceable rather than merely unfavourable.
