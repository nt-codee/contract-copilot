## Text

"30. Agreements, the meaning of which is not certain, or capable of being made certain, are void."

## In plain words

If nobody can work out what a promise actually means, and the contract gives no way of working it out, that promise is void. Vague words can leave you with nothing to enforce.

## When it matters in an NDA or service agreement

Watch scope, service levels and fees described only as "to be agreed", "reasonable efforts as determined by the vendor", or with no measurable standard. A promise you cannot pin down may be unenforceable when you need it; ask for a definition, a schedule or a formula that makes it certain.
