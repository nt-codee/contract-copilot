## Text

"26.—(1) No deduction is to be made by an employer from the salary of an employee, unless the deduction is authorised by or under any provision of this Act or is required to be made — (a) by order of a court or other authority competent to make such order;"

## In plain words

An employer may not deduct anything from an employee's salary unless the deduction is authorised by the Act or is required, for example, by a court order or a tax authority declaration. Paragraph (b) onwards list further required deductions and are not quoted here.

## When it matters in an NDA or service agreement

A contract that says the employer may deduct 'any amount it considers owed' from salary conflicts with this section unless the deduction falls within s 27 or another provision.
