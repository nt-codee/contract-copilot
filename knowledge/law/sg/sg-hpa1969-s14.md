## Text

"14.—(1) The hirer of any goods comprised in a regulated agreement may terminate the hiring by returning the goods to the owner during ordinary business hours at the place at which the owner ordinarily carries on business or to the place specified for that purpose in the agreement. (2) Where the nature of the goods comprised in a regulated agreement or the facilities available at the place or places of business of the owner or the place specified in the agreement is or are such that it would be impracticable to return the goods to such a place, the hirer may terminate the hiring by returning the goods to any place agreed to by the parties to the agreement."

"(6) When a regulated agreement is terminated under this section, the owner is entitled to recover from the hirer — (a) the amount (if any) required to be paid in those circumstances under the agreement; or (b) the amount (if any) that the owner would have been entitled to recover if he had taken possession of the goods at the date of termination of the hiring, whichever is less."

## In plain words

A hirer can end the hiring by returning the goods to the owner's place of business in business hours (or another agreed place). After that, the owner can recover from the hirer only the smaller of two amounts: what the agreement requires to be paid in those circumstances, or what the owner could have recovered had it repossessed the goods on that date.

## When it matters in an NDA or service agreement

A clause that stops the hirer from ever handing the vehicle back, or that makes the hirer pay more on ending than the Act allows, runs against this section and against s 33 (sg-hpa1969-s33). Ask how the 'amount required to be paid' on termination is worked out.
