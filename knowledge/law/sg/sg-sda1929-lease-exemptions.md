## Text

"1. Any lease or agreement for a lease mentioned in paragraph (a), (aa) or (c) executed on or after 1 April 2003 where the average rent and other consideration calculated for a whole year does not exceed $1,000."

"2. Any lease or agreement for a lease executed on or after 19 February 2011 pursuant to a duly stamped agreement, or another duly stamped agreement, for the same."

## In plain words

A lease for which the average rent and other consideration for a whole year does not exceed $1,000 is exempt from duty under Exemption 1. A lease signed under an agreement for lease that has itself been duly stamped is exempt under Exemption 2. Further exemptions follow in the Schedule and are not quoted here.

## When it matters in an NDA or service agreement

Very low-rent arrangements may carry no duty. Where an agreement for lease was stamped first, the lease itself should not be charged again.
