## Text

"(2) The liability of a guarantor shall continue notwithstanding that the owner has, pursuant to the provisions of a regulated agreement, taken possession of the goods comprised in the regulated agreement (and whether or not the goods have been redelivered to the hirer pursuant to this Act)."

"(4) No guarantor shall be liable to any further or other extent than the hirer, the performance of whose obligations he has guaranteed."

## In plain words

A guarantor stays liable even after the owner has repossessed the goods, but cannot be made liable to any greater extent than the hirer. Section 20(5) separately leaves alone a guarantor's agreement to perform an obligation that is not one of the hirer's obligations under the agreement.

## When it matters in an NDA or service agreement

If a guarantee makes the guarantor liable for 'all costs' without limit, compare it with the hirer's own liability. Note that this Act deals with guarantors of hire-purchase agreements; a guarantee of a licensed moneylender's loan is under the Moneylenders Act 2008.
