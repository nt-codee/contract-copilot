## Text

"24. An organisation must protect personal data in its possession or under its control by making reasonable security arrangements to prevent —
(a) unauthorised access, collection, use, disclosure, copying, modification or disposal, or similar risks; and
(b) the loss of any storage medium or device on which personal data is stored."

## In plain words

Any organisation holding or controlling personal data must make reasonable security arrangements to stop it being accessed, used, copied or changed without authority, and to stop devices holding it being lost. This duty applies directly to vendors acting as data intermediaries too.

## When it matters in an NDA or service agreement

A data-protection or security clause should set out what "reasonable security arrangements" means for the service, for example encryption, access control and incident handling, because both you and the vendor are bound by s 24 and you are also responsible for the vendor's processing (sg-pdpa2012-data-intermediary).
