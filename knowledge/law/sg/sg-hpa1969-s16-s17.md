## Text

"16. Where an owner has taken possession of any goods, he shall not, without the written consent of the hirer, sell or dispose of the goods or part with possession of the goods — (a) until after the expiration of 7 business days after the date of the service on the hirer of the notice referred to in section 15(3); or (b) if notice under section 17(1)(a) has been given, until the time for payment or tender pursuant to that notice has expired, whichever is the later."

"(2) Where the owner takes possession of any goods comprised in a regulated agreement, the owner is not entitled to recover any sum (whether under a judgment or order or otherwise) which if added together with — (a) the value of the goods at the time of the owner so taking possession of the goods; and (b) the amount paid or provided, whether by cash or other consideration, by or on behalf of the hirer under the agreement, exceeds the net amount payable under the agreement in respect of the goods."

## In plain words

After repossessing, the owner may not sell or part with the goods without the hirer's written consent until 7 business days after the post-repossession notice (or later if the hirer has given a notice under s 17(1)(a)). The owner also cannot recover any sum that, added to the value of the goods and what the hirer has already paid, would exceed the net amount payable under the agreement.

## When it matters in an NDA or service agreement

Look for terms that let the lender sell the car at once and still claim 'all remaining instalments'. The cap in s 17(2) limits what an owner can claim after repossession.
