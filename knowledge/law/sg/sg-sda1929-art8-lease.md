## Text

"Lease or agreement for a lease of any immovable property including any furniture, chattels, fittings or equipment and for securing the payment for the provision of services or facilities or to other matters or things in connection with such lease —"

"(aa) Without premium and executed on or after 22 February 2014"

"the total rent and other consideration payable for the period of the Lease"

"0.4% × A × 4 where A is the average rent and other consideration calculated for a whole year"

## In plain words

A lease or agreement for a lease of immovable property, without a premium, signed on or after 22 February 2014, is charged stamp duty at 0.4% of the total rent and other consideration payable for the period of the lease, and for longer leases at 0.4% × A × 4, where A is the average rent and other consideration calculated for a whole year. The table in the PDF extracted as scrambled text, so which rate applies to which lease length is read from the table's column headings (not exceeding 4 years; exceeding 4 years or any indefinite term) and should be checked against the PDF. Premiums and other cases (paragraphs (b) and (c)) are charged differently.

## When it matters in an NDA or service agreement

A tenancy agreement usually needs to be stamped. Check who the agreement says pays the duty, and whether the landlord's request that you pay 'stamp duty and admin fee' matches the duty above.
