## Text

"10.—(1) Either party to a contract of service may at any time give to the other party notice of the firstmentioned party’s intention to terminate the contract of service. (2) The length of the notice must be the same for both employer and employee and is to be determined by any provision made for the notice in the terms of the contract of service, or, in the absence of such provision, must be in accordance with subsection (3). (3) The notice to terminate the service of a person who is employed under a contract of service must be at least — (a) one day’s notice if the person has been so employed for less than 26 weeks; (b) one week’s notice if the person has been so employed for 26 weeks or more but less than 2 years; (c) 2 weeks’ notice if the person has been so employed for 2 years or more but less than 5 years; and (d) 4 weeks’ notice if the person has been so employed for 5 years or more."

"(5) The notice must be written and may be given at any time, and the day on which the notice is given is included in the period of the notice."

## In plain words

Either side can end a contract of service by notice. The notice period must be the same for the employer and the employee. If the contract does not say, the minimum is 1 day (under 26 weeks of service), 1 week (26 weeks to under 2 years), 2 weeks (2 to under 5 years) or 4 weeks (5 years or more). Notice must be in writing.

## When it matters in an NDA or service agreement

A contract giving the employer 1 month's notice but the employee 3 months', or the reverse, conflicts with s 10(2). Check the notice clause for equal periods.
