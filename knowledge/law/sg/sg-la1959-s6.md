## Text

"6.—(1) Subject to this Act, the following actions shall not be brought after the expiration of 6 years from the date on which the cause of action accrued:
(a) actions founded on a contract or on tort;
(b) actions to enforce a recognizance;
(c) actions to enforce an award;
(d) actions to recover any sum recoverable by virtue of any written law other than a penalty or forfeiture or sum by way of penalty or forfeiture."

## In plain words

In Singapore, a claim for breach of contract must normally be started within 6 years from when the claim arose, usually the date of the breach.

## When it matters in an NDA or service agreement

This is the default to compare against a vendor clause that shortens the time to bring a claim (for example "within 12 months of the event"). This knowledge base has no verified Singapore authority on whether such a clause is valid, so treat it as a point to raise rather than assume either way. Breaches of confidentiality are often found late, so a short claims window matters most in an NDA.
